## Overview | 概述

The IELTS Listening test is the same for both Academic and General Training candidates. It lasts approximately **30 minutes** (plus 10 minutes transfer time for paper-based test) and consists of **4 sections** with **40 questions** total.

雅思听力考试对学术类和培训类考生是一样的。考试时长约 **30 分钟**（纸笔考试另有 10 分钟誊写答案时间），共 **4 个部分**，**40 道题**。

### Test Format at a Glance | 考试形式一览

| Section | Content 内容 | Number of Questions 题数 | Context 场景 |
|---------|-------------|------------------------|-------------|
| 1 | Social conversation 日常对话 | 10 | Two speakers in everyday social context 两人日常社交场景 |
| 2 | Social monologue 日常独白 | 10 | One speaker in everyday social context 一人日常场景 |
| 3 | Academic discussion 学术讨论 | 10 | Two to four speakers in educational context 教育场景多人讨论 |
| 4 | Academic lecture 学术讲座 | 10 | One speaker on academic topic 学术话题讲座 |

### Difficulty Progression | 难度递进

- **Sections 1–2**: Social / survival English (easier)
- **Sections 3–4**: Academic / educational English (harder)

难度从 Section 1 到 Section 4 逐步递增。前两个部分偏日常，后两个部分偏学术。

## Key Facts | 关键信息

### Scoring | 评分

- Raw score out of 40 → Band score 1–9
- Each correct answer = 1 mark (no negative marking)
- Spelling mistakes = wrong answer
- 40 道题满分 → 换算为 1-9 分
- 每题 1 分，答错不扣分
- 拼写错误 = 答案错误

### Band Score Conversion (Approximate) | 分数换算（参考）

| Raw Score 原始分 | Band Score 雅思分数 |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| 39–40 | 9.0 |
| 37–38 | 8.5 |
| 35–36 | 8.0 |
| 32–34 | 7.5 |
| 30–31 | 7.0 |
| 26–29 | 6.5 |
| 23–25 | 6.0 |
| 18–22 | 5.5 |
| 16–17 | 5.0 |

### Paper-Based vs Computer-Delivered | 纸笔考试 vs 机考

| Feature 特征 | Paper-Based 纸笔 | Computer-Delivered 机考 |
|-------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Transfer time 誊写时间 | 10 minutes at end 结束后 10 分钟 | No transfer time (type directly) 无誊写时间（直接打字） |

## The Listening Process | 听力应试流程

### Before Listening | 听前准备

1. **Read instructions carefully** — pay attention to word limits (e.g., "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS")
   仔细阅读题目要求——注意字数限制（如"不超过两个词"）

2. **Underline keywords** in questions — names, numbers, dates, places
   在题目中划出关键词——名字、数字、日期、地点

3. **Predict answers** — think about what type of word is needed (noun? number? place?)
   预测答案——思考需要什么类型的词（名词？数字？地点？）

4. **Look at question order** — answers usually come in order
   看题目顺序——答案通常按顺序出现

### During Listening | 听的过程中

1. **Listen for keywords AND synonyms** — the recording rarely uses the exact words in the question
   听关键词和同义词——录音很少使用和题目完全相同的词

2. **Write answers as you hear them** — don't wait
   听到答案立即写下来——不要等

3. **Keep moving** — if you miss an answer, guess and move on
   继续前进——如果错过一个答案，猜一个然后继续

4. **Watch for distractors** — speakers often change their mind
   注意干扰项——说话者经常会改变主意

### After Listening | 听后检查

1. **Check spelling** — every letter counts
   检查拼写——每个字母都很重要

2. **Transfer answers carefully** (paper-based test)
   仔细誊写答案（纸笔考试）

3. **Review word limits** — make sure no answer exceeds the limit
   复查字数限制——确保没有答案超字数

## Core Skills Needed | 核心技能

### 1. Keyword Identification | 关键词识别

Learn to spot the important words in a question before you hear the recording.

学会在听录音前找出题目中的重要词汇。

**Example:**
- Question: "The library is closed on ___"
- Keywords: library, closed
- You listen for: when the library is NOT open
- 做题时找关键词：library（图书馆）、closed（关闭）
- 听的时候关注：图书馆什么时候不开门

### 2. Synonym Recognition | 同义词识别

The recording will paraphrase the question. You must recognize that different words can mean the same thing.

录音会对题目进行改写。你必须识别出不同的词可以表达相同的意思。

**Common paraphrases | 常见改写:**
- "costs" → "is priced at" / "charges"
- "located" → "situated" / "found"
- "required" → "compulsory" / "mandatory"
- "enough" → "sufficient" / "adequate"

### 3. Prediction | 预测能力

Before listening, predict what the answer could be based on context.

在听之前，根据上下文预测答案可能是什么。

**Example:**
- "The course starts in ___" → predict: a month (September, January, etc.)
- "Her phone number is ___" → predict: a series of digits
- "课程在___开始" → 预测：一个月份（九月、一月等）
- "她的电话号码是___" → 预测：一串数字

### 4. Note-Taking | 笔记技巧

Practice writing while listening. Use abbreviations and shorthand.

练习边听边写。使用缩写和速记。

**Useful abbreviations | 常用缩写:**
- w/ = with, w/o = without
- b/c = because, → = leads to, results in
- ↑ = increase, ↓ = decrease
- info = information, dept = department
- approx = approximately, esp = especially

### 5. Distractor Awareness | 干扰项意识

Speakers often:
- State something, then correct themselves
- Give an option, then reject it
- Use negative words: "not", "actually", "I mean"

说话者经常：
- 先说一件事，然后纠正自己
- 给出一个选项，然后否定
- 使用否定词："not"、"actually"、"I mean"

**Example:**
> "I thought the deadline was Friday... actually, no, it's been moved to Monday."
> Question: The deadline is on ___
> Answer: Monday (not Friday!)

## Common Pitfalls | 常见陷阱

| Pitfall 陷阱 | Solution 解决方案 |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Missing the answer because you're still thinking about the last question 还在想上一题时错过了答案 | Move on immediately; guess if needed 立即继续；必要时猜测 |
| Writing too many words 超字数 | Read word limits; count carefully 注意字数限制；仔细数 |
| Spelling errors 拼写错误 | Practice common IELTS vocabulary 练习常见雅思词汇 |
| Not reading ahead 没有提前看题 | Always read the next question while listening 听的时候始终提前看下一题 |
| Falling for distractors 落入干扰项 | Wait until the speaker finishes the thought 等说话者说完完整意思 |
| Ignoring plural/singular 忽略单复数 | Listen carefully; check grammar of the sentence 注意听；检查句子语法 |

## Recommended Study Plan | 推荐学习计划

### Week 1–2: Foundation | 第 1-2 周：基础

- Read all section guides in this module
- Learn all question types
- Do 1 full practice test to establish baseline
- 阅读本模块所有部分指南
- 学习所有题型
- 做一套完整练习测试建立基线

### Week 3–4: Skill Building | 第 3-4 周：技能提升

- Focus on your weakest question types
- Practice prediction and keyword identification daily
- Do section-by-section practice
- 重点攻克最弱的题型
- 每天练习预测和关键词识别
- 按部分逐个练习

### Week 5–6: Full Practice | 第 5-6 周：完整练习

- Do 2–3 full practice tests per week
- Analyze every mistake
- Build stamina and concentration
- 每周做 2-3 套完整练习
- 分析每一道错题
- 培养耐力和专注力

### Week 7–8: Refinement | 第 7-8 周：精细打磨

- Simulate real test conditions
- Focus on time management
- Review all notes and error patterns
- 模拟真实考试条件
- 重点关注时间管理
- 复习所有笔记和错误模式

# Section 1: Conversations | 第一部分：日常对话

> Social conversations in everyday contexts | 日常社交场景对话

---

## Overview | 概述

Section 1 is the **easiest part** of the IELTS Listening test, but it is also where you must **secure maximum marks**. A strong start builds confidence and gives you a buffer for harder sections later.

第一部分是雅思听力中**最简单的部分**，但也是你必须**拿到尽可能多分数**的地方。好的开局能建立信心，并为后面较难的部分提供分数缓冲。

### Key Characteristics | 核心特征

| Feature 特征 | Detail 详情 |
|-------------|-------------|
| Speakers 说话者 | 2 people (usually a man and a woman) 两人（通常一男一女） |
| Context 场景 | Booking, enquiring, registering, ordering 预订、咨询、注册、点单 |
| Difficulty 难度 | Easiest of all 4 sections 四个部分中最简单 |
| Questions 题数 | 10 questions 10 道题 |
| Answer types 答案类型 | Mostly form-filling (names, numbers, dates, addresses) 多为填表（姓名、数字、日期、地址） |

---

## Common Scenarios | 常见场景

### 1. Booking / Reservation | 预订

**Typical contexts | 典型场景:**
- Hotel / accommodation booking 酒店/住宿预订
- Restaurant reservation 餐厅预订
- Car rental booking 租车预订
- Event / activity sign-up 活动报名

**Example dialogue | 对话示例:**
> **Agent:** Good afternoon, Sea View Hotel. How can I help you?
> **Caller:** I'd like to book a room for the weekend, please.
> **Agent:** Certainly. Will that be for one night or two?
> **Caller:** Just one night — Saturday the 15th.
> **Agent:** And what type of room would you prefer? We have singles, doubles, and suites.
> **Caller:** A double room, please. What's the rate?
> **Agent:** It's 85 pounds per night, including breakfast.

**Sample question:**
- Date of booking: **15th** (Saturday)
- Room type: **double**
- Price per night: **85 pounds**

---

### 2. Enquiry / Information Request | 咨询/信息查询

**Typical contexts | 典型场景:**
- Library membership 图书馆会员
- Gym / leisure centre 健身房/休闲中心
- Course registration 课程注册
- Travel information 旅游信息

**Example dialogue | 对话示例:**
> **Staff:** Welcome to Riverside Sports Centre. Are you interested in membership?
> **Visitor:** Yes, I've just moved to the area. What do you offer?
> **Staff:** We have three membership levels. The basic package gives you access to the gym and pool for 30 pounds a month.
> **Visitor:** What about classes?
> **Staff:** For classes, you'd need the premium membership at 45 pounds. That includes yoga, pilates, and spinning.
> **Visitor:** Is there a student discount?
> **Staff:** Yes, students get 20% off any package.

## Question Types You Will See | 你会遇到的题型

### Form Completion (Most Common) | 填表题（最常见）

You fill in missing information on a form, typically:
- Names (spelled out) 姓名（会拼写出来）
- Addresses 地址
- Phone numbers 电话号码
- Dates 日期
- Times 时间
- Prices 价格

**Example:**

```
REGISTRATION FORM
Name: Sarah ____________ (1)
Address: 14 ____________ Street (2)
Postcode: ____________ (3)
Telephone: ____________ (4)
Date of birth: ____________ 1995 (5)
```

**Strategy:** The answers come in order. Listen for the spelling of names very carefully.

**策略：** 答案按顺序出现。仔细听姓名的拼写。

---

### Multiple Choice | 选择题

Less common in Section 1, but sometimes appears.

**Example:**
> Why is the woman calling?
> A. To cancel a booking
> B. To change a reservation ✓
> C. To make a new booking

---

### Note Completion | 笔记填写

Similar to form completion but with notes instead of a form.

## Essential Skills for Section 1 | 第一部分必备技能

### Skill 1: Listening for Spelled Names | 听拼写

Names are almost always spelled out in Section 1. Practice recognizing letters quickly.

姓名在第一部分几乎总是会拼写出来。练习快速识别字母。

**Tricky letter pairs | 容易混淆的字母对:**
| Letter 字母 | Sounds like 听起来像 |
|------------|---------------------|
| A | "ay" — can confuse with "eight" (8) |
| B | "bee" — can confuse with "D", "P", "V" |
| D | "dee" — can confuse with "B", "E", "G" |
| E | "ee" — can confuse with "I" |
| G | "jee" — can confuse with "J", "Z" |
| H | "aitch" — can confuse with "eight" (8) |
| I | "eye" — can confuse with "A" |
| J | "jay" — can confuse with "G", "K" |
| M | "em" — can confuse with "N" |
| N | "en" — can confuse with "M" |
| S | "ess" — can confuse with "F", "X" |
| V | "vee" — can confuse with "B" |

**Practice example | 练习示例:**
> "The surname is Goldstein — that's G-O-L-D-S-T-E-I-N."

**Tip | 提示:** When you hear a name being spelled, write the letters first, then check against what you hear. Don't try to recognize the name immediately — just write each letter.

听到姓名拼写时，先把字母写下来，然后对照听到的内容检查。不要试图立刻识别出名字——先把每个字母写下来。

---

### Skill 2: Listening for Numbers | 听数字

Numbers appear in almost every Section 1 question set.

数字几乎出现在每一套第一部分的题目中。

**Types of numbers | 数字类型:**

| Type 类型 | Example 示例 |
|----------|-------------|
| Phone numbers 电话号码 | 0-7-double 8-3-4-1-5 |
| Prices 价格 | 45.99 pounds / 15 dollars |
| Dates 日期 | 15th March / March 15th / 15/03 |
| Times 时间 | 2:30 PM / half past two / quarter to three |
| Addresses 门牌号 | 14A / 42B |
| Quantities 数量 | 25 students / a dozen |

**Common number traps | 常见数字陷阱:**
- "thirteen" (13) vs "thirty" (30) — stress is on different syllables
- "fifteen" (15) vs "fifty" (50) — similar stress issue
- "a hundred and fifty" (150) vs "a hundred and fifteen" (115)
- Double numbers: "double seven" = 77, "triple two" = 222

**Practice | 练习:**

Listen and write down:
1. The phone number: 0845-7-double 3-192
   - Answer: 0845733192

2. "It costs one hundred and thirty-five pounds, not one hundred and fifty-three."
   - Answer: £135

---

### Skill 3: Listening for Addresses | 听地址

**UK address format | 英国地址格式:**
```
14 Oakfield Road
Bristol
BS7 8QP
```

**Key vocabulary | 关键词汇:**
- Road / Street / Avenue / Lane / Drive / Close / Crescent
- Flat 3 / Apartment 5B / Unit 12
- First floor (UK = above ground floor)
- Opposite / next to / near / behind

**Example | 示例:**
> "The office is at 27 Greenfield Avenue — that's A-V-E-N-U-E — and the postcode is B-R-seven, space, 8-Q-P."

---

### Skill 4: Listening for Dates | 听日期

**Different ways to say dates | 日期的不同说法:**

| Written 写法 | Spoken 口语 |
|-------------|------------|
| 15th March | "the fifteenth of March" / "March the fifteenth" / "March fifteenth" |
| 2nd June | "the second of June" / "June the second" |
| 21/06/2025 | "the twenty-first of June, twenty twenty-five" |

**Months — practice spelling | 月份——练习拼写:**
January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December

**Warning | 警告:** Some candidates confuse dates like "the 13th" with "the 30th" because they sound similar when spoken quickly. Listen for the full word: "thirteenth" vs "thirtieth".

有些考生会混淆 "the 13th" 和 "the 30th"，因为快速说出来时发音相似。注意听完整单词："thirteenth" 和 "thirtieth"。

## Section 1 Practice Exercise | 第一部分练习

### Exercise 1: Form Completion | 练习 1：填表题

**Instructions:** You will hear a woman calling a travel agency. Complete the form below.

```
TRAVEL BOOKING FORM
Customer name: ____________ (1) Martin
Address: 34 ____________ Road (2)
City: ____________ (3)
Postcode: NW3 4QT
Phone: ____________ (4)
Destination: ____________ (5)
Travel dates: ____________ to 28th June (6)
Number of passengers: ____________ (7)
Special requirements: ____________ meal (8)
Total cost: £ ____________ (9)
Payment method: ____________ (10)
```

**Transcript excerpt (for self-study):**

> **Agent:** Good morning, Globe Travel. How can I help you?
> **Caller:** Hi, I'd like to book a holiday, please.
> **Agent:** Of course. Could I take your name first?
> **Caller:** It's Catherine Martin — that's C-A-T-H-E-R-I-N-E, Martin.
> **Agent:** Thank you. And your address?
> **Caller:** 34 Hillcrest Road, that's H-I-L-L-C-R-E-S-T, in Camden.
> **Agent:** And the postcode?
> **Caller:** NW3 4QT.
> **Agent:** Phone number?
> **Caller:** 07849 221076.
> **Agent:** Where would you like to go?
> **Caller:** Barcelona, Spain.
> **Agent:** When would you like to travel?
> **Caller:** From the 21st to the 28th of June.
> **Agent:** How many people?
> **Caller:** Just two — myself and my husband.
> **Agent:** Any dietary requirements?
> **Caller:** Yes, I'm vegetarian, so I'll need a vegetarian meal.
> **Agent:** The total comes to £1,240. How would you like to pay?
> **Caller:** By credit card, please.

**Answers:**
1. Catherine (spelled C-A-T-H-E-R-I-N-E)
2. Hillcrest (spelled H-I-L-L-C-R-E-S-T)
3. Camden
4. 07849 221076
5. Barcelona (Spain)
6. 21st June / 21st
7. 2 / two
8. vegetarian
9. 1240 / 1,240
10. credit card

## Common Mistakes in Section 1 | 第一部分常见错误

### Mistake 1: Overconfidence | 过于自信

Since Section 1 is "easy," many candidates relax and miss simple details.

因为第一部分"简单"，很多考生放松警惕，错过了简单细节。

**Solution:** Treat every section with full concentration.

**解决方案：** 全神贯注对待每个部分。

### Mistake 2: Ignoring Word Limits | 忽略字数限制

If the instruction says "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS," then "credit card" is fine, but "by credit card" is three words and would be marked wrong.

如果要求"不超过两个词"，那么 "credit card" 可以，但 "by credit card" 是三个词，会被判错。

**Solution:** Always check the word limit. Count your words before writing.

**解决方案：** 始终检查字数限制。写之前数一下字数。

### Mistake 3: Spelling Errors on Simple Words | 简单词汇拼写错误

Commonly misspelled words in IELTS listening:

| Correct 正确 | Common Error 常见错误 |
|-------------|---------------------|
| accommodation | accomodation |
| February | Febuary |
| library | libary |
| Wednesday | Wensday |
| government | goverment |
| restaurant | resturant |
| beginning | begining |
| separate | seperate |
| necessary | neccessary |
| immediately | immediatly |

**Solution:** Create a personal spelling list and practice daily.

**解决方案：** 创建个人拼写列表，每天练习。

### Mistake 4: Writing the First Thing You Hear | 写下听到的第一个内容

Speakers often correct themselves or change information.

说话者经常自我纠正或更改信息。

**Example:**
> "The appointment is on Tuesday... oh wait, sorry, it's Wednesday."

**Wrong answer:** Tuesday
**Correct answer:** Wednesday

**Solution:** Always wait for the speaker to confirm. Listen for phrases like:
- "actually"
- "I mean"
- "sorry, I meant"
- "let me correct that"
- "on second thought"

**解决方案：** 等说话者确认。注意听以下短语：actually, I mean, sorry I meant 等。

### Mistake 5: Not Reading Ahead | 没有提前阅读题目

If you don't read the next question before the audio plays, you might miss the answer entirely.

如果你不在音频播放前阅读下一题，你可能完全错过答案。

**Solution:** After answering a question, immediately read the next 1-2 questions.

**解决方案：** 回答一道题后，立即阅读接下来的 1-2 道题。

## Key Takeaways | 核心要点

1. **Section 1 is your foundation** — aim for 9–10 out of 10
   第一部分是你的基础——目标 9-10 分

2. **Practice spelling and numbers** until they are automatic
   练习拼写和数字直到成为条件反射

3. **Always read ahead** — don't wait for the audio to start before looking at questions
   始终提前阅读——不要等音频开始才看题目

4. **Watch for corrections** — speakers change their minds
   注意纠正——说话者会改变主意

5. **Respect word limits** — they are non-negotiable
   遵守字数限制——没有商量余地

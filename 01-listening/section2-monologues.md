# Section 2: Monologues | 第二部分：独白

> One speaker in an everyday social situation | 一人在日常社交场景中的独白

---

## Overview | 概述

Section 2 features **one speaker** giving information about a **practical, everyday topic**. It is still considered "social" English (not academic), but it is harder than Section 1 because there is only one voice — no conversation to help you follow along.

第二部分只有**一个说话者**，就一个**实用的日常话题**提供信息。它仍属于"社交"英语（非学术），但比第一部分难，因为只有一个声音——没有对话帮助你跟进。

### Key Characteristics | 核心特征

| Feature 特征 | Detail 详情 |
|-------------|-------------|
| Speaker 说话者 | 1 person 一人 |
| Context 场景 | Tourist information, facility guide, event introduction 旅游信息、设施介绍、活动介绍 |
| Difficulty 难度 | Slightly harder than Section 1 比第一部分稍难 |
| Questions 题数 | 10 questions 10 道题 |
| Common formats 常见题型 | Map labelling, multiple choice, note completion 地图标注、选择题、笔记填写 |

## Common Scenarios | 常见场景

### 1. Tourist Attraction Guide | 旅游景点介绍

**Typical content | 典型内容:**
- Museum or gallery overview 博物馆或美术馆概述
- Historical site description 历史遗址描述
- Nature park information 自然公园信息
- City tour commentary 城市导览讲解

**Example monologue excerpt | 独白示例摘录:**
> "Welcome to the Hartley Museum of Natural History. The museum was founded in 1892 by Sir Edward Hartley, a local businessman and keen naturalist. Today, the museum houses over 200,000 specimens across three floors. On the ground floor, you'll find our most popular exhibit — the dinosaur gallery, which features a complete Tyrannosaurus Rex skeleton discovered in Montana in 1908. The first floor is dedicated to marine life, including our recently renovated coral reef display..."

**What you might hear | 你可能会听到:**
- Opening times and admission fees 开放时间和入场费
- Location of different areas within the venue 场馆内不同区域的位置
- Rules and guidelines 规则和指南
- History and background 历史和背景

- Recycling scheme explanation 回收计划说明

Tuesday afternoons are reserved for our art workshops — these are open to all ages and abilities. Every Wednesday evening, there's a film screening in the auditorium. Admission is free, but we do ask for a suggested donation of 3 pounds..."

## Question Types in Section 2 | 第二部分题型

### Map / Plan Labelling (Very Common) | 地图/平面图标注（非常常见）

This is the **signature question type** of Section 2. You hear a description of a place and must label locations on a map or plan.

这是第二部分的**标志性题型**。你听到对一个地方的描述，然后必须在地图或平面图上标注位置。

**How it works | 如何作答:**

You see a map with some locations already marked (A, B, C...) and blank labels. You must match the labels to the correct locations.

你看到一张地图，上面已经标出了一些位置（A、B、C...），还有一些空白标签。你必须将标签与正确位置匹配。

**Example | 示例:**

```
        [North]
           |
    ┌──────┼──────┐
    │      │      │
    │  A   │  B   │
    │      │      │
    ├───┬──┴──┬───┤
    │   │     │   │
    │ C │  D  │ E │
    │   │     │   │
    └───┴─────┴───┘
```

> "As you enter through the main gates, the visitor centre is directly ahead of you — that's position D. The gift shop is to your left, between the entrance and the café — that would be position C. Speaking of the café, it's in the northeast corner — position B. The children's play area is behind the visitor centre, at position E..."

**Key vocabulary for map questions | 地图题关键词汇:**

| Direction 方向 | English | Chinese |
|---------------|---------|---------|
| Compass directions 罗盘方向 | north, south, east, west | 北、南、东、西 |
| Combined directions 组合方向 | northeast, southwest, etc. | 东北、西南等 |
| Relative positions 相对位置 | next to, beside, adjacent to | 旁边、相邻 |
| | opposite, across from | 对面 |
| | between...and... | 在...和...之间 |
| | behind, at the back | 后面 |
| | in front of, ahead of | 前面 |
| | to the left/right of | 左边/右边 |
| Movement 移动 | turn left/right | 左转/右转 |
| | go straight ahead | 直走 |
| | go past, pass by | 经过 |
| | take the first/second turning | 第一/第二个转弯 |
| | walk towards | 走向 |
| | enter through | 从...进入 |
| Landmarks 地标 | entrance, exit | 入口、出口 |
| | car park, parking lot | 停车场 |
| | main gate, side entrance | 大门、侧门 |
| | reception, front desk | 接待处 |
| | corridor, hallway | 走廊 |
| | staircase, stairs | 楼梯 |

**Strategy for map questions | 地图题策略:**

1. **Study the map before listening** — identify landmarks, directions, and any labels already given
   听之前先研究地图——找出地标、方向和已经给出的标签

2. **Follow the speaker's route** — the speaker usually describes locations in a logical order (e.g., "as you enter... to your left... straight ahead...")
   跟随说话者的路线——说话者通常按逻辑顺序描述位置

3. **Think in terms of the speaker's perspective** — "to your left" means the speaker's left, which might not be the same as your left when looking at the map
   从说话者的角度思考——"your left" 指的是说话者的左边，可能和你看地图时的左边不同

4. **Pay attention to reference points** — "behind the library" requires you to first locate the library
   注意参考点——"图书馆后面"要求你先找到图书馆的位置

### Multiple Choice | 选择题

**Types | 类型:**

**A. Standard multiple choice | 标准选择题:**
> According to the speaker, the new swimming pool will:
> A. open in January ✓
> B. have an Olympic-sized pool
> C. be free for children under 5

**B. Multiple choice with list | 列表选择题:**
> Which THREE things does the speaker recommend bringing?
> A. Sunscreen ✓
> B. Insect repellent ✓
> C. Warm clothes
> D. Waterproof jacket ✓
> E. Binoculars

**Strategy | 策略:**
- Read all options before listening
- Listen for synonyms/paraphrases of the options
- Don't just match keywords — understand meaning
- 在听之前阅读所有选项
- 听选项的同义词/改写
- 不要只匹配关键词——理解意思

---

### Note Completion | 笔记填写

You complete notes based on what you hear.

根据听到的内容完成笔记。

**Example | 示例:**

```
HARTLEY MUSEUM
- Founded: ____________ (1)
- Current exhibit: ____________ gallery (2)
- Number of specimens: over ____________ (3)
- Most popular: dinosaur gallery on ____________ floor (4)
- New display: ____________ reef (5)
```

**Strategy | 策略:**
- Answers are usually one or two words
- Listen for the same structure as the notes
- Grammar of the gap should match the answer
- 答案通常是一两个词
- 注意听与笔记相同的结构
- 空格的语法应与答案匹配

---

### Sentence Completion | 句子填空

You complete sentences with words from the recording.

用录音中的词完成句子。

**Example | 示例:**
> "The guided tour takes approximately ___90___ minutes."
> "Visitors are advised to wear ___comfortable___ shoes."

## Section 2 Specific Challenges | 第二部分特有挑战

### Challenge 1: Only One Speaker | 只有一个说话者

In Section 1, the conversation between two people gives you natural pauses and turn-taking cues. In Section 2, one person speaks continuously, which means:
- No natural breaks
- Information flows faster
- You must track position yourself

在第一部分，两人对话给你自然的停顿和轮流发言的提示。在第二部分，一个人连续说话，意味着：
- 没有自然停顿
- 信息流动更快
- 你必须自己追踪进度

**Solution:** Practice listening to monologues (podcasts, TED talks, tour guide recordings).

**解决方案：** 练习听独白（播客、TED 演讲、导游录音）。

### Challenge 2: Map Orientation | 地图方向

Map questions require you to think spatially while listening. This is a different skill from form-filling.

地图题要求你在听的同时进行空间思考。这是一项不同于填表的技能。

**Practice technique | 练习技巧:**
1. Draw simple maps from verbal descriptions
2. Give someone directions and have them draw your route
3. Practice with Google Maps — describe a location to someone
4. 用语言描述画简单地图
5. 给别人指路，让他们画出路线
6. 用谷歌地图练习——给别人描述一个位置

### Challenge 3: Recognizing When the Speaker Moves to a New Point | 识别说话者转换话题

**Transition phrases to listen for | 注意听的过渡短语:**
- "Moving on to..." 接下来说说...
- "Now, let's talk about..." 现在我们来谈谈...
- "The next thing I want to mention is..." 我要提到的下一点是...
- "Turning our attention to..." 让我们把注意力转向...
- "Another important feature is..." 另一个重要特点是...
- "So that's [X]. Now, [Y]..." 关于 X 就说这么多。现在说说 Y...

## Section 2 Practice Exercise | 第二部分练习

### Exercise: Note Completion | 练习：笔记填写

**Instructions:** You will hear a guide talking about a wildlife park. Complete the notes.

**GREENFIELD WILDLIFE PARK**

```
General Information:
- Opened: ____________ (1)
- Total area: ____________ hectares (2)
- Number of species: ____________ (3)
- Most popular animal: ____________ (4)

Visitor Information:
- Opening hours: 9 AM to ____________ (5)
- Adult ticket: £ ____________ (6)
- Child ticket (under 12): £ ____________ (7)
- Family ticket (2+2): £ ____________ (8)

New for this year:
- ____________ centre (9)
- ____________ experience (10)
```

**Transcript excerpt (for self-study):**

> "Welcome to Greenfield Wildlife Park. The park was established in 1967, so we're celebrating our 55th anniversary this year. We cover approximately 120 hectares of natural woodland and grassland, making us one of the largest wildlife parks in the region. We're currently home to over 350 different species from around the world.
>
> Our most popular attraction, especially with children, is the Asian elephant enclosure — we have a family of six elephants, including a baby born just last year.
>
> In terms of visiting, we're open every day from 9 AM to 5:30 PM, with last entry at 4:30. Tickets are £16.50 for adults and £9.75 for children under 12. We also offer a family ticket for two adults and two children at £42 — that's a saving of over 10 pounds.
>
> New for this year, we've opened a conservation education centre near the main entrance, and we're very excited about our new behind-the-scenes experience, where visitors can help feed the penguins."

**Answers:**
1. 1967
2. 120
3. 350
4. Asian elephant(s) / elephants
5. 5:30 / 5.30 PM
6. 16.50
7. 9.75
8. 42
9. conservation education
10. behind-the-scenes / behind the scenes

## Common Mistakes in Section 2 | 第二部分常见错误

### Mistake 1: Losing Place on Map Questions | 地图题迷失位置

You hear the speaker describe location C, but you're still looking at location A.

你听到说话者描述位置 C，但你还在看位置 A。

**Solution:** Always keep your finger or pencil on the current location the speaker is describing.

**解决方案：** 始终用手指或铅笔指向说话者正在描述的位置。

### Mistake 2: Not Using the Map Orientation | 没有利用地图方向

If the map has a compass arrow (N↑), always note which direction is north before listening begins.

如果地图有指南针箭头（N↑），在开始听之前始终标记北方。

**Solution:** Draw a quick compass on your paper. Label N, S, E, W.

**解决方案：** 在纸上快速画一个指南针。标记北、南、东、西。

### Mistake 3: Answering Too Quickly on Multiple Choice | 选择题回答过快

The speaker may mention all options but only one is correct. Or they may describe one option and then change to another.

说话者可能提到所有选项，但只有一个正确。或者他们可能描述一个选项然后转向另一个。

**Solution:** Don't circle the answer until you're sure. Listen for negative or contrasting language.

**解决方案：** 在确定之前不要圈答案。注意听否定或对比语言。

### Mistake 4: Ignoring the Speaker's Emphasis | 忽略说话者的强调

In monologues, speakers use stress and intonation to highlight important information. If they say "what's REALLY important is..." or "the MAIN attraction is...", they are giving you a signal.

在独白中，说话者用重音和语调来突出重要信息。如果他们说 "what's REALLY important is..." 或 "the MAIN attraction is..."，他们在给你信号。

**Solution:** Listen for emphasis words: really, especially, particularly, importantly, notably.

**解决方案：** 注意听强调词：really, especially, particularly, importantly, notably。

### Mistake 5: Confusing "North" on the Map vs "North" in Reality | 混淆地图上的"北"和实际的"北"

In map questions, the speaker describes directions from their own perspective or from a specific entry point.

在地图题中，说话者从自己的角度或特定入口描述方向。

**Solution:** The speaker usually says "as you enter" or "from the main gate" — use this to orient yourself.

**解决方案：** 说话者通常会说 "as you enter" 或 "from the main gate"——用这个来确定方向。

## Key Takeaways | 核心要点

1. **Map questions require spatial thinking** — practice with any map you find
   地图题需要空间思维——用任何能找到的地图练习

2. **Follow the speaker's route** — they describe locations in a logical order
   跟随说话者的路线——他们按逻辑顺序描述位置

3. **Watch for transitions** — the speaker signals when they move to a new topic
   注意话题转换——说话者在转换话题时会发出信号

4. **Practice listening to monologues** — podcasts, audiobooks, and lectures all help
   练习听独白——播客、有声书和讲座都有帮助

5. **Don't rush on multiple choice** — wait for the speaker to complete their thought
   选择题不要急——等说话者说完完整意思

# IELTS Listening Question Types | 雅思听力题型详解

> A complete guide to every question type you will encounter | 完整指南：你会遇到的所有题型

---

## Overview | 概述

The IELTS Listening test uses **6 main question types** across its 40 questions. Each type tests a different skill. Understanding each type and having a specific strategy for it is essential for a high score.

雅思听力考试的 40 道题使用 **6 种主要题型**。每种题型测试不同的技能。理解每种题型并有具体的策略对取得高分至关重要。

### Question Type Summary | 题型总结

| Question Type 题型 | Frequency 出现频率 | Difficulty 难度 | Sections 出现部分 |
|-------------------|-------------------|-----------------|------------------|
| Form / Note Completion 填表/笔记填写 | Very common | Easy–Medium | 1, 2, 4 |
| Sentence Completion 句子填空 | Very common | Medium | 2, 3, 4 |
| Multiple Choice 选择题 | Common | Medium–Hard | 1, 2, 3, 4 |
| Matching 匹配题 | Common | Medium–Hard | 2, 3 |
| Map / Plan Labelling 地图/平面图标注 | Common | Medium | 2 |
| Short Answer Questions 简答题 | Less common | Easy–Medium | 1, 2 |

---

## 1. Form Completion | 填表题

### What It Is | 是什么

You fill in gaps in a form, such as a registration form, booking form, or application form. This is the most common question type in Section 1.

你在表格中填空，如登记表、预订表或申请表。这是第一部分最常见的题型。

### Example | 示例

```
APPOINTMENT BOOKING FORM
Patient name: ____________ (1) Roberts
Date of birth: ____________ (2) 1987
Address: 15 ____________ Street (3)
Telephone: ____________ (4)
GP surgery: ____________ Medical Centre (5)
Reason for visit: ____________ check (6)
Preferred date: ____________ (7)
Preferred time: ____________ (8)
```

### Strategy | 策略

1. **Read the form before listening** — understand what information is needed
   听之前阅读表格——了解需要什么信息

2. **Predict answer types** — name? number? date? address?
   预测答案类型——姓名？数字？日期？地址？

3. **Listen in order** — form answers usually follow the order of the recording
   按顺序听——表格答案通常按录音顺序

4. **Write exactly what you hear** — don't paraphrase
   写下你听到的——不要改写

5. **Watch word limits** — "ONE WORD ONLY" means exactly one word
   注意字数限制——"仅限一个词"就意味着一个词

### Common Pitfalls | 常见陷阱

- **Names are spelled out** — listen letter by letter
  姓名会拼写出来——逐个字母听

- **Numbers may be said differently** — "oh" for zero, "double five" for 55
  数字可能用不同方式说——"oh" 代表零，"double five" 代表 55

- **Dates can be in different formats** — "the third of May" / "May the third" / "May third"
  日期可以用不同格式——"the third of May" / "May the third" / "May third"

## 2. Note Completion | 笔记填写

### What It Is | 是什么

You complete notes or a summary based on the recording. The notes follow the structure of the talk but with gaps.

根据录音完成笔记或总结。笔记跟随讲座结构，但有空缺。

### Example | 示例

```
WILDLIFE CONSERVATION PROJECT
Location: ____________ National Park (1)
Duration: ____________ months (2)
Species studied: ____________ (3)
Main threat: loss of ____________ (4)
Population decline: ____________ percent (5)
Solution: create wildlife ____________ (6)
Funding from: ____________ (7)
```

### Strategy | 策略

1. **Read the notes heading** — it tells you the topic
   阅读笔记标题——它告诉你话题

2. **Note the structure** — the notes usually follow the order of the lecture
   注意结构——笔记通常按讲座顺序

3. **Use the existing words as anchors** — they help you know when the answer is coming
   用已有的词作为锚点——它们帮助你知道答案何时到来

4. **Check grammar** — the answer must fit grammatically in the gap
   检查语法——答案必须在语法上适合空格

5. **Respect word limits** — count your answer words carefully
   遵守字数限制——仔细数你的答案词数

### Types of Notes You Might See | 你可能看到的笔记类型

- Lecture notes 讲座笔记
- Summary paragraphs 摘要段落
- Table completion 表格填写
- Flow chart completion 流程图填写
- Classification table 分类表

## 3. Sentence Completion | 句子填空

### What It Is | 是什么

You complete sentences using information from the recording. The sentences are usually a paraphrase of what the speaker says.

用录音中的信息完成句子。这些句子通常是对说话者所说内容的改写。

### Example | 示例

> Complete the sentences below.
> Write NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS for each answer.
>
> 1. The library was originally built to serve the needs of ____________.
> 2. The building underwent major renovation work in ____________.
> 3. The new wing contains a collection of over ____________ books.
> 4. Library cards are available free of charge to all ____________.
> 5. The speaker recommends visiting during the ____________ to avoid crowds.

### Strategy | 策略

1. **Read the incomplete sentence** — understand what it's asking
   阅读不完整的句子——理解它在问什么

2. **Predict the answer type** — noun? adjective? number? proper noun?
   预测答案类型——名词？形容词？数字？专有名词？

3. **Listen for the sentence's keyword** — this tells you when the answer is coming
   听句子的关键词——这告诉你答案何时到来

4. **The answer must complete the sentence grammatically** — check subject-verb agreement, articles, etc.
   答案必须在语法上完成句子——检查主谓一致、冠词等

5. **Use the same words as the speaker** — don't paraphrase your answer
   使用和说话者相同的词——不要改写你的答案

### Grammar Check Examples | 语法检查示例

| Sentence with gap | Prediction | Correct answer |
|-------------------|-----------|----------------|
| "The ___ was established in 1990." | noun (singular) | "school" / "centre" |
| "Results were ___ significant." | adverb | "highly" / "very" |
| "The majority of ___ were female." | noun (plural) | "participants" / "students" |
| "She was ___ impressed by the results." | adverb | "very" / "extremely" |

## 4. Multiple Choice | 选择题

### What It Is | 是什么

You choose the correct answer from a list of options. This can be a single answer or multiple answers (e.g., "Choose THREE letters").

你从选项列表中选择正确答案。可以是单选或多选（如"选择三个字母"）。

### Types | 类型

**A. Single answer multiple choice | 单选题**

> What reason does the speaker give for choosing this topic?
> A. It was recommended by their supervisor
> B. Personal experience motivated their interest ✓
> C. It was the only topic available
> D. Other students suggested it

**B. Multiple answer multiple choice | 多选题**

> Which THREE factors contributed to the decline in bee populations?
> A. Pesticide use ✓
> B. Climate change ✓
> C. Over-harvesting of honey
> D. Habitat loss ✓
> E. Natural predators
> F. Disease

### Strategy | 策略

1. **Read ALL options before listening** — you need to know what to listen for
   听之前阅读所有选项——你需要知道要听什么

2. **Underline keywords in each option** — find what makes each option different
   在每个选项中划出关键词——找出每个选项的不同之处

3. **Listen for synonyms and paraphrases** — the speaker won't use the exact words from the options
   听同义词和改写——说话者不会使用选项中的原话

4. **Beware of distractors** — the speaker may mention information from incorrect options
   注意干扰项——说话者可能提到不正确选项中的信息

5. **Don't match keywords blindly** — "university" in the recording doesn't mean option A with "university" is correct; understand the full meaning
   不要盲目匹配关键词——录音中的 "university" 不代表有 "university" 的选项 A 就是正确的；理解完整意思

### Common Distractor Patterns | 常见干扰模式

| Pattern 模式 | Example 示例 |
|-------------|-------------|
| State then correct | "It costs 50 pounds... actually, no, it's 45." |
| Give then reject | "We considered option A, but decided against it." |
| One speaker states, another corrects | A: "It's on Friday." B: "No, it's Saturday." |
| Mention in negative context | "It's NOT a popular choice." |

## 5. Matching | 匹配题

### What It Is | 是什么

You match items from two lists. For example, match speakers to their opinions, or match features to descriptions.

你匹配两个列表中的项目。例如，将说话者与观点匹配，或将特征与描述匹配。

### Example | 示例

> Match each speaker to their opinion about online learning.
>
> **List of speakers:**
> 1. Speaker A (Tom)
> 2. Speaker B (Lisa)
> 3. Speaker C (James)
>
> **List of opinions:**
> A. Prefers face-to-face interaction
> B. Values the flexibility of online learning
> C. Thinks online learning is equally effective
> D. Has concerns about student motivation
> E. Believes technology improves access
>
> **Answers:**
> 1. Tom → B
> 2. Lisa → A
> 3. James → D

### Strategy | 策略

1. **Read both lists carefully** — understand what you're matching
   仔细阅读两个列表——理解你在匹配什么

2. **Write speaker names/initials on your notepad** — create a column for each
   在笔记纸上写下说话者姓名/首字母——为每人创建一栏

3. **Listen for opinion signals** — "I think...", "In my view...", "I'd say..."
   听观点信号——"I think...", "In my view...", "I'd say..."

4. **You can use each option more than once** (unless stated otherwise)
   每个选项可以使用多次（除非另有说明）

5. **Answers may come in any order** — Tom might express his opinion last
   答案可能以任何顺序出现——Tom 可能最后表达他的观点

## 6. Map / Plan Labelling | 地图/平面图标注

### What It Is | 是什么

You label locations on a map or plan based on verbal descriptions. This is mainly found in Section 2.

根据语言描述在地图或平面图上标注位置。主要出现在第二部分。

### Example | 示例

```
         N
         ↑
    ┌────┼─────────┐
    │    │         │
    │  A │    B    │
    │    │         │
    ├──┬─┴──┬──────┤
    │  │    │      │
    │C │ D  │  E   │
    │  │    │      │
    └──┴────┴──────┘
         ↓
    Entrance

    Listen and label:
    1. Gift shop: ___
    2. Café: ___
    3. Exhibition hall: ___
    4. Restrooms: ___
    5. Information desk: ___
```

### Strategy | 策略

1. **Study the map before listening** — identify landmarks, compass directions, and any labels already given
   听之前研究地图——识别地标、指南针方向和已有的标签

2. **Draw compass points** — N, S, E, W — on your question paper
   在试卷上画指南针方向——N、S、E、W

3. **Follow the speaker's description path** — they usually describe locations in a logical route
   跟随说话者的描述路线——他们通常按逻辑路线描述位置

4. **Keep your pencil on the current location** — this helps you stay oriented
   铅笔指向当前位置——这帮助你保持方向

5. **Listen for direction words** — left, right, opposite, adjacent, behind, in front of
   听方向词——左、右、对面、相邻、后面、前面

## 7. Short Answer Questions | 简答题

### What It Is | 是什么

You answer questions with short phrases taken directly from the recording.

你用直接从录音中提取的短语回答问题。

### Example | 示例

> Write NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS for each answer.
>
> 1. What does the speaker say is the main cause of deforestation?
>    Answer: **agricultural expansion**
>
> 2. When did the conservation programme begin?
>    Answer: **2012**
>
> 3. Who funded the research project?
>    Answer: **the government** / **local government**

### Strategy | 策略

1. **Read the question carefully** — understand exactly what is being asked
   仔细阅读题目——准确理解被问的内容

2. **Identify question words** — what (thing), when (time), where (place), who (person), why (reason), how (method)
   识别疑问词——what（事物）、when（时间）、where（地点）、who（人物）、why（原因）、how（方法）

3. **Predict the type of answer** — "When?" → time/date; "Who?" → person/organization
   预测答案类型——"When?" → 时间/日期; "Who?" → 人/组织

4. **Answer in the fewest words possible** — if it says "NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS," use 1–3 words
   用尽可能少的词回答——如果要求"不超过三个词"，用 1-3 个词

5. **Use the speaker's exact words** — don't paraphrase
   使用说话者的原话——不要改写

## Cross-Cutting Strategies | 通用策略

### The Word Limit Rule | 字数限制规则

**This applies to ALL gap-fill question types.**

这适用于所有填空题型。

| Instruction | Meaning | Example Correct | Example Wrong |
|-------------|---------|----------------|---------------|
| ONE WORD ONLY | Exactly one word | "library" | "the library" |
| NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS | 1–2 words | "credit card" | "pay by credit card" |
| NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER | 1–2 words and/or a number | "Room 15" / "£50" | "Room number 15" |
| NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS | 1–3 words | "by credit card" | "using a credit card payment" |

### The Prediction Technique | 预测技巧

**Before the audio plays, for EVERY gap:**

在音频播放前，对每一个空格：

1. Read the words around the gap
   阅读空格周围的词

2. Ask: "What type of word fits here grammatically?"
   问："什么类型的词在语法上适合这里？"

3. Ask: "What type of word fits here logically?"
   问："什么类型的词在逻辑上适合这里？"

**Examples:**

| Gap context | Grammar prediction | Logic prediction |
|-------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| "The ___ was founded in 1990." | singular noun | institution/building |
| "Participants reported feeling ___." | adjective | emotion (happy, anxious, etc.) |
| "The course runs for ___ weeks." | number | 6, 8, 10, 12, etc. |
| "She works as a ___." | singular noun | job title |

### The Synonym Trap | 同义词陷阱

The recording almost NEVER uses the exact words from the question. You must listen for synonyms.

录音几乎从不使用题目中的原词。你必须听同义词。

**Examples:**

| Question says | Recording says | Same meaning? |
|--------------|---------------|---------------|
| "costs" | "is priced at" | Yes ✓ |
| "required" | "compulsory" | Yes ✓ |
| "important" | "significant" | Yes ✓ |
| "located" | "situated" | Yes ✓ |
| "big" | "enormous" | Yes ✓ |
| "showed" | "demonstrated" | Yes ✓ |
| "changed" | "modified" | Yes ✓ |
| "enough" | "sufficient" | Yes ✓ |
| "help" | "assist" | Yes ✓ |
| "use" | "utilize" | Yes ✓ |

### The Order Principle | 顺序原则

In most IELTS Listening questions, **answers appear in order**. This means:
- If you heard the answer to question 5, the answer to question 4 has already passed
- If you're looking for the answer to question 7, you don't need to go back to something said earlier

在大多数雅思听力题中，**答案按顺序出现**。这意味着：
- 如果你听到了第 5 题的答案，第 4 题的答案已经过去了
- 如果你在找第 7 题的答案，你不需要回到之前说的内容

**Exception:** In matching questions and some multiple choice, answers may not be in order.

**例外：** 在匹配题和一些选择题中，答案可能不按顺序。

## Practice Exercise: Mixed Question Types | 练习：混合题型

### Instructions | 说明

You will hear a lecture about renewable energy. Answer questions 1–10.

你将听到一场关于可再生能源的讲座。回答第 1-10 题。

**Questions 1–4: Note Completion**

```
RENEWABLE ENERGY OVERVIEW
- Global renewable energy share: ____________ percent (1)
- Fastest growing source: ____________ (2)
- Main advantage: produces no ____________ (3)
- Biggest challenge: ____________ of supply (4)
```

**Questions 5–7: Sentence Completion**

> 5. Solar panel costs have decreased by ____________ percent since 2010.
> 6. Wind turbines are most effective in areas with consistent ____________.
> 7. The speaker predicts that renewables will supply ____________ of global energy by 2050.

**Questions 8–10: Multiple Choice**

> 8. What does the speaker say about nuclear energy?
> A. It is the cleanest option available
> B. It produces waste that is difficult to store ✓
> C. It is cheaper than solar energy
>
> 9. According to the speaker, what is the main barrier to solar adoption in developing countries?
> A. Lack of sunlight
> B. High initial installation costs ✓
> C. Government regulations
>
> 10. What does the speaker recommend as the most immediate action?
> A. Investing in nuclear power
> B. Reducing individual energy consumption ✓
> C. Building more wind farms

**Answers:** 1. 29, 2. solar, 3. carbon emissions / emissions, 4. intermittency / inconsistency, 5. 89, 6. wind, 7. 60 percent / 60%, 8. B, 9. B, 10. B

## Common Mistakes Across All Question Types | 所有题型的常见错误

### 1. Not Reading Instructions | 不读说明

Every question set has instructions that tell you the word limit. Read them every time.

每组题都有告诉你字数限制的说明。每次都读。

### 2. Leaving Answers Blank | 留空答案

There is no negative marking. Always guess, even if you're unsure.

没有倒扣分。即使不确定也要猜。

### 3. Poor Spelling | 拼写不好

Spelling errors = wrong answer. Practice commonly misspelled words.

拼写错误 = 错误答案。练习常见的拼写错误词。

### 4. Not Using Preview Time | 不利用预览时间

The 30-second preview before each section is crucial. Use it to read ahead.

每部分前 30 秒的预览至关重要。利用它提前阅读。

### 5. Writing Too Many Words | 写太多词

If the limit is "TWO WORDS" and you write three, you lose the mark. Every time.

如果限制是"两个词"你写了三个，你就丢分。每次都这样。

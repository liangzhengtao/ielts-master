## 🏆 Tier 1: Essential Channels | 必看频道

## 💡 Tips for Learning from YouTube | YouTube 学习技巧

### Active Watching | 主动观看

1. **Take notes** — Don't just watch passively (做笔记 — 不要被动观看)
2. **Pause and practice** — Try what the teacher teaches (暂停练习 — 尝试老师教的内容)
3. **Watch at 1.25x speed** — Save time, still understand (1.25 倍速 — 省时间还能理解)
4. **Use subtitles** — English subtitles for listening practice (用英文字幕练听力)
5. **Re-watch important videos** — Repetition aids memory (重看重要视频 — 重复有助记忆)

### Organize Your Learning | 组织学习

1. **Create playlists** — Group videos by section (创建播放列表 — 按科目分组)
2. **Use "Watch Later"** — Save videos for focused study time (用"稍后观看" — 保存到专注学习时间)
3. **Take notes in a notebook** — Use our [error notebook template](../templates/error-notebook.md) (在笔记本中做笔记)
4. **Review weekly** — Go back to key videos each week (每周回顾 — 每周回看关键视频)

### Avoid These Mistakes | 避免这些错误

1. **Don't binge-watch** — Quality over quantity (不要一次看太多 — 质量重于数量)
2. **Don't skip practice** — Watching isn't practicing (不要跳过练习 — 看不等于练)
3. **Don't watch only one channel** — Different perspectives help (不要只看一个频道 — 不同视角有帮助)
4. **Don't watch without purpose** — Have a goal for each session (不要无目的地看 — 每次要有目标)

## 💡 Tips for Using Resources | 资源使用技巧

### Do | 推荐做法

1. **Start with official materials first** — Cambridge books are the gold standard
   **先用官方材料** — 剑桥书籍是黄金标准
2. **Mix different resource types** — Combine books, apps, and videos
   **混合使用不同资源类型** — 结合书籍、应用和视频
3. **Focus on quality over quantity** — One good book beats five mediocre ones
   **质量重于数量** — 一本好书胜过五本一般的书
4. **Update regularly** — New editions reflect the latest exam trends
   **定期更新** — 新版本反映最新考试趋势
5. **Track your progress** — Use the [score tracker template](../templates/score-tracker.md)
   **追踪进度** — 使用[分数追踪模板](../templates/score-tracker.md)

### Don't | 避免做法

1. **Don't buy everything at once** — Start with essentials, add as needed
   **不要一次性购买所有资源** — 从必备开始，按需添加
2. **Don't rely on one resource only** — Different sources complement each other
   **不要只依赖一个资源** — 不同来源互补
3. **Don't use outdated materials** — IELTS format evolves over time
   **不要使用过时材料** — 雅思形式会随时间变化
4. **Don't skip official sources** — They are the most accurate
   **不要跳过官方来源** — 它们最准确

## 🌟 Recommended Study Path | 推荐学习路径

### Phase 1: Assessment (Week 1) | 第一阶段：评估 (第一周)

Take a full mock test to identify your current level and weakest areas.
做一次完整模考，确定当前水平和最薄弱领域。

**Resources needed | 需要的资源**: Cambridge IELTS book, timer, error notebook

### Phase 2: Foundation (Weeks 2-4) | 第二阶段：基础 (第 2-4 周)

Focus on building core skills in your weakest section.
专注于最薄弱科目的核心技能建设。

**Resources needed | 需要的资源**: Section-specific books, strategy websites, YouTube channels

### Phase 3: Practice (Weeks 5-8) | 第三阶段：练习 (第 5-8 周)

Intensive practice with real exam materials under timed conditions.
在计时条件下用真实考试材料进行强化练习。

**Resources needed | 需要的资源**: Cambridge IELTS books (multiple), mock test websites, error notebook

### Phase 4: Polish (Final 2 weeks) | 第四阶段：冲刺 (最后 2 周)

Simulate real exam conditions, review error patterns, build confidence.
模拟真实考试条件，复习错误模式，建立信心。

**Resources needed | 需要的资源**: Latest Cambridge IELTS book, speaking partner, score tracker

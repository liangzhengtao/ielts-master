## 🏛️ Category 1: Official Sources | 官方来源

These are the **must-visit** websites — the organizations that create and administer IELTS.

这些是**必访**网站 — 创建和管理雅思的组织。

### 1. IELTS.org — Official IELTS Website | 官方雅思网站

- **URL**: https://ielts.org
- **Best for | 最适合**: Official test information, registration, free practice tests
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: The single source of truth for IELTS. Download free sample questions, understand the test format, check score requirements for institutions.
- **Chinese tip | 中文提示**: 提供中文界面，可以查看中国区考点和考试时间。

### 2. Take IELTS — British Council | 英国文化协会

- **URL**: https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org
- **Best for | 最适合**: Free preparation resources, practice tests, video tutorials
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: Offers free online preparation course "Road to IELTS" (limited version), practice tests, and expert tips.
- **Chinese tip | 中文提示**: 英国文化协会在中国设有多个考点，可在网站上预约考试。

### 3. IELTS IDP | IDP 雅思

- **URL**: https://ielts.idp.com
- **Best for | 最适合**: Test booking, preparation materials, score checking
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: One of the two official test administrators. Provides free practice materials and detailed test information.
- **Chinese tip | 中文提示**: IDP 与 British Council 共同管理雅思考试，内容和评分标准完全一致。

## 📖 Category 2: Strategy & Tips | 策略与技巧

Websites that teach you **how** to take the test, not just content.

教你**如何**考试的网站，不只是内容。

### 4. IELTS Liz

- **URL**: https://ieltsliz.com
- **Best for | 最适合**: Writing Task 2 strategies, video lessons, sample answers
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: Created by a former IELTS examiner. Clear, practical advice with video explanations. Excellent for understanding what examiners look for.
- **Key features | 主要特点**: Free video lessons, model answers, detailed writing tips
- **Chinese tip | 中文提示**: 网站内容简洁易懂，即使英语基础较弱也能跟上。

### 5. IELTS Simon (ielts-simon.com)

- **URL**: https://ielts-simon.com
- **Best for | 最适合**: Writing techniques, daily lessons, exam strategies
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: Run by another former IELTS examiner. Famous for his "simple but effective" approach to writing. Daily lessons are gold.
- **Key features | 主要特点**: Daily writing tips, straightforward advice, real exam experience
- **Chinese tip | 中文提示**: Simon 的写作方法强调"简洁有效"，适合中国考生摆脱模板依赖。

### 6. IELTS Advantage

- **URL**: https://ieltsadvantage.com
- **Best for | 最适合**: Writing and Speaking improvement, step-by-step guides
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: Provides detailed guides for each section with clear structures and examples. Good for understanding the difference between Band 6 and Band 8.
- **Key features | 主要特点**: Section-by-section guides, score improvement strategies, video courses (some paid)

### 7. LearnEnglish — British Council

- **URL**: https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org
- **Best for | 最适合**: General English improvement, grammar, vocabulary
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: Not IELTS-specific but excellent for building English fundamentals. Free grammar explanations, vocabulary exercises, and listening practice.
- **Key features | 主要特点**: Free courses, grammar guides, vocabulary builders, mobile-friendly
- **Chinese tip | 中文提示**: 适合基础薄弱的考生先打基础，再进入专门的雅思练习。

## 📝 Category 3: Practice & Tests | 练习与测试

Websites with practice questions, mock tests, and sample materials.

提供练习题、模拟测试和样题材料的网站。

### 8. IELTS Buddy

- **URL**: https://www.ieltsbuddy.com
- **Best for | 最适合**: All sections practice, sample answers, vocabulary
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: Comprehensive free resource covering all four sections with practice questions, sample answers, and explanations.
- **Key features | 主要特点**: Free practice for all sections, model answers, vocabulary lists

### 9. IELTS Mentor

- **URL**: https://www.ielts-mentor.com
- **Best for | 最适合**: Large collection of practice questions, sample essays
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: Huge database of practice questions organized by type. Great for targeted practice on specific question types.
- **Key features | 主要特点**: 1000+ practice questions, organized by type, free access

### 10. IELTS Online Tests

- **URL**: https://ieltsonlinetests.com
- **Best for | 最适合**: Full-length mock tests, timed practice
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: Offers free full-length mock tests with automatic scoring. Simulates real test conditions with timer.
- **Key features | 主要特点**: Free mock tests, automatic scoring, detailed explanations
- **Chinese tip | 中文提示**: 支持中文界面，可以方便地进行计时练习。

### 11. Road to IELTS

- **URL**: https://roadtoielts.britishcouncil.org
- **Best for | 最适合**: Structured online course, progressive learning
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: Official British Council online course. Free version has limited content; paid version is comprehensive. Excellent structure.
- **Key features | 主要特点**: Structured lessons, interactive exercises, progress tracking
- **Pricing | 价格**: Free (limited) / Paid (full access)

### 12. 4Tests IELTS

- **URL**: https://www.4tests.com/ielts
- **Best for | 最适合**: Quick practice tests, self-assessment
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: Simple, no-registration-required practice tests for quick self-assessment.
- **Key features | 主要特点**: No sign-up required, quick tests, immediate results

### 13. IELTS Exam

- **URL**: https://www.ielts-exam.net
- **Best for | 最适合**: Practice tests with answers, past exam questions
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: Collection of practice tests with answer keys and explanations.
- **Key features | 主要特点**: Past exam questions, answer keys, free access

## 🎧 Category 4: Listening & Reading Practice | 听力与阅读练习

Websites that help you improve general English skills needed for IELTS.

帮助你提高雅思所需通用英语技能的网站。

### 14. BBC Learning English

- **URL**: https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish
- **Best for | 最适合**: Listening practice, current affairs vocabulary, pronunciation
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: High-quality audio and video content updated daily. Perfect for improving listening skills with British English accents.
- **Key features | 主要特点**: Daily lessons, podcasts, grammar guides, vocabulary
- **Chinese tip | 中文提示**: 适合每天花 15-30 分钟做听力训练，内容多样不枯燥。

### 15. ELLLO (English Listening Lesson Library Online)

- **URL**: https://www.elllo.org
- **Best for | 最适合**: Listening practice with various accents
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: Over 3,000 free listening lessons with transcripts. Covers multiple English accents (American, British, Australian, etc.).
- **Key features | 主要特点**: 3000+ lessons, multiple accents, free transcripts, quiz questions

### 16. Breaking News English

- **URL**: https://breakingnewsenglish.com
- **Best for | 最适合**: Reading comprehension, vocabulary building
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: Real news stories adapted for English learners at different levels. Each lesson includes reading, vocabulary, and comprehension activities.
- **Key features | 主要特点**: Leveled lessons, current events, vocabulary exercises, free

## ✍️ Category 5: Writing & Grammar | 写作与语法

Websites focused on improving your writing and grammar skills.

专注于提高写作和语法技能的网站。

### 17. Write & Fix (writefix.com)

- **URL**: https://www.writefix.com
- **Best for | 最适合**: IELTS essay writing, common mistakes
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: Specifically designed for IELTS writing. Shows common mistakes and how to fix them. Great for self-editing.
- **Key features | 主要特点**: IELTS-specific, error correction, sample essays

### 18. English Grammar (englishgrammar.org)

- **URL**: https://www.englishgrammar.org
- **Best for | 最适合**: Grammar rules, usage explanations
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: Comprehensive grammar reference. Useful for checking specific grammar rules before writing essays.
- **Key features | 主要特点**: Clear explanations, examples, quizzes

### 19. Academic English Cafe

- **URL**: https://www.aec.edu
- **Best for | 最适合**: Academic writing, vocabulary, paragraph structure
- **Why use it | 为什么用**: Focused on academic English, which is exactly what IELTS tests. Good for improving formal writing style.
- **Key features | 主要特点**: Academic vocabulary, paragraph exercises, essay structure guides

## 📚 Category 6: Vocabulary Building | 词汇构建

### 20. Vocabulary.com

- **URL**: https://www.vocabulary.com

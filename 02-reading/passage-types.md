## Academic 类文章类型 | Academic Passage Types

Academic 阅读的三篇文章通常来自**真实出版物**，包括书籍、学术期刊、杂志和报纸。

The three passages in Academic Reading come from **real publications** including books, academic journals, magazines, and newspapers.

### 来源分类 | Source Categories

| 来源 | 特点 | 举例 |
|------|------|------|
| **学术期刊** | 专业术语多，论证严密 | Nature, Science, The Lancet |
| **科普书籍** | 语言相对易懂，逻辑清晰 | A Short History of Nearly Everything |
| **杂志/报纸** | 时事性强，观点鲜明 | The Economist, National Geographic |
| **政府报告** | 数据密集，客观描述 | UNESCO reports, WHO publications |

### 文章体裁 | Text Genres

#### 1. 说明文 (Expository) — 最常见

**特点 / Features**:
- 客观描述事物、现象或过程
- 使用第三人称
- 逻辑清晰，按时间/空间/重要性排列

**常见结构 / Common Structures**:
```
定义 → 分类 → 举例 → 总结
Definition → Classification → Examples → Summary

问题 → 原因 → 解决方案
Problem → Causes → Solutions

现象 → 理论 → 证据 → 结论
Phenomenon → Theory → Evidence → Conclusion
```

**示例话题 / Example Topics**:
- 动物行为（Animal behavior）
- 环境问题（Environmental issues）
- 科技发展（Technological advances）
- 历史事件（Historical events）

#### 2. 议论文 (Argumentative)

**特点 / Features**:
- 提出观点并进行论证
- 可能包含正反两方观点
- 使用转折词（however, nevertheless, on the other hand）

**常见结构 / Common Structures**:
```
论点 → 论据1 → 论据2 → 反驳 → 结论
Thesis → Argument 1 → Argument 2 → Counter-argument → Conclusion

立场A → 立场B → 作者评价
Position A → Position B → Author's evaluation
```

**示例话题 / Example Topics**:
- 教育方法（Education methods）
- 城市规划（Urban planning）
- 医学争议（Medical debates）
- 经济政策（Economic policies）

#### 3. 叙述文 (Narrative)

**特点 / Features**:
- 按时间顺序讲述事件
- 可能包含人物和情节
- 相对较少出现

**示例话题 / Example Topics**:
- 历史发现（Historical discoveries）
- 探险故事（Exploration stories）
- 科学研究过程（Research processes）

#### 4. 分析文 (Analytical)

**特点 / Features**:
- 深入分析某个主题
- 多角度探讨
- 数据和研究引用

**示例话题 / Example Topics**:
- 社会现象分析（Social phenomena analysis）
- 商业案例研究（Business case studies）
- 心理学研究（Psychology research）

## General Training 类文章类型 | GT Passage Types

General Training 的文章更加**生活化和实用化**，分为三个 Section。

GT Reading passages are more **practical and everyday-oriented**, divided into three sections.

### Section 1: 日常生活 | Social Survival

**特点 / Features**:
- 2-3 篇短文
- 内容涉及日常生活
- 语言简单直接

**常见文本类型 / Common Text Types**:
- 广告（Advertisements）
- 通知/公告（Notices/Announcements）
- 时刻表（Timetables）
- 菜单（Menus）
- 产品说明（Product descriptions）
- 租房信息（Accommodation listings）
- 活动介绍（Event descriptions）

**示例 / Example**:
```
RIVERSIDE COMMUNITY CENTRE
Summer Programme 2025

Swimming Lessons
Beginners: Mon/Wed 9:00-10:00am - $50/term
Intermediate: Tue/Thu 10:00-11:30am - $65/term
Advanced: Fri 4:00-5:30pm - $70/term

All equipment provided. Enrolment closes 15th June.
Contact: admin@riversidecc.org or call 0800 123 456
```

### Section 2: 工作相关 | Work Survival

**特点 / Features**:
- 2-3 篇短文
- 涉及工作场所和职业
- 语言正式一些

**常见文本类型 / Common Text Types**:
- 公司手册（Company handbooks）
- 培训材料（Training materials）
- 工作描述（Job descriptions）
- 健康安全指南（Health & safety guidelines）
- 员工须知（Employee notices）
- 会议记录（Meeting minutes）

**示例 / Example**:
```
EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK - SECTION 4: LEAVE POLICY

Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 25 days annual leave per 
calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Leave must be 
approved by your line manager at least two weeks in advance.

Sick Leave
For absences of more than 3 consecutive days, a medical 
certificate is required. Please notify your supervisor before 
9:00am on the first day of absence.
```

### Section 3: 学术阅读 | Academic Reading

**特点 / Features**:
- 1 篇较长的学术文章
- 类似 Academic 类的难度
- 来源与 Academic 类相似

**常见话题 / Common Topics**:
- 社会议题（Social issues）
- 科普知识（Popular science）
- 历史文化（History and culture）
- 环境保护（Environmental protection）

## 文章结构分析 | Passage Structure Analysis

### 常见段落结构 | Common Paragraph Structures

#### 结构一：总分总 (General-Specific-General)

```
段落1: 引入话题 + 提出观点
       Introduce topic + State main idea

段落2-4: 分论点 + 证据/例子
         Sub-arguments + Evidence/Examples

段落5: 总结 + 展望
       Conclusion + Future outlook
```

**识别方法 / How to Identify**:
- 首段有 "In recent years...", "It is widely believed that..."
- 末段有 "In conclusion...", "To sum up..."

#### 结构二：问题-解决 (Problem-Solution)

```
段落1-2: 描述问题或现象
         Describe problem or phenomenon

段落3-4: 提出可能的解决方案
         Propose possible solutions

段落5: 评估方案 / 总结
       Evaluate solutions / Summarize
```

**识别方法 / How to Identify**:
- 关键词: "problem", "issue", "challenge", "solution", "approach", "strategy"
- 转折: "However", "Despite this", "Nevertheless"

#### 结构三：时间顺序 (Chronological)

```
段落1: 历史背景
       Historical background

段落2-3: 发展过程
         Development process

段落4: 现状
       Current situation

段落5: 未来展望
       Future outlook
```

**识别方法 / How to Identify**:
- 时间词: "In the 19th century", "By the 1950s", "In recent decades"
- 顺序词: "first", "then", "subsequently", "finally"

#### 结构四：对比比较 (Compare & Contrast)

```
段落1: 引入两个主题
       Introduce two subjects

段落2-3: 主题A的特点
         Characteristics of Subject A

段落4-5: 主题B的特点 + 与A的对比
         Characteristics of Subject B + Comparison
```

**识别方法 / How to Identify**:
- 对比词: "while", "whereas", "in contrast", "on the other hand", "similarly", "differently"

## 不同类型的阅读策略 | Reading Strategies by Type

### 说明文阅读策略 | Reading Expository Texts

```
1. 快速浏览首段，了解主题
   Skim the first paragraph to understand the topic

2. 识别每段的主题句（通常在段首）
   Identify topic sentences (usually at the beginning)

3. 注意信号词：for example, such as, including
   Watch for signal words: for example, such as, including

4. 关注数据和研究结果
   Pay attention to data and research findings

5. 记住因果关系（cause-effect）
   Remember cause-effect relationships
```

### 议论文阅读策略 | Reading Argumentative Texts

```
1. 找出作者的立场/论点
   Find the author's position/thesis

2. 区分事实和观点（fact vs opinion）
   Distinguish facts from opinions

3. 注意转折词后的观点（however, but, yet）
   Focus on views after transition words

4. 标记正反两方的论据
   Mark arguments for and against

5. 关注结论段
   Pay attention to the conclusion paragraph
```

### 叙述文阅读策略 | Reading Narrative Texts

```
1. 抓住时间线索
   Follow the timeline

2. 识别关键人物和事件
   Identify key people and events

3. 注意因果关系
   Note cause-effect relationships

4. 关注结果和影响
   Focus on outcomes and impacts
```

### General Training 短文阅读策略 | Reading GT Short Texts

```
1. 先看题目的具体问题
   Read specific questions first

2. 扫描找关键词（数字、大写字母、专有名词）
   Scan for keywords (numbers, capitals, proper nouns)

3. 注意小字和脚注
   Read fine print and footnotes

4. 比较多个文本的信息
   Compare information across multiple texts
```

## 真题实例分析 | Real Examples

### 实例 1: 科技类文章分析 | Example 1: Technology Article

**原文片段 / Passage Excerpt**:
```
The development of artificial intelligence has raised fundamental 
questions about the nature of consciousness. While machines can now 
defeat humans at complex games like chess and Go, researchers remain 
divided on whether this constitutes genuine intelligence or merely 
sophisticated pattern recognition. Professor Sarah Chen of MIT argues 
that 'true intelligence requires understanding, not just processing 
power.' However, her colleague Dr. James Liu counters that 'we may 
need to redefine intelligence itself, rather than impose human-centric 
criteria on non-biological systems.'
```

**分析要点 / Analysis Points**:

| 元素 | 内容 | Element | Content |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| **主题** | AI与意识 | **Topic** | AI and consciousness |
| **结构** | 引入 → 争议 → 正方 → 反方 | **Structure** | Intro → Debate → For → Against |
| **观点A** | AI不是真正的智能 (Sarah Chen) | **View A** | AI is not real intelligence |
| **观点B** | 需要重新定义智能 (James Liu) | **View B** | Need to redefine intelligence |
| **信号词** | While, However | **Signal words** | While, However |

**可能出题 / Possible Questions**:
- Matching Headings: "Debates surrounding AI capabilities"
- T/F/NG: "Professor Chen believes AI has achieved true consciousness" → FALSE
- Multiple Choice: What is Dr. Liu's position?

### 实例 2: 自然科学类分析 | Example 2: Natural Science

**原文片段 / Passage Excerpt**:
```
For centuries, scientists believed that the deep ocean floor was a 
barren wasteland, devoid of life. This assumption was shattered in 
1977 when geologists exploring the Galápagos Rift discovered thriving 
communities of organisms around hydrothermal vents — underwater 
geysers that spew superheated water rich in minerals. These ecosystems 
operate entirely independently of sunlight, deriving energy instead 
from chemical reactions in a process known as chemosynthesis. The 
discovery fundamentally altered our understanding of where life can 
exist and has significant implications for the search for 
extraterrestrial life.
```

**分析要点 / Analysis Points**:

| 元素 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| **主题** | 深海热泉生态系统 |
| **关键年份** | 1977 |
| **关键地点** | Galápagos Rift |
| **核心概念** | chemosynthesis (化学合成) |
| **影响** | 改变了对生命存在条件的理解 |

**可能出题 / Possible Questions**:
- Sentence Completion: "Scientists previously thought the ocean floor was _______"
  → Answer: "barren" or "a barren wasteland"
- T/F/NG: "Hydrothermal vents were first discovered in the 1990s" → FALSE (1977)
- Summary Completion: 关于 chemosynthesis 的填空

### 实例 3: General Training Section 1 | Example 3: GT Section 1

**原文片段 / Passage Excerpt**:
```
MELBOURNE PUBLIC LIBRARY

Join our exciting summer reading challenge! Read 10 books between 
December 1st and February 28th and earn rewards.

TEENS (13-17):
- Complete 10 books → Movie ticket + certificate

CHILDREN (Under 13):
```

## 快速识别文章类型技巧 | Quick Tips to Identify Passage Types

### 看首段判断 | Identify from First Paragraph

| 首段特征 | 文章类型 |
|----------|----------|
| "Recent studies have shown that..." | 科学研究类 |
| "It is often argued that..." | 议论/观点类 |
| "In 1859, Charles Darwin published..." | 历史/传记类 |
| "The phenomenon of... has puzzled scientists" | 现象解释类 |
| "Imagine a world where..." | 推测/未来类 |
| "For thousands of years, humans have..." | 历史/文化类 |

### 看段落数量判断 | Identify from Number of Paragraphs

| 段落数 | 通常特征 |
|--------|----------|
| 5-7 段 | 正常长度，中等难度 |
| 8-12 段 | 信息分散，需要整合 |
| 3-4 段 | 长段落，需要精读能力 |

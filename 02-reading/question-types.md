## 1. 判断题类 | True/False/Not Given & Yes/No/Not Given

### 1.1 True/False/Not Given

#### 题型特点 | Question Features

- 考查对**事实信息**的理解
- 给出一系列陈述句，判断与文章内容是否一致
- 答案只能是 TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN（注意大写）

#### 三种答案的定义 | Definitions

| 答案 | 定义 | Definition |
|------|------|------------|
| **TRUE** | 与文章内容**完全一致** | Exactly matches the passage |
| **FALSE** | 与文章内容**直接矛盾** | Directly contradicts the passage |
| **NOT GIVEN** | 文章中**没有提及**相关信息 | Information not in the passage |

#### 核心策略 | Core Strategies

```
步骤一：划出题目中的关键词
Step 1: Circle keywords in the statement

步骤二：在文章中定位相关段落
Step 2: Locate the relevant paragraph

步骤三：仔细对比题目和原文
Step 3: Compare the statement with the passage carefully

步骤四：判断是"一致"、"矛盾"还是"未提及"
Step 4: Determine if it matches, contradicts, or is not mentioned
```

#### 实例解析 | Example Analysis

**文章原文 / Passage**:
```
The university was founded in 1857 and originally consisted of just 
three departments: Arts, Science, and Law. By 1920, it had expanded 
to include Medicine and Engineering. The university now has over 
15,000 students enrolled across eight faculties.
```

**题目 1 / Question 1**:
> "The university was established in the second half of the 19th century."

**分析 / Analysis**:
- 关键词: established, second half, 19th century
- 原文: founded in 1857
- 1857 = 19世纪下半叶 ✓
- **答案: TRUE**

**题目 2 / Question 2**:
> "The university began with five academic departments."

**分析 / Analysis**:
- 关键词: began, five departments
- 原文: originally consisted of just **three** departments
- 五 ≠ 三，直接矛盾
- **答案: FALSE**

**题目 3 / Question 3**:
> "The university's medical school is the largest faculty."

**分析 / Analysis**:
- 关键词: medical school, largest
- 原文提到有 Medicine，但没有说它是最大的
- 文章没有提供比较信息
- **答案: NOT GIVEN**

#### 常见陷阱 | Common Traps

| 陷阱 | 说明 | Example |
|------|------|---------|
| **部分正确** | 句子只有一部分正确 | "The university was founded in 1857 and has 20,000 students" → 前半对，后半错 = FALSE |
| **程度词改变** | some → all, often → always | 原文 "some students" vs 题目 "all students" → FALSE |
| **因果颠倒** | A导致B vs B导致A | 原文 "A caused B" vs 题目 "B caused A" → FALSE |
| **混淆未提及** | 文章有相关话题但没有回答问题 | 文章谈了大学历史，但没说具体排名 → NOT GIVEN |
| **同义替换** | 用不同词汇表达相同意思 | 原文 "expand" vs 题目 "grow" → TRUE |

#### 判断 NOT GIVEN 的关键技巧 | Key Tips for NOT GIVEN

当以下情况出现时，很可能是 NOT GIVEN:
- 题目涉及**比较**，但原文没有比较 (Comparison not in passage)
- 题目涉及**原因**，但原文只描述了现象 (Reason not given)
- 题目涉及**目的/意图**，但原文只描述了行为 (Purpose not stated)
- 题目涉及**具体数字/时间**，但原文没有提供 (Specific number not mentioned)

### 1.2 Yes/No/Not Given

#### 与 T/F/NG 的区别 | Difference from T/F/NG

| 对比项 | True/False/Not Given | Yes/No/Not Given |
|--------|---------------------|------------------|
| **考查内容** | 事实信息 Facts | 观点、看法 Views/Opinions |
| **题目特征** | 描述客观事实 | 包含 "believe", "argue", "claim", "suggest" |
| **答案含义** | 与事实一致/矛盾/未提及 | 与作者观点一致/矛盾/未提及 |

#### 实例解析 | Example Analysis

**文章原文 / Passage**:
```
Professor Williams believes that climate change poses the greatest 
threat to global food security. She argues that without immediate 
action, crop yields could decline by up to 25% within the next 
two decades. However, not all scientists share this pessimistic 
view. Dr. Thompson, for instance, contends that technological 
advances in agriculture will be sufficient to offset any negative 
effects of climate change.
```

**题目 1 / Question 1**:
> "Climate change is the biggest danger to the world's food supply."

**分析 / Analysis**:
- 这是 Professor Williams 的观点
- 原文: "poses the greatest threat to global food security"
- 最大威胁 = biggest danger ✓
- **答案: YES**

**题目 2 / Question 2**:
> "Every scientist agrees that climate change will harm food production."

**分析 / Analysis**:
- 原文: "not all scientists share this pessimistic view"
- 不是所有科学家都同意 = 不是每个科学家都同意
- **答案: NO**

**题目 3 / Question 3**:
> "Dr. Thompson has published research on agricultural technology."

**分析 / Analysis**:
- 文章提到 Dr. Thompson 的观点
- 但没有说他是否发表了研究论文
- **答案: NOT GIVEN**

## 2. 配对题类 | Matching

### 2.1 Matching Headings | 标题配对

#### 题型特点 | Question Features

- 给出一组标题（A-J 等），为每个段落选择最合适的标题
- 标题数量**多于**段落数量（干扰项）
- **考查段落主旨**，不是细节

#### 核心策略 | Core Strategies

```
步骤一：先读所有标题，理解大意
Step 1: Read all headings first

步骤二：逐段阅读，找到每段的主题句
Step 2: Read each paragraph and find the topic sentence

步骤三：主题句通常在段首或段尾
Step 3: Topic sentences are usually at the start or end

步骤四：排除法——用过的标题划掉，缩小范围
Step 4: Process of elimination — cross out used headings

步骤五：最后处理难段——看剩下的标题
Step 5: Handle difficult paragraphs last — check remaining headings
```

#### 实例解析 | Example Analysis

**标题选项 / Heading Options**:
```
(i)   The need for new regulations
(ii)  Historical background of the industry
(iii) Environmental consequences of mining
(iv)  Modern extraction techniques
(v)   Economic benefits of the resource
(vi)  Opposition from local communities
```

**段落 / Paragraph A**:
```
Gold mining has a long history dating back to ancient civilizations. 
The Romans were among the first to develop systematic mining methods, 
using hydraulic techniques to extract gold from riverbeds in Spain 
and Wales. During the California Gold Rush of 1849, thousands of 
prospectors migrated westward in search of fortune, fundamentally 
transforming the American landscape.
```

**分析 / Analysis**:
- 关键词: long history, ancient, Romans, California Gold Rush 1849
- 全段在讲历史背景
- **答案: (ii) Historical background of the industry**

#### 技巧要点 | Key Tips

| 技巧 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **不要看细节** | 关注段落的**核心主题**，忽略例子和数据 |
| **警惕干扰项** | 标题可能包含段落中出现的词，但主旨不对 |
| **先易后难** | 先做确定的段落，最后用排除法做难的 |
| **主题句位置** | 英文段落主题句通常在**第一句**或**最后一句** |

### 2.2 Matching Information | 信息配对

#### 题型特点 | Question Features

- 给出若干信息/描述，找出哪段包含该信息
- **可能乱序**——信息可能出现在文章的任何段落
- 一个段落可能被选多次

#### 核心策略 | Core Strategies

```
1. 仔细读题，理解每条信息的含义
   Read questions carefully, understand each piece of information

2. 划出关键词（特别是具体数据、人名、年份）
   Circle keywords (especially numbers, names, dates)

3. 逐条回文章搜索，不要按段落顺序做
   Search passage for each item — don't follow paragraph order

4. 注意同义替换
   Watch for paraphrasing
```

#### 实例解析 | Example Analysis

**题目 / Question**:
> "Which paragraph contains information about the economic cost of the project?"

**关键词 / Keywords**: economic cost, project

**文章相关句 / Relevant Sentence** (in Paragraph C):
```
The financial burden of the initiative proved substantial, with total 
expenditure reaching $2.3 billion — nearly double the initial budget.
```

**分析 / Analysis**:
- economic cost = financial burden, expenditure
- project = initiative
- **答案: Paragraph C**

---

### 2.3 Matching Features | 特征配对

#### 题型特点 | Question Features

- 给出一组人物/理论/地点等，将描述与之配对
- 选项可重复使用
- **通常乱序**

#### 核心策略 | Core Strategies

```
1. 先在文章中找到所有人名/理论名，做标记
   Find all names/theories in the passage and mark them

2. 回到题目，逐条定位
   Return to questions and locate each one

3. 注意：一个人可能有多个观点
   Note: One person may hold multiple views

4. 注意引用符号："..." 表示直接引用
   Watch for quotation marks: "..." indicates direct quotes
```

#### 实例解析 | Example Analysis

**选项 / Options**:
```
A - Dr. Smith
B - Professor Johnson
C - Dr. Lee
D - Professor Brown
```

**题目 / Questions**:
> 1. Believes the project will be completed on time
> 2. Has concerns about the environmental impact
> 3. Developed the original theory in the 1990s

**文章相关句 / Relevant Sentences**:
```
Dr. Smith expressed confidence in the timeline: 'We're on track to 
finish by December.' However, Professor Johnson has repeatedly warned 
that the construction could damage the local ecosystem. The theoretical 
framework was first proposed by Dr. Lee in 1994.
```

**答案 / Answers**:
1. A (Dr. Smith)
2. B (Professor Johnson)
3. C (Dr. Lee)

## 3. 填空题类 | Completion

### 3.1 Sentence Completion | 句子填空

#### 题型特点 | Question Features

- 根据文章内容完成句子
- 答案**必须来自原文**，不能自己编造
- 注意字数限制（如 NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS）

#### 核心策略 | Core Strategies

```
步骤一：读题，理解句子意思
Step 1: Read the question, understand the sentence

步骤二：划出句子中的关键词
Step 2: Circle keywords in the sentence

步骤三：在文章中定位（顺序原则）
Step 3: Locate in the passage (order principle)

步骤四：找到答案后，检查语法是否正确
Step 4: Check if the answer is grammatically correct

步骤五：检查字数限制
Step 5: Check the word limit
```

#### 实例解析 | Example Analysis

**文章原文 / Passage**:
```
The research team collected samples from various locations along the 
river. After extensive analysis, they discovered that water pollution 
levels had increased dramatically over the past five years, primarily 
due to industrial waste from nearby factories.
```

**题目 / Question**:
> Water pollution in the river has risen significantly because of 
> _______ from factories in the area. (NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS)

**分析 / Analysis**:
- 关键词: risen significantly, factories
- 原文: "increased dramatically" = "risen significantly" (同义替换)
- 原文: "primarily due to industrial waste from nearby factories"
- **答案: industrial waste**（2 个词，符合字数限制）

#### 字数限制规则 | Word Limit Rules

| 限制 | 可接受答案 | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| ONE WORD ONLY | 只能一个词 | "pollution" |
| NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS | 最多两个词 | "industrial waste" |
| NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER | 最多三个词和/或一个数字 | "25 percent increase" |

> **注意 / Important**:
> - "a" 和 "the" 算一个词
> - 连字符词算一个词 (e.g., "well-known" = 1 word)
> - 数字算一个词 (e.g., "1857" = 1 word)

### 3.2 Summary Completion | 摘要填空

#### 题型特点 | Question Features

- 文章的摘要，挖掉若干关键词
- 可能从文章中选词，也可能从选项中选词
- 摘要可能只覆盖文章的部分段落

#### 两种形式 | Two Formats

| 形式 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **从文章选词** | Word bank from passage |
| **从选项选词** | Word bank provided in question |

#### 核心策略 | Core Strategies

```
1. 通读摘要，理解大意
   Read the summary to understand the overall meaning

2. 确定摘要对应文章的哪部分
   Identify which part of the passage the summary covers

3. 逐空填词，注意语法和词性
   Fill each blank, check grammar and word form

4. 如果有选项，用排除法
   If options are given, use elimination

5. 检查答案是否让摘要逻辑通顺
   Check if answers make the summary logically coherent
```

#### 实例解析 | Example Analysis

**选项 / Word Bank**:
```
A. species    B. habitat    C. migration    D. extinction
E. breeding   F. decline    G. conservation  H. predators
```

**摘要 / Summary**:
```
Many animal 1. _______ are facing 2. _______ due to loss of natural 
3. _______. Conservation programs focus on protecting 4. _______ 
grounds and reducing the impact of 5. _______.
```

**文章相关段落 / Passage**:
```
Numerous species worldwide are experiencing rapid population decline. 
The primary cause is habitat destruction, which forces animals into 
smaller areas. Conservation efforts prioritize protecting breeding 
sites and managing predator populations.
```

**答案 / Answers**:
1. A (species)
2. F (decline)
3. B (habitat)
4. E (breeding)
5. H (predators)

### 3.3 Note/Table/Flow Chart Completion

#### 题型特点 | Question Features

| 类型 | 特点 |
|------|------|
| **Note Completion** | 笔记形式，信息点式 |
| **Table Completion** | 表格形式，行列对比 |
| **Flow Chart Completion** | 流程图，按顺序排列 |

#### 核心策略 | Core Strategies

```
1. 观察结构：了解信息的组织方式
   Observe structure: understand how information is organized

2. 利用已知信息定位
   Use known information to locate answers

3. 注意顺序关系（特别是流程图）
   Pay attention to order (especially flow charts)

4. 语法检查：确保答案在语法上正确
   Grammar check: ensure answers are grammatically correct
```

#### 实例解析：流程图 | Example: Flow Chart

**题目 / Question**:
```
The Process of Making Cheese

Fresh milk → Heat to 1. _______°C → Add 2. _______ →
Wait for curds to form → Separate curds from 3. _______ →
Press and shape → 4. _______ for weeks/months
```

**文章相关段落 / Passage**:
```
Cheese production begins with fresh milk, which is heated to 
approximately 32°C. A bacterial culture and rennet are then added 
to cause the milk to coagulate. Once curds have formed, they are 
separated from the liquid whey. The curds are pressed into molds 
and then aged in controlled conditions for a period ranging from 
a few weeks to several months.
```

**答案 / Answers**:
1. 32 (原文: heated to approximately 32°C)
2. bacterial culture (或 rennet, 原文: A bacterial culture and rennet are added)
3. whey (原文: separated from the liquid whey)
4. Aged (原文: aged in controlled conditions)

### 3.4 Diagram Label Completion | 图表标签填空

#### 题型特点 | Question Features

- 给出一个**示意图/图表**，要求填写标签
- 通常描述某种结构、流程或设备
- 答案直接来自文章

#### 核心策略 | Core Strategies

```
1. 理解图表展示的是什么
   Understand what the diagram represents

2. 在文章中找到描述该图的段落
   Find the paragraph describing the diagram

3. 按照空间/位置顺序填写标签
   Fill labels in spatial/positional order

4. 注意文章中描述位置关系的词汇
   Watch for positional vocabulary in the passage
```

#### 位置关键词 | Position Keywords

| 英文 | 中文 | English | Chinese |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| above | 上方 | below | 下方 |
| adjacent to | 邻近 | beneath | 正下方 |
| on the left/right | 左/右侧 | in the center | 中央 |
| at the top/bottom | 顶部/底部 | surrounding | 周围 |
| inside/within | 内部 | outer/external | 外部 |
| upper/lower | 上部/下部 | front/back | 前/后 |

## 4. Short Answer Questions | 简答题

#### 题型特点 | Question Features

- 根据文章内容回答问题
- 答案通常很短（1-3 个词）
- 按顺序出题

#### 核心策略 | Core Strategies

```
1. 看清问题：What/Where/When/Who/How
   Read the question carefully: What/Where/When/Who/How

2. 确定答案词性：问 What → 名词/名词短语
   Determine answer type: What → noun/noun phrase

3. 在文章中定位答案
   Locate the answer in the passage

4. 注意字数限制
   Watch the word limit
```

#### 实例解析 | Example Analysis

**文章原文 / Passage**:
```
The Great Barrier Reef, located off the coast of Queensland, Australia, 
is the world's largest coral reef system. It stretches over 2,300 
kilometres and is composed of over 2,900 individual reef systems. The 
reef was designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1981.
```

**题目 / Questions**:
> 1. Where is the Great Barrier Reef situated? (NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS)
> 2. How long is the reef system? (NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER)
> 3. When did it become a World Heritage Site? (ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER)

**答案 / Answers**:
1. off the coast of Queensland (或 Queensland, Australia)
2. 2,300 kilometres (或 over 2,300 kilometres)
3. 1981

## 5. Multiple Choice | 选择题

#### 题型特点 | Question Features

- 单选题（4 选 1）或多选题
- 考查细节理解或主旨大意
- 干扰项具有迷惑性

#### 两种类型 | Two Types

| 类型 | 特点 | 策略 |
|------|------|------|
| **细节题** | 问某个具体信息 | 定位后逐项对比 |
| **主旨题** | 问整段/全文大意 | 关注首尾段和每段首句 |

#### 核心策略 | Core Strategies

```
1. 先读题干，明确问什么
   Read the question stem first, know what's being asked

2. 回文章定位相关段落
   Locate the relevant paragraph

3. 逐个选项对比原文
   Compare each option with the passage

4. 排除法：先排除明显错误的
   Elimination: remove clearly wrong options first

5. 警惕"原文复现"——正确答案通常是同义替换
   Watch for "exact match" — correct answers are usually paraphrased
```

#### 实例解析 | Example Analysis

**文章原文 / Passage**:
```
Research suggests that bilingual children often outperform their 
monolingual peers in tasks requiring cognitive flexibility. This 
advantage, known as the 'bilingual effect,' appears to stem from 
the constant need to manage two language systems, which strengthens 
executive function skills. However, some researchers question whether 
this advantage persists into adulthood.
```

**题目 / Question**:
> What does the 'bilingual effect' refer to?

**选项 / Options**:
```
A. Bilingual children learn to speak earlier than monolingual children
B. Children who speak two languages show better cognitive flexibility
C. Bilingual children have higher IQ scores than monolingual children  
D. Children who speak two languages are better at learning new languages
```

**分析 / Analysis**:
- A: 文章没有提到说话更早 → 排除
- B: "outperform... in tasks requiring cognitive flexibility" ✓
- C: 文章没有提到 IQ → 排除
- D: 文章没有提到学习新语言 → 排除
- **答案: B**

#### 常见干扰项类型 | Common Distractor Types

| 干扰项类型 | 说明 | Example |
|------------|------|---------|
| **原文未提及** | 看似合理但文章没有说 | 文章没提到 IQ 分数 |
| **偷换概念** | 改变了关键信息 | "some" → "all" |
| **因果颠倒** | 原因和结果对调 | A导致B → B导致A |
| **范围扩大/缩小** | 改变了范围 | "children" → "all people" |
| **过度推断** | 从文章信息推出的结论 | 文章没明确说这个结论 |

## 6. 题型频率与难度总结 | Summary

### 题型难度排名 | Difficulty Ranking

| 排名 | 题型 | 难度 | 出现频率 | 备注 |
|------|------|------|----------|------|
| 1 | T/F/NG | ★★★★★ | 每次必考 | 最易错的题型 |
| 2 | Matching Headings | ★★★★☆ | 常见 | 考查段落主旨 |
| 3 | Matching Information | ★★★★☆ | 常见 | 乱序，耗时 |
| 4 | Multiple Choice | ★★★★☆ | 常见 | 干扰项多 |
| 5 | Matching Features | ★★★☆☆ | 偶尔 | 需要快速定位 |
| 6 | Summary Completion | ★★★☆☆ | 常见 | 需要语法配合 |
| 7 | Sentence Completion | ★★★☆☆ | 常见 | 按顺序出题 |
| 8 | Note/Table/Flow | ★★☆☆☆ | 偶尔 | 结构辅助理解 |
| 9 | Diagram Label | ★★☆☆☆ | 偶尔 | 空间词汇 |
| 10 | Short Answer | ★★☆☆☆ | 偶尔 | 简单直接 |

### 答题顺序建议 | Recommended Answer Order

```
推荐顺序 / Recommended Order:
1. 先做填空题类（Sentence/Summary/Note/Table/Flow/Diagram）
   → 答案直接来自原文，容易定位

2. 再做 Short Answer Questions
   → 简单直接

3. 然后做 Multiple Choice
   → 逐项对比

4. 接着做 T/F/NG 和 Y/N/NG
   → 需要仔细对比

5. 最后做 Matching 题
   → 可能乱序，需要全文理解

注意：实际考试中按试卷顺序做即可，但遇到难题先跳过
Note: In the actual test, follow the paper order, but skip hard questions
```

### 每种题型的通用技巧 | Universal Tips

| 技巧 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **同义替换** | 所有题型都考同义替换能力 |
| **顺序原则** | 大多数题型答案按顺序出现 |
| **关键词定位** | 先找关键词，再精读相关句子 |
| **字数限制** | 填空题必须严格遵守字数限制 |
| **不要留空** | 不确定也要猜，没有倒扣分 |

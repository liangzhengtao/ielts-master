## 📋 How to Use This Notebook | 如何使用错题本

### Purpose | 目的

An error notebook (错题本) is one of the **most effective study tools** for IELTS improvement. By recording and analyzing your mistakes, you can:

错题本是雅思提分**最有效的学习工具**之一。通过记录和分析错误，你可以：

1. **Identify patterns** in your mistakes | 识别错误模式
2. **Focus your study** on weak areas | 集中学习薄弱环节
3. **Track your progress** over time | 追踪进步
4. **Avoid repeating** the same errors | 避免重复同样的错误
5. **Build confidence** by seeing improvement | 通过看到进步建立信心

### Method | 方法

1. **Record every mistake** after practice or mock tests | 练习或模考后记录每个错误
2. **Analyze why** you made the mistake | 分析犯错原因
3. **Write the correct answer** with explanation | 写出正确答案和解释
4. **Review weekly** before starting new practice | 每周开始新练习前复习
5. **Update patterns** as you identify them | 识别出模式后更新

## 💡 Tips for Using Your Error Notebook | 错题本使用技巧

### Do | 推荐做法

1. **Be honest** — Record ALL mistakes, not just the "embarrassing" ones
   **诚实** — 记录所有错误，不只是"尴尬"的
2. **Be specific** — Write exactly what went wrong and why
   **具体** — 精确写出什么出了错以及为什么
3. **Review regularly** — Spend 15 minutes each week reviewing errors
   **定期复习** — 每周花 15 分钟复习错误
4. **Track patterns** — Look for recurring mistake types
   **追踪模式** — 寻找反复出现的错误类型
5. **Update action plans** — When you see a pattern, create a specific improvement plan
   **更新行动计划** — 看到模式时创建具体的改进计划

### Don't | 避免做法

1. **Don't just record — review** | 不要只记录不复习
2. **Don't be too vague** — "I got it wrong" isn't helpful | 不要太笼统 — "我错了"没有帮助
3. **Don't give up** — Error analysis is hard but effective | 不要放弃 — 错误分析很难但有效
4. **Don't skip the "why"** — Understanding the cause prevents repetition | 不要跳过"为什么" — 理解原因才能防止重复

### How to Calculate IELTS Band Scores | 如何计算雅思分数

**Listening & Reading (40 questions each) | 听力和阅读 (各 40 题)**:

| Correct Answers | Band Score |
|-----------------|------------|
| 39-40 | 9.0 |
| 37-38 | 8.5 |
| 35-36 | 8.0 |
| 32-34 | 7.5 |
| 30-31 | 7.0 |
| 26-29 | 6.5 |
| 23-25 | 6.0 |
| 18-22 | 5.5 |
| 16-17 | 5.0 |

**Writing & Speaking**: Scored by examers on band descriptors (0-9 scale).

**Overall Band Score**: Average of all four sections, rounded to nearest 0.5.

**总分**: 四科平均分，四舍五入到最近的 0.5。

## 💡 Tips for Score Tracking | 分数追踪技巧

### Do | 推荐做法

1. **Be consistent** — Take mock tests on the same day each week
   **保持一致** — 每周同一天做模拟考试
2. **Use official materials** — Cambridge books give the most accurate scores
   **使用官方材料** — 剑桥书籍给出最准确的分数
3. **Score honestly** — Don't inflate your scores; it won't help you improve
   **诚实评分** — 不要虚高分数；这不会帮你提高
4. **Track details** — Section-level data helps identify specific weaknesses
   **追踪细节** — 部分级别的数据有助于识别特定弱点
5. **Review trends** — Look at 4-week trends, not individual test scores
   **查看趋势** — 看 4 周趋势，而不是单次考试分数

### Don't | 避免做法

1. **Don't test too often** — Once a week is sufficient for mock tests
   **不要测试太频繁** — 每周一次模拟考试足够
2. **Don't panic over one bad score** — It's the trend that matters
   **不要因一次低分恐慌** — 趋势才重要
3. **Don't compare with others** — Focus on your own progress
   **不要与他人比较** — 专注自己的进步
4. **Don't skip scoring** — An unscored test is a wasted test
   **不要跳过评分** — 没有评分的考试是浪费

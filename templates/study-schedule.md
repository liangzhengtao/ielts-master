## 📋 How to Use This Template | 如何使用此模板

1. **Choose your level** — Select the schedule that matches your available time

2. **Fill in your targets** — Set specific goals for each session
   **填写目标** — 为每次学习设定具体目标
3. **Track completion** — Mark off what you've done
   **追踪完成** — 标记已完成的内容
4. **Review weekly** — Every Sunday, review what worked and what didn't
   **每周回顾** — 每周日回顾什么有效什么无效
5. **Adjust** — Modify the schedule based on your progress
   **调整** — 根据进度修改计划

## 💡 Tips for Following Your Schedule | 执行计划的技巧

### Do | 推荐做法

1. **Start small** — If you can't do 2 hours, do 1 hour consistently
   **从小开始** — 如果做不到 2 小时，就坚持 1 小时
2. **Be flexible** — Life happens; adjust without guilt
   **灵活** — 生活会有意外；调整即可，不要有负罪感
3. **Use a timer** — Stick to time limits for each activity
   **用计时器** — 遵守每项活动的时间限制
4. **Reward yourself** — Celebrate weekly milestones
   **奖励自己** — 庆祝每周里程碑
5. **Study at the same time** — Build a routine
   **固定时间学习** — 建立规律

### Don't | 避免做法

1. **Don't skip review days** — Review is when learning solidifies
   **不要跳过复习日** — 复习是巩固学习的时候
2. **Don't study when exhausted** — Quality > quantity
   **不要累的时候学习** — 质量 > 数量
3. **Don't change the plan daily** — Stick to it for a full week before adjusting
   **不要每天改计划** — 坚持一周再调整
4. **Don't neglect sleep** — 7-8 hours is essential for memory
   **不要忽视睡眠** — 7-8 小时对记忆至关重要

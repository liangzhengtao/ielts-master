# Task 2: Problem-Solution Essays | 任务2：问题解决类文章

> How to write problem-solution essays in IELTS Writing Task 2
> 如何在雅思写作任务2中写问题解决类文章

---

## What is a Problem-Solution Essay? | 什么是问题解决类文章？

A problem-solution essay asks you to identify **problems** related to a topic and propose **solutions**. You must analyze the causes and suggest realistic, specific remedies.
问题解决类文章要求你识别与话题相关的**问题**并提出**解决方案**。你必须分析原因并提出现实、具体的解决办法。

### Common Question Types | 常见题目类型

```
"What are the problems associated with...? What solutions can you suggest?"

"Why is this a problem? What can be done to solve it?"

"What are the causes of...? What measures can be taken to address this?"

"Some people say... is a serious problem. What are the causes and solutions?"
```

---

## Essay Structure | 文章结构

### 4-Paragraph Structure (Recommended) | 四段结构（推荐）

```
Paragraph 1: Introduction
  - Paraphrase the problem
  - State that you will discuss causes/problems and solutions

Paragraph 2: Body Paragraph 1 — Problems/Causes
  - Problem 1 + explanation + example
  - Problem 2 + explanation + example

Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 2 — Solutions
  - Solution 1 (linked to Problem 1) + explanation + example
  - Solution 2 (linked to Problem 2) + explanation + example

Paragraph 4: Conclusion
  - Summarize the problems and solutions
  - Final thought or recommendation
```

### 5-Paragraph Structure (For Extended Analysis) | 五段结构（用于扩展分析）

```
Paragraph 1: Introduction
Paragraph 2: Problem 1 (in detail)
Paragraph 3: Problem 2 (in detail)
Paragraph 4: Solutions (for both problems)
Paragraph 5: Conclusion
```

## Template: 4-Paragraph Problem-Solution Essay | 模板：四段问题解决类文章

### Paragraph 1: Introduction | 第一段：引言

```
[Paraphrase the problem/issue]. This is a growing concern in many
[societies/countries/communities]. This essay will examine the main
causes of this problem and propose some practical solutions.

OR

[Paraphrase the problem/issue]. There are several factors contributing
to this issue, and a number of measures could be implemented to address
it. This essay will discuss both the causes and potential solutions.

OR

[Paraphrase the problem/issue] has become an increasingly serious issue
in recent years. This essay will explore the root causes of this problem
and suggest some effective ways to tackle it.
```

**Example**:
```
The rising cost of housing has made it increasingly difficult for young
people to buy their first home in many major cities around the world.
This is a growing concern that affects not only individuals but also the
broader economy. This essay will examine the main causes of this housing
affordability crisis and propose some practical solutions.
```

### Paragraph 2: Problems/Causes | 第二段：问题/原因

```
There are several factors contributing to this problem. Firstly,
[Problem 1]. [Explanation]. For example, [specific example]. Secondly,
[Problem 2]. [Explanation]. This is illustrated by [example].

OR

The main causes of this issue are [cause 1] and [cause 2]. The primary
factor is [cause 1]. [Explanation and example]. In addition, [cause 2]
plays a significant role. [Explanation and example].
```

**Example**:
```
Several factors have contributed to the housing affordability crisis.
The primary cause is the imbalance between housing supply and demand.
In many cities, the construction of new homes has not kept pace with
rapid population growth, leading to intense competition for limited
properties. For example, in London, an estimated 66,000 new homes are
needed annually, but only around 35,000 are being built. Furthermore,
the influx of foreign investment in property has driven up prices, as
wealthy investors purchase homes as assets rather than places to live.
Low interest rates have also made borrowing cheaper, which has increased
demand and pushed prices even higher.
```

### Paragraph 3: Solutions | 第三段：解决方案

```
To address these problems, several measures could be taken. Firstly,
[Solution 1 linked to Problem 1]. [Explanation]. For example, [specific
example of this solution working]. Secondly, [Solution 2 linked to
Problem 2]. [Explanation]. This approach has been successfully implemented
in [example].

OR

These problems can be tackled through a combination of government action
and individual responsibility. The most effective solution would be to
[solution 1]. [Explanation and example]. Additionally, [solution 2]
could help to [outcome]. [Explanation and example].
```

**Example**:
```
To address these problems, governments could implement several measures.
Firstly, authorities should invest in the construction of affordable
housing and relax planning regulations to encourage new developments.
For example, Singapore's public housing programme has successfully
provided homes for over 80% of its population through government-led
construction initiatives. Secondly, governments could introduce stricter
regulations on foreign property investment and implement higher taxes on
empty properties to discourage speculative buying. Additionally, offering
first-time buyer incentives, such as reduced stamp duty or government-backed
mortgage schemes, could help young people get onto the property ladder.
```

### Paragraph 4: Conclusion | 第四段：结论

```
In conclusion, the problem of [issue] is caused by [main causes] and
has serious consequences for [affected groups]. However, by implementing
[solutions], it is possible to [positive outcome]. If these measures are
adopted, [prediction or hope].

OR

In conclusion, [issue] is a complex problem with multiple causes.
Nevertheless, through a combination of [solution 1] and [solution 2],
meaningful progress can be made. It is essential that [who] take
immediate action to [desired outcome].
```

**Example**:
```
In conclusion, the housing affordability crisis is driven by supply
shortages, speculative investment, and low interest rates. However, by
combining increased affordable housing construction with stricter
investment regulations and first-time buyer support, governments can
make homeownership more accessible for young people. If these measures
are implemented promptly, future generations will have a fairer chance
of achieving the dream of owning their own home.
```

## Sample Problem-Solution Essay | 问题解决类文章范文

### Question | 题目

"In many countries, the amount of waste produced by households and businesses is increasing. What are the causes of this problem, and what solutions can you suggest?"

### Full Sample Answer | 完整范文

```
The growing volume of waste generated by households and businesses has
become a pressing environmental concern in many parts of the world.
This essay will examine the primary causes of this problem and propose
some effective solutions.

Several factors have contributed to the increase in waste production.
The main cause is the rise of consumer culture and the prevalence of
disposable products. In modern society, people are accustomed to using
items once and discarding them, from plastic bags and food packaging
to electronic devices with short lifespans. For example, the average
person in the United States produces approximately 4.4 pounds of waste
per day, much of which consists of single-use packaging. Furthermore,
rapid economic growth in developing countries has led to increased
consumption and, consequently, more waste. As people earn more money,
they tend to buy more products and generate more rubbish.

To address this growing problem, a multi-faceted approach is needed.
Firstly, governments should implement stricter regulations on packaging
and encourage manufacturers to adopt sustainable practices. For instance,
the European Union's directive banning single-use plastics has already
shown promising results in reducing plastic waste. Secondly, investment
in recycling infrastructure and public education campaigns can help
increase recycling rates. Countries like Germany, which have comprehensive
recycling programmes, achieve recycling rates of over 60%, compared to
the global average of approximately 13%. Additionally, businesses should
be incentivized to adopt circular economy models, where products are
designed to be reused, repaired, or recycled rather than discarded.

In conclusion, the increase in waste is primarily driven by consumer
culture, disposable products, and economic growth. However, through a
combination of government regulation, improved recycling infrastructure,
and corporate responsibility, it is possible to significantly reduce
the amount of waste we produce. If these measures are widely adopted,
we can move towards a more sustainable future.
```

**Word count**: 280 words

## Linking Problems to Solutions | 将问题与解决方案联系起来

### Problem-Solution Pairs | 问题-解决方案配对

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Air pollution from cars | Invest in public transport and cycling infrastructure |
| Rising obesity rates | Education campaigns and regulate food advertising |
| Youth unemployment | Vocational training and internship programs |
| Traffic congestion | Congestion charges and remote work policies |
| Housing shortage | Build affordable housing and control speculation |
| Water scarcity | Invest in desalination and water conservation education |
| Crime rates | Community policing and rehabilitation programs |
| Climate change | Carbon taxes and renewable energy investment |

### Linking Language | 连接语言

```
To address this problem, [solution].
In order to tackle this issue, [solution].
One effective way to solve this is to [solution].
This problem could be alleviated by [solution].
A possible remedy for this would be to [solution].
To combat this, [who] should [solution].
This challenge can be met through [solution].
```

| Phrase | Example |
|--------|---------|

## Common Mistakes in Problem-Solution Essays | 问题解决类文章常见错误

### 1. Problems and Solutions Don't Match ❌

**Wrong**: Problem: "People are too lazy to exercise." Solution: "Build more hospitals."
**Right**: Problem: "People lack access to safe exercise spaces." Solution: "Invest in public parks and free exercise facilities."

Solutions must directly address the problems you identified.
解决方案必须直接针对你识别的问题。

### 2. Vague Solutions ❌

**Wrong**: "The government should do something about it."
**Right**: "The government should increase funding for public recycling programs and impose fines on companies that fail to meet recycling targets."

### 3. Unrealistic Solutions ❌

**Wrong**: "Ban all cars." / "End all pollution."
**Right**: "Introduce congestion charges in city centers and invest in electric vehicle infrastructure."

### 4. Not Explaining How Solutions Work ❌

Don't just state a solution. Explain WHY it would be effective and give an example.
不要只是陈述一个解决方案。解释为什么它会有效并给出一个例子。

### 5. Forgetting to Link Solutions to Problems ❌

Each solution should clearly connect to a specific problem you mentioned.
每个解决方案应清楚地连接到你提到的具体问题。

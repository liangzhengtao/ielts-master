# Task 1: Pie Charts | 任务1：饼图

> How to describe pie charts in IELTS Writing Task 1
> 如何在雅思写作任务1中描述饼图

---

## What are Pie Charts? | 什么是饼图？

Pie charts show **proportions and percentages** of a whole. Each segment represents a category's share of the total.
饼图展示**整体的比例和百分比**。每个扇形代表一个类别在总数中的份额。

### Key Features of Pie Charts | 饼图的关键特征

- **Segments**: Represent different categories
- **Percentages**: Show proportion of the whole (usually adds up to 100%)
- **Size**: Larger segments = higher proportion
- **Multiple charts**: Often compare proportions at different times

- **扇形**：代表不同的类别
- **百分比**：显示占整体的比例（通常加起来为100%）
- **大小**：较大的扇形 = 较高的比例
- **多图**：经常比较不同时间的比例

## Language for Describing Proportions | 描述比例的语言

### Fraction Language | 分数语言

| Fraction | Percentage | Example |
|----------|------------|---------|
| a half | 50% | Half of all spending went on food. |
| a third | 33% | A third of respondents preferred tea. |
| a quarter | 25% | A quarter of the budget was allocated to research. |
| two-thirds | 67% | Two-thirds of students chose science subjects. |
| three-quarters | 75% | Three-quarters of households owned a car. |
| a fifth | 20% | A fifth of the population lived in rural areas. |
| a tenth | 10% | Just a tenth of the total was spent on education. |

### Approximate Language | 近似语言

| Phrase | Percentage Range | Example |
|--------|-----------------|---------|
| just over | 51-55% | Just over half of all visitors were female. |
| just under | 45-49% | Just under a third were aged 18-25. |
| approximately | ±3% | Approximately 40% of households had pets. |
| roughly | ±5% | Roughly one in five respondents agreed. |
| around | ±5% | Around 60% of the total was from exports. |
| nearly/almost | 95-99% | Nearly a quarter of all sales were online. |
| more than | above stated % | More than half of all workers were part-time. |
| less than | below stated % | Less than 10% of energy came from renewables. |
| the majority of | >50% | The majority of income came from services. |
| a minority of | <50% | A minority of students studied abroad. |

### Ranking Proportions | 比例排名

| Phrase | Example |
|--------|---------|
| the largest segment/share | The largest share was education at 35%. |
| the smallest segment/share | The smallest proportion was healthcare at 5%. |
| the second largest | The second largest category was food at 28%. |
| accounting for [X%] | Transport, accounting for 15%, was the third largest. |
| representing [X%] | Entertainment, representing 8%, was the smallest. |
| making up [X%] | Housing costs made up 22% of total spending. |
| comprising [X%] | Savings comprised just 5% of household income. |
| constituting [X%] | Other expenses constituted the remaining 10%. |

## 4-Paragraph Structure | 四段结构

### Paragraph 1: Introduction | 第一段：引言

**Single pie chart**:
```
The pie chart illustrates the proportion of [what] in [where] in [year].

The pie chart shows the breakdown of [what] by [category] for [who/where].
```

**Multiple pie charts**:
```
The pie charts compare [what] in [where] in [year 1] and [year 2].

The two pie charts illustrate changes in the distribution of [what]
between [year] and [year].
```

**Example**:
```
The two pie charts compare the proportion of household expenditure in
the United Kingdom in 1990 and 2020.
```

### Paragraph 2: Overview | 第二段：概述

Identify the most significant changes or patterns.
找出最显著的变化或模式。

**Example**:
```
Overall, it is evident that the pattern of household spending changed
significantly over the 30-year period. While food and housing remained
the two largest categories, the proportion spent on food decreased
considerably, whereas expenditure on entertainment and transport grew
substantially.
```

### Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 1 | 第三段：主体段落1

Describe the largest or most notable categories.
描述最大或最显著的类别。

**Example**:
```
In 1990, food accounted for the largest share of household spending at
35%, followed by housing at 25%. By 2020, however, housing had become
the dominant category, rising to 32%, while food expenditure fell to
just 20%. Together, these two categories represented 60% of total
spending in 1990 but only 52% in 2020.
```

### Paragraph 4: Body Paragraph 2 | 第四段：主体段落2

Describe the remaining categories and any notable changes.
描述剩余类别和值得注意的变化。

**Example**:
```
Entertainment and transport showed the most dramatic increases. Spending
on entertainment rose from 5% in 1990 to 18% in 2020, while transport
costs grew from 10% to 15%. In contrast, savings declined from 15% to
just 8%, and the proportion allocated to other expenses remained relatively
stable at around 7-8%.
```

## Describing Changes Between Two Pie Charts | 描述两个饼图之间的变化

### Key Phrases for Change | 变化的关键短语

| Direction | Phrase | Example |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Increase | rose from X% to Y% | The figure rose from 15% to 25%. |
| Increase | grew from X% to Y% | The proportion grew from 10% to 20%. |
| Increase | increased by X% | The share increased by 15 percentage points. |
| Increase | doubled/tripled | The percentage doubled from 10% to 20%. |
| Decrease | fell from X% to Y% | The figure fell from 30% to 15%. |
| Decrease | declined from X% to Y% | The proportion declined from 25% to 12%. |
| Decrease | decreased by X% | The share decreased by 13 percentage points. |
| Decrease | halved | The figure halved from 40% to 20%. |
| Stable | remained the same at X% | The figure remained the same at 8%. |
| Stable | stayed at X% | The proportion stayed at around 12%. |
| Stable | was unchanged | The percentage was unchanged at 5%. |

### Important Note: Percentage vs Percentage Points | 重要说明：百分比 vs 百分点

| Expression | Meaning | Example |
|------------|---------|---------|
| increased by 10% | 10% of the original value | If it was 20%, it became 22% (20% × 1.10) |
| increased by 10 percentage points | 10 points added | If it was 20%, it became 30% |

**In IELTS, use "percentage points" for clarity**:
```
The share of food spending fell by 15 percentage points, from 35% to 20%.
```

## Key Sentence Structures | 关键句型

### Structure 1: Account for / Represent / Make up
```
[Category] accounted for / represented / made up [X%] of the total.

Example: Food expenditure accounted for 35% of total household spending.
```

### Structure 2: The Largest/Smallest Share
```
The largest/smallest share was [Category], at [X%].

Example: The largest share was allocated to housing, at 32%.
```

### Structure 3: Compared with / In Comparison
```
Compared with [Year 1], [Year 2] saw a [higher/lower] proportion of...

Example: Compared with 1990, 2020 saw a higher proportion of income
spent on entertainment.
```

### Structure 4: While X Increased, Y Decreased
```
While the proportion of [Category A] [increased/rose] from [X%] to [Y%],
[Category B] [decreased/fell] from [X%] to [Y%].

Example: While the proportion of online shopping rose from 5% to 25%,
in-store purchases fell from 90% to 65%.
```

### Structure 5: Together / Combined
```
Together, [Category A] and [Category B] accounted for [X%] of the total.

Example: Together, food and housing represented 55% of household expenditure.
```

### Structure 6: Remaining / Other
```
The remaining [X%] was divided among [categories].

The other categories collectively made up [X%] of the total.
```

## Full Sample Response | 完整范文

### Question | 题目

"The pie charts below show the main reasons why students chose a particular university in 2010 and 2020."

**Data summary**:
- 2010: Reputation (40%), Location (25%), Courses (20%), Cost (10%), Other (5%)
- 2020: Reputation (25%), Location (20%), Courses (30%), Cost (15%), Other (10%)

### Sample Answer | 范文

```
The two pie charts compare the main factors influencing students' choice
of university in 2010 and 2020.

Overall, it is evident that while university reputation was the most
important factor in 2010, it had been overtaken by course offerings by
2020. The proportion of students choosing based on cost and other factors
also increased over the decade.

In 2010, reputation was the dominant consideration, accounting for 40%
of all responses. Location was the second most common factor at 25%,
followed by courses at 20%. Cost and other reasons together made up
just 15% of the total.

By 2020, however, the picture had changed significantly. Course offerings
had become the leading factor, rising to 30%, while reputation fell from
40% to 25%. Location also declined slightly to 20%. Notably, the proportion
of students concerned about cost increased from 10% to 15%, and other
factors doubled from 5% to 10%. This suggests that students had become
more focused on practical considerations rather than institutional prestige.
```

**Word count**: 175 words

## Comparing Multiple Pie Charts | 比较多个饼图

### Strategy | 策略

When comparing 2 or more pie charts:
比较2个或更多饼图时：

1. **Start with the biggest change**: What category changed the most?
2. **Look for patterns**: Did all categories shift in the same direction?
3. **Compare totals**: Did the overall structure change?
4. **Note what stayed the same**: Were any categories stable?

1. **从最大的变化开始**：哪个类别变化最大？
2. **寻找模式**：所有类别的变化方向是否相同？
3. **比较总数**：整体结构是否改变？
4. **注意不变的部分**：哪些类别是稳定的？

**Example comparing two time periods**:
```
The two pie charts show how the energy mix in Country X changed between
2000 and 2020. The most dramatic shift was the decline of coal, which
fell from 45% to just 15%, while renewable energy sources grew from 5%
to 30%. Natural gas remained relatively stable, accounting for
approximately 25% in both years.
```

## Common Mistakes with Pie Charts | 饼图常见错误

### 1. Confusing Percentages and Actual Numbers ❌

**Wrong**: The number of people spending on food was 35%.
**Right**: The proportion/percentage of spending on food was 35%.

Pie charts show proportions, not absolute numbers (unless specifically stated).
饼图显示的是比例，而不是绝对数字（除非特别说明）。

### 2. Not Making Comparisons ❌

**Wrong**: Food was 35%. Housing was 25%. Transport was 15%.
**Right**: Food was the largest category at 35%, followed by housing at 25%. Transport accounted for a smaller share at 15%.

### 3. Missing the Overview ❌

Always include an overview paragraph that identifies the key pattern or change.
始终包含一个概述段落来识别关键模式或变化。

### 4. Using "Number" Instead of "Proportion" ❌

**Wrong**: The number of food spending was 35%.
**Right**: The proportion of food spending was 35%.

### 5. Confusing "Percentage" and "Percentage Points" ❌

**Wrong**: Food spending decreased by 15% (when it went from 35% to 20%).
**Right**: Food spending decreased by 15 percentage points (from 35% to 20%).

### Exercise 1: Describe Proportions | 练习1：描述比例

Write 3-4 sentences describing this data:
- World energy consumption by source 2020: Oil (31%), Coal (27%), Natural Gas (24%), Renewables (12%), Nuclear (6%)

```
In 2020, oil was the world's primary energy source, accounting for just
under a third of total consumption at 31%. Coal was the second largest
source at 27%, followed closely by natural gas at 24%. Together, these
three fossil fuels comprised over 80% of global energy consumption.
Renewable energy sources made up 12%, while nuclear power contributed
just 6% of the total.
```

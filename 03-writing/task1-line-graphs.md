# Task 1: Line Graphs | 任务1：折线图

> How to describe line graphs in IELTS Writing Task 1
> 如何在雅思写作任务1中描述折线图

---

## What are Line Graphs? | 什么是折线图？

Line graphs show **changes over time**. They are the most common chart type in IELTS Task 1.
折线图展示**随时间的变化**。它们是雅思任务1中最常见的图表类型。

### Key Features of Line Graphs | 折线图的关键特征

- **X-axis**: Usually time (years, months, days)
- **Y-axis**: The measured quantity (percentage, number, amount)
- **Lines**: Represent different categories or groups
- **Trends**: Upward, downward, stable, or fluctuating

- **X轴**：通常是时间（年、月、日）
- **Y轴**：测量的数量（百分比、数字、数量）
- **线条**：代表不同的类别或群体
- **趋势**：上升、下降、稳定或波动

## 4-Paragraph Structure | 四段结构

### Paragraph 1: Introduction | 第一段：引言

Paraphrase the question. State what the graph shows, where, and when.
改写题目。说明图表展示了什么、在哪里、什么时间。

**Templates**:
```
The line graph illustrates/compares/shows [what] in [where] between [when] and [when].

The line graph depicts changes in [what] over a period of [time span] starting from [year].

The line graph gives information about [what] in [where] from [start year] to [end year].
```

**Example**:
```
The line graph compares the amount of electricity generated from five
different renewable energy sources in the United States between 1980 and 2020.
```

### Paragraph 2: Overview | 第二段：概述

Identify 2-3 main trends WITHOUT specific numbers.
找出2-3个主要趋势，**不要**用具体数字。

**Templates**:
```
Overall, it is clear that [main trend 1]. While [category A] showed the
most significant [increase/decrease], [category B] remained relatively stable.
[Additional trend if applicable].
```

**Example**:
```
Overall, it is evident that electricity production from renewable sources
increased in all five categories over the 40-year period. Wind and solar
energy showed the most dramatic growth, while hydroelectric power remained
the dominant source throughout the period despite relatively modest increases.
```

### Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 1 | 第三段：主体段落1

Describe the first group of data or the first time period.
描述第一组数据或第一个时间段。

**Strategy**: Group lines that follow similar trends together.
**策略**：将趋势相似的线条分在一起。

**Example**:
```
Looking at the sources that showed the most dramatic growth, wind energy
production rose from virtually zero in 1980 to approximately 300 billion
kilowatt-hours by 2020. Similarly, solar energy, which also started from
a negligible level, climbed steadily after 2005 and reached around 100
billion kilowatt-hours by the end of the period.
```

### Paragraph 4: Body Paragraph 2 | 第四段：主体段落2

Describe the remaining data or compare different time periods.
描述剩余数据或比较不同时间段。

**Example**:
```
In contrast, hydroelectric power, which began as the leading source at
roughly 280 billion kilowatt-hours in 1980, experienced only a modest
increase to about 320 billion kilowatt-hours by 2020. Meanwhile, biomass
and geothermal energy contributed relatively small amounts throughout
the period, with biomass rising from 10 to 55 billion kilowatt-hours
and geothermal remaining under 20 billion kilowatt-hours.
```

## Describing Different Trend Types | 描述不同类型的趋势

### 1. Upward Trends | 上升趋势

| Level | Verb | Adjective + Noun | Adverb + Verb |
|-------|------|------------------|---------------|
| Large | soar, surge, rocket | a dramatic/sharp/significant increase | dramatically/significantly increased |
| Medium | rise, grow, climb | a steady/moderate/gradual rise | steadily/gradually rose |
| Small | increase, edge up | a slight/marginal/small increase | slightly/marginally increased |

**Example sentences**:
```
The number of tourists soared from 2 million to 8 million between 2010 and 2020.
There was a dramatic increase in online shopping during this period.
Sales rose steadily from 500 units in January to 700 units in June.
The figure for renewable energy climbed gradually over the two decades.
```

### 2. Downward Trends | 下降趋势

| Level | Verb | Adjective + Noun | Adverb + Verb |
|-------|------|------------------|---------------|
| Large | plunge, plummet, crash | a dramatic/sharp/dramatic fall | dramatically/plummeted |
| Medium | decline, decrease, drop | a steady/moderate decline | steadily declined |
| Small | fall, dip, edge down | a slight/marginal drop | slightly decreased |

**Example sentences**:
```
Unemployment rates plunged from 12% to just 3% over the decade.
There was a sharp decline in the birth rate between 1960 and 1980.
Sales dropped gradually throughout the year.
The figure dipped slightly in March before recovering in April.
```

### 3. Stability | 稳定

| Phrase | Example |
|--------|---------|
| remain stable/constant | The rate remained stable at around 5%. |
| stay the same | The figure stayed the same throughout the period. |
| level off | After rising sharply, the rate leveled off at 60%. |
| plateau | Production plateaued at 500 units per month. |
| hold steady | Exports held steady at approximately $2 billion. |

### 4. Fluctuation | 波动

| Phrase | Example |
|--------|---------|
| fluctuate | Prices fluctuated between $20 and $30. |
| vary | The rate varied considerably over the year. |
| go up and down | Sales went up and down throughout the period. |
| experience ups and downs | The market experienced significant ups and downs. |
| instability | There was considerable instability in the exchange rate. |

### 5. Peaks and Troughs | 峰值和谷值

| Phrase | Example |
|--------|---------|
| reach a peak of | Tourism reached a peak of 5 million in July. |
| peak at | Sales peaked at $8 million in 2015. |
| hit a high of | Unemployment hit a high of 15% in 2009. |
| reach a low of | Production reached a low of 200 units. |
| hit a trough of | Prices hit a trough of $10 in March. |
| bottom out at | The rate bottomed out at 2% before recovering. |

## Time Expressions for Line Graphs | 折线图的时间表达

### Starting Points | 起始点

```
In 1990, / At the beginning of the period, / In the initial year,
Starting at 20%, / Beginning from 500 units, / From the outset,
At the start of the period, / Initially,
```

### Changes Over Time | 随时间的变化

```
Over the next ten years, / During the following decade,
From 1990 to 2000, / Between 1990 and 2000,
Over the period shown, / Throughout the timeframe,
In the first half of the period, / In the latter half,
```

### Ending Points | 结束点

```
By 2020, / At the end of the period, / By the end of the timeframe,
In the final year, / In 2020, the last year shown,
By the year 2020, / At the close of the period,
```

## Describing Multiple Lines | 描述多条线

### Strategy for 3+ Lines | 3条以上线的策略

1. **Group similar trends**: Lines going up together, lines going down together
2. **Start with the most dramatic**: Begin with the most notable change
3. **Compare and contrast**: Use linking words to connect descriptions
4. **Don't describe every point**: Focus on key features

1. **将趋势相似的分在一起**：上升的线放一起，下降的线放一起
2. **从最显著的开始**：从最明显的变化开始描述
3. **比较和对比**：使用连接词连接描述
4. **不要描述每个点**：关注关键特征

### Grouping Examples | 分组示例

**If you have 5 lines**:
- Paragraph 3: Describe the 2 lines that increased the most
- Paragraph 4: Describe the remaining 3 lines

**如果有5条线**：
- 第三段：描述增长最大的2条线
- 第四段：描述剩余的3条线

## Full Sample Response | 完整范文

### Question | 题目

"The line graph below shows the number of tourists visiting three different Caribbean islands between 2010 and 2020."

**Data summary**:
- Jamaica: Started at 1.5M, rose to 3.5M
- Cuba: Started at 1M, rose to 2.5M
- Barbados: Started at 0.5M, rose slowly to 0.8M

### Sample Answer | 范文

```
The line graph illustrates the number of tourists who visited three
Caribbean islands — Jamaica, Cuba, and Barbados — over an 11-year
period from 2010 to 2020.

Overall, it is evident that all three islands experienced an increase
in tourist arrivals over the period. Jamaica consistently attracted the
highest number of visitors, while Barbados had the lowest figures
throughout. Notably, the gap between Jamaica and the other two islands
widened over time.

Looking at the details, Jamaica started the period with approximately
1.5 million tourists in 2010 and saw a steady increase over the decade,
reaching around 3.5 million visitors by 2020. Cuba followed a similar
upward trajectory, beginning at 1 million and climbing to 2.5 million
by the end of the period. Both islands experienced particularly sharp
growth between 2015 and 2018.

In contrast, Barbados showed a much more modest increase. Starting at
roughly 500,000 visitors in 2010, the figure rose gradually to just
under 800,000 by 2020. Unlike Jamaica and Cuba, Barbados experienced
a brief dip in tourist numbers around 2017 before recovering slightly
in the final years of the period.
```

**Word count**: 198 words

## Common Mistakes with Line Graphs | 折线图常见错误

### 1. Describing Every Point ❌

**Wrong**: In 1990, it was 20%. In 1991, it was 22%. In 1992, it was 25%. In 1993, it was 23%.

**Right**: The figure rose from 20% in 1990 to a peak of 25% in 1992, before declining slightly to 23% the following year.

### 2. Missing the Overview ❌

Without an overview, you cannot score above Band 5 for Task Achievement.
没有概述，任务完成度不会超过5分。

### 3. Using "Line" Repeatedly ❌

**Wrong**: The blue line shows... The red line shows... The green line shows...

**Right**: Use "the figure for [category]", "the rate for [category]", "the number of [category]"

### 4. Forgetting Units ❌

**Wrong**: It increased from 50 to 80.
**Right**: It increased from 50 million to 80 million.

Always mention what the numbers represent (millions, percentages, etc.).
始终提及数字代表什么（百万、百分比等）。

### 5. Confusing "Amount" and "Number" ❌

- **Number** + countable nouns: the number of tourists, the number of cars
- **Amount** + uncountable nouns: the amount of electricity, the amount of water

The percentage of smartphone users in Country X rose significantly over
the six-year period. Starting at 40% in 2015, the figure climbed steadily
to reach 88% by 2021. The most rapid increase occurred between 2017 and
2019, when usage jumped by 17 percentage points.

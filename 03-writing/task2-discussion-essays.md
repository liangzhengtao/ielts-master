# Task 2: Discussion Essays | 任务2：讨论类文章

> How to write discussion essays in IELTS Writing Task 2
> 如何在雅思写作任务2中写讨论类文章

---

## What is a Discussion Essay? | 什么是讨论类文章？

A discussion essay presents **two opposing views** on a topic. You must discuss **both sides** and give **your own opinion**.
讨论类文章就一个话题呈现**两种对立的观点**。你必须讨论**双方**并给出**你自己的观点**。

### Common Question Types | 常见题目类型

```
"Discuss both views and give your own opinion."

"Some people think A, while others think B. Discuss both views."

"What are the advantages and disadvantages of both positions?"

"Discuss both sides of this argument and give your opinion."
```

### Key Distinction: Discussion vs Opinion | 关键区别：讨论 vs 观点

| Discussion Essay | Opinion Essay |
|------------------|---------------|
| MUST discuss both views | Focus on YOUR view |
| Both sides get equal treatment | Your side gets more space |
| Your opinion comes after both views | Your opinion is stated from the beginning |

| 讨论类文章 | 观点类文章 |
|------------|------------|
| 必须讨论双方观点 | 侧重于你的观点 |
| 两方得到平等对待 | 你的立场获得更多篇幅 |
| 你的观点在两方之后出现 | 你的观点从一开始就陈述 |

## Essay Structure | 文章结构

### 5-Paragraph Structure (Recommended) | 五段结构（推荐）

```
Paragraph 1: Introduction
  - Paraphrase the topic
  - Mention both sides
  - State that you will discuss both and give your opinion

Paragraph 2: Body Paragraph 1 — View 1 (the view you DISAGREE with)
  - Explain the first view
  - Give reasons and examples
  - (This view should be shorter than your preferred view)

Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 2 — View 2 (the view you AGREE with)
  - Explain the second view
  - Give reasons and examples
  - (This should be more detailed and convincing)

Paragraph 4: Body Paragraph 3 — Your Opinion
  - State your position clearly
  - Give your main reasons
  - Support with examples

Paragraph 5: Conclusion
  - Summarize both views
  - Restate your opinion
```

### 4-Paragraph Structure (Alternative) | 四段结构（替代方案）

```
Paragraph 1: Introduction (paraphrase + outline)
Paragraph 2: View 1 + View 2 (discuss both in one paragraph)
Paragraph 3: Your Opinion (detailed with examples)
Paragraph 4: Conclusion (summarize + restate)
```

## Template: 5-Paragraph Discussion Essay | 模板：五段讨论类文章

### Paragraph 1: Introduction | 第一段：引言

```
[Paraphrase of the topic]. While some people argue that [View 1],
others believe that [View 2]. This essay will discuss both perspectives
before presenting my own opinion.

OR

[Paraphrase of the topic]. There are valid arguments on both sides of
this issue. This essay will examine both viewpoints and explain why
I believe that [your position].
```

**Example**:
```
The role of technology in education has become a subject of intense
debate. While some people argue that technology enhances the learning
experience, others believe that it is a distraction that undermines
traditional teaching methods. This essay will discuss both perspectives
before presenting my own opinion.
```

### Paragraph 2: View 1 (The View You DISAGREE With) | 第二段：观点1（你不同意的观点）

```
On the one hand, those who support [View 1] argue that [main reason].
[Explanation]. For example, [specific example]. Furthermore, [additional
reason]. This is a valid point because [explanation].

OR

Proponents of [View 1] contend that [main reason]. They argue that
[explanation]. For instance, [example]. This view is understandable
given that [reason].
```

**Example**:
```
On the one hand, those who oppose technology in education argue that it
is a significant distraction. They contend that devices such as laptops
and smartphones divert students' attention away from the lesson, leading
to reduced engagement and poorer academic performance. For example, a
study by the London School of Economics found that students who were
banned from using smartphones in class showed a 6% improvement in test
scores. This view is understandable, given the prevalence of social media
and gaming applications that compete for students' attention.
```

### Paragraph 3: View 2 (The View You AGREE With) | 第三段：观点2（你同意的观点）

```
On the other hand, supporters of [View 2] believe that [main reason].
[Explanation]. For example, [specific example]. Moreover, [additional
reason]. This argument is particularly compelling because [explanation].

OR

However, those in favour of [View 2] maintain that [main reason]. They
point out that [explanation]. A clear example of this is [example].
This is a strong argument because [reason].
```

**Example**:
```
On the other hand, supporters of educational technology argue that it
significantly enhances the learning experience. They maintain that
digital tools provide access to a vast range of resources, interactive
content, and personalized learning opportunities that traditional methods
cannot match. For instance, platforms like Khan Academy and Coursera
have enabled millions of students worldwide to access high-quality
educational content for free. Moreover, technology allows teachers to
adapt their instruction to individual students' needs through data-driven
insights and adaptive learning software.
```

### Paragraph 4: Your Opinion | 第四段：你的观点

```
In my opinion, [state your position]. I believe this because [reason 1]
and [reason 2]. [Explanation and example]. Therefore, [conclusion].

OR

Having considered both sides, I believe that [your position]. While I
acknowledge that [concession], I am convinced that [your main argument]
because [reason]. For example, [example].
```

**Example**:
```
Having considered both sides, I believe that technology, when used
appropriately, is a valuable tool that enhances rather than detracts
from education. While I acknowledge the potential for distraction, this
can be managed through proper classroom management and digital literacy
education. The benefits of access to information, personalized learning,
and global connectivity far outweigh the risks. For example, during the
COVID-19 pandemic, technology enabled millions of students to continue
their education remotely, demonstrating its essential role in modern
education.
```

### Paragraph 5: Conclusion | 第五段：结论

```
In conclusion, both sides of this debate present valid arguments. While
[opponents' view] has some merit, I believe that [your view] because
[main reason]. Ultimately, [final thought or recommendation].

OR

In conclusion, this is a complex issue with reasonable arguments on both
sides. However, I firmly believe that [your position], and I would
recommend that [suggestion or prediction].
```

**Example**:
```
In conclusion, the debate about technology in education involves legitimate
concerns on both sides. However, I firmly believe that the educational
benefits of technology far outweigh its potential drawbacks, provided
that its use is guided by thoughtful policies and effective teaching
strategies. The key is not to eliminate technology from the classroom,
but to harness its potential responsibly and effectively.
```

## Sample Discussion Essay | 讨论类文章范文

### Question | 题目

"Some people think that the government should invest more money in teaching science subjects in schools. Others believe that the government should spend more on arts and humanities. Discuss both views and give your own opinion."

### Full Sample Answer | 完整范文

```
The allocation of government funding for education is a topic that
generates considerable debate. While some people believe that science
subjects should receive greater investment, others argue that arts and
humanities deserve more support. This essay will discuss both perspectives
before presenting my own view.

On the one hand, advocates for increased science funding argue that
scientific education is essential for economic growth and technological
innovation. They contend that countries that invest heavily in science
education produce more engineers, researchers, and innovators who drive
economic development. For example, South Korea's significant investment
in science and technology education has been credited as a major factor
in its transformation from a developing to a developed economy within
a few decades. Furthermore, in an increasingly technology-driven world,
scientific literacy is becoming a fundamental requirement for many jobs.

On the other hand, supporters of arts and humanities education maintain
that these subjects are equally important for a well-rounded society.
They argue that arts education fosters creativity, critical thinking,
and emotional intelligence — skills that are increasingly valued in the
modern workplace. For instance, many successful technology companies,
including Apple, actively seek employees with backgrounds in design and
the humanities, recognizing that technical skills alone are insufficient
for innovation. Moreover, arts and humanities play a crucial role in
preserving cultural heritage and promoting social cohesion.

In my opinion, both science and arts education deserve adequate funding,
and governments should not prioritize one at the expense of the other.
I believe that the most effective education systems integrate both
disciplines, recognizing that they complement each other. For example,
subjects like architecture, medical illustration, and environmental
design require both scientific knowledge and artistic skill. A balanced
curriculum that values both areas produces well-rounded graduates who
can contribute to society in diverse ways.

In conclusion, while the arguments for increased funding in both science
and arts education have merit, I believe that a balanced approach is the
most beneficial. Governments should aim to provide adequate resources for
all subjects, ensuring that students receive a comprehensive education
that prepares them for the complexities of the modern world.
```

**Word count**: 310 words

## Key Language for Discussion Essays | 讨论类文章关键语言

### Introducing Views | 引入观点

| View 1 (First Side) | View 2 (Second Side) |
|---------------------|---------------------|
| On the one hand, | On the other hand, |
| Those who support [X] argue that... | However, those in favour of [Y] maintain... |
| Proponents of [X] contend that... | Conversely, advocates for [Y] believe... |
| Supporters of [X] claim that... | Conversely, opponents argue that... |
| It is often argued that... | However, others contend that... |
| One perspective is that... | An alternative view is that... |

### Giving Your Opinion | 给出你的观点

| Phrase | Example |
|--------|---------|
| In my opinion, | In my opinion, the latter view is more convincing. |
| I believe that | I believe that both sides have valid points. |
| I am convinced that | I am convinced that education is the key. |
| Having considered both sides, | Having considered both sides, I lean towards... |
| After weighing both arguments, | After weighing both arguments, I believe... |
| My view is that | My view is that a balanced approach is best. |
| I tend to agree with those who | I tend to agree with those who support... |

### Balanced Language | 平衡语言

| Phrase | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Both sides have valid points. | Both sides have valid points in this debate. |
| There are merits to both arguments. | There are merits to both arguments. |
| This is a complex issue. | This is a complex issue with no easy answer. |
| A balanced approach is needed. | A balanced approach is needed to address this. |
| Neither view is entirely correct. | Neither view is entirely correct on its own. |

## Common Mistakes in Discussion Essays | 讨论类文章常见错误

### 1. Only Discussing One Side ❌

**Wrong**: Only writing about why you agree.
**Right**: Discuss BOTH views before giving your opinion.

### 2. Not Giving Your Opinion ❌

**Wrong**: "Both views have merits." (without stating YOUR view)
**Right**: "While both views have merits, I believe that..."

You MUST give your opinion in a discussion essay.
你必须在讨论类文章中给出你的观点。

### 3. Discussing Both Sides Equally When You Have a Clear Position ❌

If you clearly favor one side, make that side's paragraph stronger and more detailed.
如果你明显偏向一方，让那一方的段落更强有力、更详细。

### 4. Being Too Balanced ❌

**Wrong**: "Both views are equally valid." (sounds indecisive)
**Right**: "While View A has some merit, I believe View B is more convincing because..."

### 5. Not Using Examples ❌

Both sides need examples, not just your preferred side.
双方都需要例子，而不仅仅是你偏好的一方。

## Summary | 总结

| Key Point | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Structure | Intro → View 1 → View 2 → Your Opinion → Conclusion |
| Balance | Discuss both sides, but give YOUR opinion |
| View 1 | The side you disagree with (shorter paragraph) |
| View 2 | The side you agree with (longer, more detailed) |
| Your opinion | Clear and supported with reasons and examples |

| 关键点 | 描述 |
|--------|------|
| 结构 | 引言→观点1→观点2→你的观点→结论 |
| 平衡 | 讨论双方，但给出你的观点 |
| 观点1 | 你不同意的一方（较短段落） |
| 观点2 | 你同意的一方（较长、更详细） |
| 你的观点 | 明确且有理由和例子支持 |

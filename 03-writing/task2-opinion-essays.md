# Task 2: Opinion Essays | 任务2：观点类文章

> How to write agree/disagree essays in IELTS Writing Task 2
> 如何在雅思写作任务2中写同意/不同意类文章

---

## What is an Opinion Essay? | 什么是观点类文章？

An opinion essay asks you to state **how much you agree or disagree** with a given statement. You must take a clear position and support it with reasons and examples.
观点类文章要求你说明你对给定陈述**在多大程度上同意或不同意**。你必须采取明确的立场，并用理由和例子来支持它。

### Common Question Types | 常见题目类型

```
"To what extent do you agree or disagree?"

"Do you agree or disagree?"

"Some people believe that... Do you agree?"

"To what extent do you agree that...?"
```

---

## Choosing Your Position | 选择你的立场

### Option 1: Strongly Agree/Disagree | 选项1：完全同意/不同意

**Structure**: All body paragraphs support one side.
**结构**：所有主体段落支持一方。

```
Introduction: I completely agree/disagree because...
Body 1: Reason 1 (supporting your position)
Body 2: Reason 2 (supporting your position)
Body 3: Reason 3 (supporting your position)
Conclusion: Restate strong agreement/disagreement
```

### Option 2: Partially Agree/Disagree (Recommended for Band 7+) | 选项2：部分同意/不同意（推荐用于7分以上）

**Structure**: Acknowledge one side, then argue for the other.
**结构**：承认一方，然后为另一方辩论。

```
Introduction: While there are some reasons to agree, I largely disagree/agree because...
Body 1: Concession (acknowledge the other side briefly)
Body 2: Main argument 1 (your position)
Body 3: Main argument 2 (your position)
Conclusion: Restate your balanced position
```

### Option 3: Balanced View | 选项3：平衡观点

**Structure**: Both sides are equally valid.
**结构**：两方同样有效。

```
Introduction: This issue has valid points on both sides.
Body 1: Arguments for agreement
Body 2: Arguments for disagreement
Body 3: Your balanced conclusion
Conclusion: Both sides have merit, but...
```

## Essay Structure: Strong Agree/Disagree | 文章结构：完全同意/不同意

### Template | 模板

```
Paragraph 1: Introduction
  - Paraphrase the question
  - State your clear position (I strongly agree/disagree)

Paragraph 2: Body Paragraph 1 — Strongest argument
  - Topic sentence (main point)
  - Explanation
  - Example
  - Linking sentence

Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 2 — Second argument
  - Topic sentence
  - Explanation
  - Example
  - Linking sentence

Paragraph 4: Body Paragraph 3 — Third argument (or counter-argument + rebuttal)
  - Topic sentence
  - Explanation
  - Example
  - Linking sentence

Paragraph 5: Conclusion
  - Summarize main points
  - Restate your position
```

### Sample: Strong Agree | 范文：完全同意

**Question**: "Some people think that the best way to reduce crime is to give longer prison sentences. To what extent do you agree or disagree?"

```
Many people believe that extending prison sentences is the most effective
approach to reducing crime rates. I strongly disagree with this view, as
I believe that longer sentences are neither the most effective nor the
most cost-efficient way to tackle criminal behaviour.

Firstly, longer prison sentences fail to address the root causes of crime.
Most criminal behaviour stems from social issues such as poverty, lack
of education, and limited employment opportunities. For example, in the
United States, which has some of the longest prison sentences in the
developed world, recidivism rates remain extremely high, with over 75%
of released prisoners reoffending within five years. This suggests that
simply keeping offenders in prison longer does not prevent them from
committing crimes again upon release.

Secondly, investing in rehabilitation programmes has proven to be far
more effective than extended incarceration. Countries like Norway, which
focus on education, vocational training, and psychological support for
prisoners, have recidivism rates of just 20%. By helping prisoners develop
skills and address the underlying issues that led to their criminal
behaviour, these programmes significantly reduce the likelihood of
reoffending.

Furthermore, longer prison sentences place an enormous financial burden
on taxpayers. The cost of housing a prisoner in the United States averages
$35,000 per year. These resources could be better spent on crime
prevention initiatives, such as community policing, youth outreach
programmes, and social services that address the conditions that give
rise to criminal activity in the first place.

In conclusion, while longer prison sentences may seem like a straightforward
solution to crime, they are neither effective nor economical. Instead,
governments should focus on addressing the root causes of crime through
social investment and rehabilitation programmes, which have been proven
to produce better outcomes for both offenders and society as a whole.
```

**Word count**: 267 words

## Essay Structure: Partially Agree/Disagree | 文章结构：部分同意/不同意

### Template | 模板

```
Paragraph 1: Introduction
  - Paraphrase the question
  - Acknowledge both sides
  - State your position (I largely agree/disagree, but acknowledge the other side)

Paragraph 2: Body Paragraph 1 — Concession (the other side)
  - Acknowledge why some people hold the opposing view
  - Give a reason and example
  - BUT transition to your view

Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 2 — Your main argument 1
  - Strong topic sentence
  - Explanation
  - Example
  - Linking sentence

Paragraph 4: Body Paragraph 3 — Your main argument 2
  - Strong topic sentence
  - Explanation
  - Example
  - Linking sentence

Paragraph 5: Conclusion
  - Summarize
  - Restate your balanced position
```

### Sample: Partially Agree | 范文：部分同意

**Question**: "Some people think that social media has a negative impact on society. To what extent do you agree or disagree?"

```
Social media has become an integral part of modern life, and there is
considerable debate about its impact on society. While I acknowledge
that social media offers some significant benefits, I largely agree
that its negative effects outweigh the positives.

Admittedly, social media has brought some notable advantages to society.
It enables people to stay connected with friends and family across the
globe, and it has become a powerful tool for raising awareness about
social and political issues. For instance, movements such as #MeToo and
#BlackLivesMatter gained global momentum largely through social media
platforms, leading to meaningful social change.

However, the detrimental effects of social media on mental health,
particularly among young people, cannot be ignored. Numerous studies
have linked excessive social media use to increased rates of anxiety,
depression, and low self-esteem. Young people are constantly exposed
to unrealistic standards of beauty, success, and happiness, which can
lead to feelings of inadequacy and social comparison. For example,
research by the Royal Society for Public Health in the UK found that
Instagram was the worst social media platform for young people's mental
wellbeing.

Moreover, social media has contributed to the spread of misinformation
and the erosion of meaningful human interaction. The algorithms used by
platforms like Facebook and Twitter create "echo chambers" where users
are only exposed to views that reinforce their existing beliefs, leading
to increased social polarisation. Furthermore, the superficial nature
of online interactions often replaces deeper, more meaningful face-to-face
relationships.

In conclusion, while social media has facilitated global connectivity
and social awareness, its negative impact on mental health and social
cohesion means that its overall effect on society is more harmful than
beneficial. Greater regulation and digital literacy education are needed
to mitigate these negative consequences.
```

**Word count**: 261 words

## Key Language for Opinion Essays | 观点类文章关键语言

### Stating Your Position | 表明你的立场

| Strong Agreement | Partial Agreement |
|------------------|-------------------|
| I firmly believe that... | While I acknowledge..., I believe... |
| I strongly agree that... | I largely agree, although... |
| I am convinced that... | I partially agree because... |
| It is my firm belief that... | I agree to some extent, but... |
| I completely disagree with... | While there are some merits, I disagree... |

### Concession Language | 让步语言

| Phrase | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Admittedly, | Admittedly, there are some benefits to this approach. |
| It is true that, | It is true that technology has improved communication. |
| While/Although | While some may argue this, I believe... |
| Granted, | Granted, there are valid points on both sides. |
| I acknowledge that | I acknowledge that this view has some merit. |
| Despite this, | Despite this, the drawbacks are more significant. |
| Nevertheless, | Nevertheless, the overall impact is negative. |
| Notwithstanding, | Notwithstanding these advantages, I still believe... |

### Giving Examples | 给出例子

| Phrase | Usage |
|--------|-------|
| For example/instance, | For example, many European countries have adopted this policy. |
| A clear example of this is | A clear example of this is the success of Finland's education system. |
| To illustrate, | To illustrate, consider the impact of social media on elections. |
| This is demonstrated by | This is demonstrated by the rapid growth of renewable energy. |
| A case in point is | A case in point is the recent pandemic response. |
| Take...as an example | Take Japan as an example of effective public transport. |

### Drawing Conclusions | 得出结论

| Phrase | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Therefore, | Therefore, it is clear that this approach is ineffective. |
| This suggests that | This suggests that governments need to take stronger action. |
| It can be concluded that | It can be concluded that the benefits outweigh the drawbacks. |
| In light of this evidence, | In light of this evidence, policy changes are needed. |
| Based on these arguments, | Based on these arguments, I firmly believe... |

## Common Mistakes in Opinion Essays | 观点类文章常见错误

### 1. Not Taking a Clear Position ❌

**Wrong**: "There are arguments for and against..." (without stating your view)
**Right**: "While there are arguments on both sides, I believe that..."

Always state YOUR opinion clearly in the introduction.
始终在引言中清楚地陈述你的观点。

### 2. Changing Your Position ❌

**Wrong**: Agreeing in the introduction, but disagreeing in the conclusion.
**Right**: Maintain a consistent position throughout the essay.

### 3. Being Too Absolute ❌

**Wrong**: "Everyone knows that..." / "It is obvious that..."
**Right**: "Evidence suggests that..." / "It could be argued that..."

### 4. Not Supporting Your Arguments ❌

Every point must be explained and supported with examples.
每个论点都必须用例子来解释和支持。

### 5. Writing About Both Sides Equally When Asked "To What Extent" ❌

If you agree, focus 70-80% of your essay on why you agree.
如果你同意，将70-80%的篇幅集中在你为什么同意上。

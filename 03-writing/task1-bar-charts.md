# Task 1: Bar Charts | 任务1：柱状图

> How to describe bar charts in IELTS Writing Task 1
> 如何在雅思写作任务1中描述柱状图

---

## What are Bar Charts? | 什么是柱状图？

Bar charts use rectangular bars to show **comparisons between categories** or **changes over time**. They are the second most common chart type in IELTS Task 1.
柱状图使用矩形条来展示**类别之间的比较**或**随时间的变化**。它们是雅思任务1中第二常见的图表类型。

### Types of Bar Charts | 柱状图的类型

| Type | Description | Chinese |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Vertical bars** | Bars going up (most common) | 竖直柱状图 |
| **Horizontal bars** | Bars going sideways | 水平柱状图 |
| **Grouped bars** | Multiple bars per category (comparison) | 分组柱状图 |
| **Stacked bars** | Bars divided into segments | 堆叠柱状图 |

---

## Key Differences from Line Graphs | 与折线图的关键区别

| Line Graphs | Bar Charts |
|-------------|------------|
| Shows continuous change over time | Shows discrete comparisons or time points |
| Focus on trends | Focus on comparisons |
| Use "rose/fell steadily" | Use "was higher/lower than" |
| Describe direction of lines | Describe height/length of bars |

| 折线图 | 柱状图 |
|--------|--------|
| 展示随时间的连续变化 | 展示离散的比较或时间点 |
| 关注趋势 | 关注比较 |
| 使用"稳步上升/下降" | 使用"高于/低于" |
| 描述线条的方向 | 描述柱子的高度/长度 |

## 4-Paragraph Structure | 四段结构

### Paragraph 1: Introduction | 第一段：引言

**Templates**:
```
The bar chart illustrates/compares/shows [what] in [where] in [year/period].

The bar chart compares the [quantity] of [what] across [number] categories.

The bar chart gives information about [what] among [groups] between [year] and [year].
```

**Example**:
```
The bar chart compares the percentage of household income spent on food
and drink in five European countries in 2020.
```

### Paragraph 2: Overview | 第二段：概述

Focus on the most noticeable comparisons.
关注最明显的比较。

**Templates**:
```
Overall, it is clear that [highest category] had the highest [figure],
while [lowest category] had the lowest. [Additional comparison trend].

The most striking feature is that [comparison]. In contrast, [opposite comparison].
```

**Example**:
```
Overall, it is evident that Turkey had the highest proportion of income
spent on food and drink, while the UK had the lowest. There was a clear
east-west divide, with Eastern European countries generally spending a
higher percentage of their income on these items than Western European nations.
```

### Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 1 | 第三段：主体段落1

Describe the highest/lowest values and notable patterns.
描述最高/最低值和显著模式。

**Example**:
```
Looking at the countries with the highest expenditure, Turkey allocated
approximately 32% of household income to food and drink. This was followed
by Poland at 25% and Romania at 22%. In all three countries, spending on
food represented more than one-fifth of total household income.
```

### Paragraph 4: Body Paragraph 2 | 第四段：主体段落2

Describe the remaining categories and any notable comparisons.
描述剩余类别和值得注意的比较。

**Example**:
```
In contrast, Western European nations showed significantly lower figures.
The UK spent only 10% of household income on food and drink, while France
and Germany recorded figures of 13% and 14% respectively. The difference
between the highest (Turkey) and lowest (UK) spending was approximately
22 percentage points.
```

### Comparing Two Items | 比较两个项目

| Phrase | Example |
|--------|---------|
| higher than | The figure for Japan was higher than that of China. |
| lower than | Sales in March were lower than in February. |
| more than | Germany spent more than France on education. |
| less than | Italy consumed less than Spain. |
| compared to/with | Spending was high compared to other nations. |
| in comparison with | In comparison with 2010, 2020 figures were higher. |

## Key Sentence Structures | 关键句型

### Structure 1: Comparing Two Items
```
[Country A] had a [higher/lower] [figure/rate/percentage] than [Country B],
at [X%] compared to [Y%].

Example: Germany had a higher literacy rate than Brazil, at 99% compared to 93%.
```

### Structure 2: Superlative + Category
```
The [highest/lowest/largest] [figure/rate/amount] was recorded by [country/category],
at [X%].

Example: The highest unemployment rate was recorded by Spain, at 15.3%.
```

### Structure 3: While/Whereas Contrast
```
While [Category A] [verb] [X], [Category B] [verb] [Y].

Example: While Japan spent $500 per capita on healthcare, Brazil spent only $150.
```

### Structure 4: Not Only...But Also
```
[Country A] not only had the highest [figure] but also showed the greatest
[increase/change].

Example: China not only had the largest population but also showed the
fastest growth rate.
```

### Structure 5: With + Data
```
[Country/Category], with [X%], [was/had] the [highest/lowest].

Example: The United States, with 75%, had the highest car ownership rate.
```

## Describing Grouped Bar Charts | 描述分组柱状图

Grouped bar charts show **two or more data series** for each category. Focus on:
分组柱状图为每个类别显示**两个或更多数据系列**。关注：

### Strategy | 策略

1. **Compare within categories**: How do the bars in each group differ?
2. **Compare across categories**: How do the same-colored bars compare?
3. **Identify patterns**: Do some categories consistently show higher/lower values?

1. **在类别内比较**：每组中的柱子有何不同？
2. **跨类别比较**：相同颜色的柱子如何比较？
3. **识别模式**：某些类别是否一直显示较高/较低的值？

**Example**:
```
The grouped bar chart compares the percentage of men and women who
participated in three types of sport in Australia in 2020.

Overall, men had higher participation rates than women in all three sports,
with the gender gap being most pronounced in football.

In football, 45% of men participated compared to just 12% of women, making
it the sport with the largest gender disparity. Swimming showed a more
balanced picture, with 38% of women and 32% of men taking part. Tennis
had the smallest gap, with participation rates of 25% for men and 22%
for women respectively.
```

## Describing Stacked Bar Charts | 描述堆叠柱状图

Stacked bar charts show **parts of a whole** for each category. They combine features of bar charts and pie charts.
堆叠柱状图显示每个类别的**整体各部分**。它们结合了柱状图和饼图的特点。

### Strategy | 策略

1. **Focus on the total**: Which categories have the highest/lowest totals?
2. **Compare segments**: How do the individual components differ?
3. **Identify proportions**: Which segment dominates in each category?

1. **关注总数**：哪些类别的总数最高/最低？
2. **比较分段**：各组成部分有何不同？
3. **识别比例**：每个类别中哪个分段占主导？

## Full Sample Response | 完整范文

### Question | 题目

"The bar chart below shows the top five countries by number of international tourist arrivals in 2019 and 2022."

**Data summary**:
- France: 2019 (90M), 2022 (80M)
- Spain: 2019 (84M), 2022 (72M)
- USA: 2019 (80M), 2022 (65M)
- China: 2019 (66M), 2022 (30M)
- Italy: 2019 (65M), 2022 (50M)

### Sample Answer | 范文

```
The bar chart compares the number of international tourist arrivals in
five countries in 2019 and 2022.

Overall, it is clear that all five countries experienced a decline in
tourist numbers between 2019 and 2022. France consistently received the
most visitors in both years, while China saw the most dramatic drop
relative to its 2019 figures.

Looking at the countries with the highest arrivals, France led with
approximately 90 million tourists in 2019, falling to 80 million by 2022.
Spain followed a similar pattern, declining from 84 million to 72 million
over the same period. The United States also saw a notable decrease,
dropping from 80 million to 65 million visitors.

In contrast, China experienced the sharpest decline among all five
countries. Its tourist arrivals fell from 66 million in 2019 to just
30 million in 2022, representing a decrease of more than 50%. Italy
recorded a more moderate fall from 65 million to 50 million visitors.
The gap between France and the other four countries widened considerably
in 2022, largely due to the varying impacts of travel restrictions.
```

**Word count**: 178 words

## Common Mistakes with Bar Charts | 柱状图常见错误

### 1. Confusing "Highest" and "Most" ❌

**Wrong**: France had the most tourists. (vague)
**Right**: France had the highest number of tourists. (precise)

### 2. Missing Comparisons ❌

Don't just list data for each bar. Always compare bars to each other.
不要只是列出每个柱子的数据。始终将柱子相互比较。

**Wrong**: France had 90 million. Spain had 84 million. The USA had 80 million.
**Right**: France had the highest figure at 90 million, followed by Spain at 84 million and the USA at 80 million.

### 3. Forgetting Units ❌

Always specify what the numbers represent (millions, percentages, dollars per capita, etc.).
始终说明数字代表什么（百万、百分比、人均美元等）。

### 4. Not Grouping Data ❌

Organize your description logically rather than going bar by bar.
逻辑地组织描述，而不是逐个柱子描述。

- Internet penetration rate 2020: UK (95%), Germany (93%), France (90%), Brazil (75%), India (50%)

**Model answer**:
```
In 2020, the UK had the highest internet penetration rate at 95%, closely
followed by Germany at 93%. France was slightly lower at 90%. In contrast,
Brazil and India had significantly lower rates, at 75% and 50% respectively.
The gap between the European countries and the developing nations was
approximately 20 to 45 percentage points.
```

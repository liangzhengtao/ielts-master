# IELTS Writing Scoring Criteria | 雅思写作评分标准

> Detailed explanation of how IELTS Writing is assessed
> 雅思写作如何评估的详细说明

---

## Overview | 概述

IELTS Writing is assessed by trained examiners using **four criteria**, each contributing **25%** of the total writing score. Understanding these criteria is essential for achieving a high band score.
雅思写作由训练有素的考官使用**四个标准**进行评估，每个标准占总写作分数的**25%**。理解这些标准对于获得高分至关重要。

---

## The Four Criteria | 四个评分标准

### Criterion 1: Task Achievement (Task 1) / Task Response (Task 2) | 标准1：任务完成度（任务1）/ 任务回应（任务2）

This criterion assesses **how well you address the task requirements**.
这个标准评估你**如何满足任务要求**。

#### Band 9 | 9分
- Fully addresses all parts of the task
- Presents a fully developed position with relevant, extended, and well-supported ideas
- 完全回应任务的所有部分
- 提出一个充分发展的立场，包含相关、扩展和充分支持的观点

#### Band 7 | 7分
- Addresses all parts of the task
- Presents a clear position throughout the response
- Presents, extends, and supports main ideas, but there may be a tendency to over-generalize
- 回应任务的所有部分
- 在整个回答中保持清晰的立场
- 提出、扩展和支持主要观点，但可能有过度概括的倾向

#### Band 5 | 5分
- Addresses the task only partially
- The format may be inappropriate in places
- Expresses a position but the development is not always clear
- Only partially回应任务
- 格式可能在某些地方不合适
- 表达了立场，但发展不总是清晰的

#### Key Tips for Task Achievement/Response | 任务完成度/回应的关键技巧

| Task 1 | Task 2 |
|--------|--------|
| Write an overview paragraph | Answer ALL parts of the question |
| Describe key features, not every detail | Take a clear position |
| Don't give opinions | Support all points with examples |
| Write at least 150 words | Write at least 250 words |
| Cover all data types shown | Stay on topic throughout |

| 任务1 | 任务2 |
|-------|-------|
| 写一个概述段落 | 回答问题的所有部分 |
| 描述关键特征，不是每个细节 | 采取明确的立场 |
| 不要给出观点 | 用例子支持所有论点 |
| 写至少150词 | 写至少250词 |
| 涵盖所有显示的数据类型 | 全程紧扣主题 |

### Criterion 2: Coherence and Cohesion | 标准2：连贯与衔接

This criterion assesses **how well you organize and link your ideas**.
这个标准评估你**如何组织和连接你的想法**。

#### Band 9 | 9分
- Uses cohesion in such a way that it attracts no attention
- Skilfully manages paragraphing
- 使用衔接手段，但不引起注意
- 熟练地管理段落划分

#### Band 7 | 7分
- Logically organizes information and ideas; there is clear progression throughout
- Uses a range of cohesive devices appropriately although there may be some under/over-use
- Presents a clear central topic within each paragraph
- 逻辑地组织信息和想法；整个文章有清晰的推进
- 适当地使用各种衔接手段，尽管可能有些使用不足/过度
- 每个段落都有一个清晰的中心话题

#### Band 5 | 5分
- Presents information with some organization but there may be a lack of overall progression
- Makes inadequate, inaccurate, or over-use of cohesive devices
- May not always use referencing clearly or appropriately
- 以某种方式组织信息，但可能缺乏整体推进
- 使用不足、不准确或过度使用衔接手段
- 可能不总是清晰或适当地使用引用

#### Coherence: Logical Organization | 连贯：逻辑组织

| Principle | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **One idea per paragraph** | Each paragraph should have ONE central topic | Don't mix advantages and disadvantages in one paragraph |
| **Clear progression** | Ideas should build on each other | Problem → Cause → Solution → Result |
| **Topic sentences** | Start each paragraph with a clear topic sentence | "One of the main advantages is..." |
| **Logical order** | Arrange ideas in a logical sequence | Chronological, cause-effect, general-specific |

| 原则 | 描述 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| **每段一个观点** | 每个段落应有一个中心话题 | 不要把优势和劣势混在一段 |
| **清晰的推进** | 观点应相互构建 | 问题→原因→解决方案→结果 |
| **主题句** | 以清晰的主题句开始每个段落 | "其中一个主要优势是..." |
| **逻辑顺序** | 按逻辑顺序排列观点 | 时间顺序、因果关系、一般-具体 |

#### Cohesion: Linking Words | 补充：连接词

| Function | Words/Phrases |
|----------|---------------|
| **Addition** | furthermore, moreover, in addition, additionally, also |
| **Contrast** | however, nevertheless, on the other hand, in contrast, conversely |
| **Cause/Effect** | therefore, consequently, as a result, thus, hence |
| **Examples** | for example, for instance, such as, to illustrate |
| **Sequence** | firstly, secondly, finally, subsequently, following this |
| **Summary** | in conclusion, to sum up, overall, in summary |

| 功能 | 词/短语 |
|------|---------|
| **添加** | furthermore, moreover, in addition, additionally, also |
| **对比** | however, nevertheless, on the other hand, in contrast, conversely |
| **因果** | therefore, consequently, as a result, thus, hence |
| **举例** | for example, for instance, such as, to illustrate |
| **顺序** | firstly, secondly, finally, subsequently, following this |
| **总结** | in conclusion, to sum up, overall, in summary |

### Criterion 3: Lexical Resource | 标准3：词汇丰富度

This criterion assesses **the range and accuracy of your vocabulary**.
这个标准评估你**词汇的范围和准确性**。

#### Band 9 | 9分
- Uses a wide range of vocabulary with very natural and sophisticated control
- Rare minor errors occur only as slips
- 使用广泛的词汇，具有非常自然和复杂的控制
- 罕见的小错误仅作为笔误出现

#### Band 7 | 7分
- Uses a sufficient range of vocabulary to allow some flexibility and precision
- Uses less common lexical items with some awareness of style and collocation
- May produce occasional errors in word choice, spelling, and/or word formation
- 使用足够的词汇范围，允许一些灵活性和精确性
- 使用不太常见的词汇项目，对风格和搭配有一定意识
- 可能在选词、拼写和/或构词方面偶尔出错

#### Band 5 | 5分
- Uses a limited range of vocabulary, but this is minimally adequate for the task
- May make noticeable errors in spelling and/or word formation that may cause some difficulty for the reader
- 使用有限的词汇范围，但这对任务来说是最低限度足够的
- 可能在拼写和/或构词方面犯明显错误，可能给读者造成一些困难

#### Vocabulary Strategies by Band Score | 按分数段的词汇策略

| Band | Strategy | Example |
|------|----------|---------|
| **5-6** | Use basic topic vocabulary correctly | "Technology is important for education." |
| **7** | Use less common vocabulary and collocations | "Technology plays a pivotal role in enhancing educational outcomes." |
| **8-9** | Use sophisticated, natural vocabulary with precision | "Technological innovation has fundamentally transformed pedagogical approaches." |

| 分数段 | 策略 | 示例 |
|--------|------|------|
| **5-6** | 正确使用基本话题词汇 | "Technology is important for education." |
| **7** | 使用不太常见的词汇和搭配 | "Technology plays a pivotal role in enhancing educational outcomes." |
| **8-9** | 使用复杂、自然的词汇，精确表达 | "Technological innovation has fundamentally transformed pedagogical approaches." |

#### Common Collocations | 常见搭配

| Category | Collocations |
|----------|-------------|

| **Environment** | protect the environment, cause pollution, reduce emissions, conserve resources |
| **Economy** | boost the economy, create jobs, generate income, stimulate growth |
| **Society** | face challenges, raise awareness, bridge the gap, play a role |

| 类别 | 搭配 |
|------|------|
| **教育** | receive education, gain knowledge, acquire skills, pursue studies |
| **健康** | suffer from, recover from, improve health, pose a threat |
| **环境** | protect the environment, cause pollution, reduce emissions, conserve resources |
| **经济** | boost the economy, create jobs, generate income, stimulate growth |
| **社会** | face challenges, raise awareness, bridge the gap, play a role |

### Criterion 4: Grammatical Range and Accuracy | 标准4：语法多样性与准确性

This criterion assesses **the variety and correctness of your grammar**.
这个标准评估你**语法的多样性和正确性**。

#### Band 9 | 9分
- Uses a wide range of structures with full flexibility and accuracy
- Rare minor errors occur only as slips
- 使用广泛的句式，具有完全的灵活性和准确性
- 罕见的小错误仅作为笔误出现

#### Band 7 | 7分
- Uses a variety of complex structures
- Produces frequent error-free sentences
- Has good control of grammar and punctuation but may make a few errors
- 使用各种复杂句式
- 产生频繁的无错误句子
- 对语法和标点有良好的控制，但可能犯一些错误

#### Band 5 | 5分
- Uses only a limited range of structures
- Attempts complex sentences but these tend to be less accurate than simple sentences
- May make frequent grammatical errors and punctuation may be faulty
- 只使用有限的句式范围
- 尝试使用复杂句，但这些往往不如简单句准确
- 可能犯频繁的语法错误，标点可能有问题

#### Grammar Structures for Band 7+ | 7分以上语法结构

| Structure | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| **Conditionals** | If governments invested more in education, the situation would improve. |
| **Passive voice** | New measures have been implemented to address this issue. |
| **Relative clauses** | The policy, which was introduced last year, has been effective. |
| **Participle clauses** | Having considered both sides, I believe that... |
| **Inversion** | Not only does this benefit individuals, but it also helps society. |
| **Cleft sentences** | It is education that plays the most crucial role. |
| **Subordinate clauses** | While I acknowledge the drawbacks, I believe the benefits outweigh them. |

| 结构 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| **条件句** | If governments invested more in education, the situation would improve. |
| **被动语态** | New measures have been implemented to address this issue. |
| **定语从句** | The policy, which was introduced last year, has been effective. |
| **分词从句** | Having considered both sides, I believe that... |
| **倒装句** | Not only does this benefit individuals, but it also helps society. |
| **强调句** | It is education that plays the most crucial role. |
| **从属从句** | While I acknowledge the drawbacks, I believe the benefits outweigh them. |

## How to Improve Each Criterion | 如何提高每个标准

### Improving Task Achievement/Response | 提高任务完成度/回应

1. **Read the question carefully** — Underline key words
2. **Plan your essay** — Spend 3-5 minutes planning
3. **Answer ALL parts** — Don't miss any part of the question
4. **Include an overview** (Task 1) — This is essential for Band 7+
5. **Stay on topic** — Every sentence should relate to the question

1. **仔细阅读题目**——划出关键词
2. **规划你的文章**——花3-5分钟做计划
3. **回答所有部分**——不要遗漏问题的任何部分
4. **包含概述**（任务1）——这对7分以上至关重要
5. **紧扣主题**——每句话都应该与题目相关

### Improving Coherence and Cohesion | 提高连贯与衔接

1. **Use clear paragraphing** — One idea per paragraph
2. **Start with topic sentences** — State the main idea first
3. **Use linking words naturally** — Don't overuse them
4. **Maintain clear progression** — Ideas should flow logically
5. **Use referencing** — "this", "these", "such", "it"

1. **使用清晰的段落划分**——每段一个观点
2. **以主题句开头**——先陈述主要观点
3. **自然地使用连接词**——不要过度使用
4. **保持清晰的推进**——观点应逻辑流畅
5. **使用指代**——"this", "these", "such", "it"

### Improving Lexical Resource | 提高词汇丰富度

1. **Learn topic vocabulary** — Study words for common IELTS topics
2. **Learn collocations** — Words that go together naturally
3. **Avoid repetition** — Use synonyms and paraphrasing
4. **Use less common vocabulary** — But only if you're confident
5. **Check spelling** — Poor spelling lowers your score

1. **学习话题词汇**——学习常见雅思话题的词汇
2. **学习搭配**——自然搭配在一起的词
3. **避免重复**——使用同义词和改写
4. **使用不太常见的词汇**——但只有在你有把握的情况下
5. **检查拼写**——拼写差会降低你的分数

### Improving Grammatical Range and Accuracy | 提高语法多样性与准确性

1. **Use a variety of structures** — Mix simple, compound, and complex sentences
2. **Practice complex structures** — Conditionals, passives, relative clauses
3. **Focus on accuracy first** — Then work on range
4. **Proofread for errors** — Check for common mistakes
5. **Learn from mistakes** — Keep an error log

1. **使用多种句式**——混合使用简单句、复合句和复杂句
2. **练习复杂结构**——条件句、被动语态、定语从句
3. **先关注准确性**——然后是多样性
4. **校对错误**——检查常见错误
5. **从错误中学习**——保持错误日志

## Band Score Calculation | 分数计算

| Criterion | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Task Achievement/Response | 25% |
| Coherence and Cohesion | 25% |
| Lexical Resource | 25% |
| Grammatical Range and Accuracy | 25% |

### Task 1 vs Task 2 Weighting | 任务1 vs 任务2权重

Task 2 carries **twice the weight** of Task 1 in your overall writing score.
任务2在你的写作总分中**权重是任务1的两倍**。

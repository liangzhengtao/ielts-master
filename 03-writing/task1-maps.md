# Task 1: Maps | 任务1：地图

> How to describe maps in IELTS Writing Task 1
> 如何在雅思写作任务1中描述地图

---

## What are Map Questions? | 什么是地图题？

Map questions show **changes to a place over time**. You are usually given two or three maps of the same location at different points in time and asked to describe the changes.
地图题展示**一个地方随时间的变化**。通常会给你同一地点在不同时间点的两到三张地图，要求你描述变化。

### Types of Map Questions | 地图题的类型

| Type | Description | Chinese |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Past → Present** | Shows changes that have already happened | 过去→现在（已发生的变化） |
| **Present → Future** | Shows planned or proposed changes | 现在→未来（计划或拟议的变化） |
| **Past → Present → Future** | Shows changes over three time periods | 过去→现在→未来 |

## Key Language for Maps | 地图题的关键语言

### Location Language | 位置语言

| Phrase | Example |
|--------|---------|
| in the north/south/east/west | The factory was located in the north. |
| in the northeast/southwest/etc. | The park is situated in the northeast corner. |
| to the north of | A lake was situated to the north of the village. |
| in the center/middle | The town center was in the middle of the map. |
| on the coast/coastline | The port was on the southern coastline. |
| along the river | Houses were built along the river. |
| adjacent to / next to / beside | A school was built adjacent to the hospital. |
| opposite / across from | The supermarket was opposite the bank. |
| between...and... | The park was located between the school and the river. |
| on the outskirts of | New suburbs developed on the outskirts. |
| in the vicinity of | A shopping center was built in the vicinity of the station. |

### Change Language | 变化语言

#### Buildings and Infrastructure | 建筑和基础设施

| Action | Phrase |
|--------|--------|
| **Built/New** | was built, was constructed, was established, was developed, was added |
| **Removed** | was removed, was demolished, was knocked down, was torn down |
| **Replaced** | was replaced by, was converted into, was transformed into |
| **Expanded** | was expanded, was extended, was enlarged, was widened |
| **Renovated** | was renovated, was refurbished, was modernized |

#### Land Use | 土地使用

| Action | Phrase |
|--------|--------|
| **Developed** | was developed into, was turned into, became |
| **Cleared** | was cleared to make way for, was cleared for |
| **Preserved** | was preserved, was protected, remained unchanged |
| **Residential** | became residential area, was zoned for housing |
| **Commercial** | was designated for commercial use |

### Describing the Amount of Change | 描述变化的程度

| Degree | Phrases |
|--------|---------|
| **Complete change** | completely transformed, entirely replaced, totally redesigned |
| **Major change** | significantly altered, substantially modified, considerably changed |
| **Minor change** | slightly modified, marginally altered, partially changed |
| **No change** | remained unchanged, was preserved, stayed the same, was retained |

## 4-Paragraph Structure | 四段结构

### Paragraph 1: Introduction | 第一段：引言

State what the maps show and the time periods.
说明地图展示了什么以及时间段。

**Templates**:
```
The maps illustrate the changes that [have taken place / are planned] in
[location] between [year] and [year].

The two maps compare [location] as it was in [year] with proposed changes
for [year].

The maps show [location] at two different points in time: [year] and [year].
```

**Example**:
```
The two maps illustrate the changes that have taken place in the town
of Riverside between 1990 and 2020.
```

### Paragraph 2: Overview | 第二段：概述

Summarize the most significant changes. Don't include specific details.
总结最显著的变化。不要包含具体细节。

**Templates**:
```
Overall, the most significant changes include [change 1] and [change 2].
The area has been [substantially/completely] transformed from [old state]
to [new state].

The most noticeable transformation is [change]. Additionally, [change 2]
and [change 3] are also evident.
```

**Example**:
```
Overall, the town has been significantly modernized and expanded over
the 30-year period. The most notable changes include the construction
of a new shopping center, the development of residential areas on the
outskirts, and the removal of the old factory. The town center has been
largely pedestrianized.
```

### Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 1 (Past/Earlier Map) | 第三段：主体段落1（过去/早期地图）

Describe what the location looked like in the earlier period.
描述该地点在早期的样子。

**Example**:
```
In 1990, the town center consisted of a small number of shops along a
main road, with a large factory situated to the east. A river ran through
the western side of the town, and there was a bridge connecting the two
parts. The area to the north was mainly farmland, while a few houses
were scattered along the southern road. The only green space was a small
park near the river.
```

### Paragraph 4: Body Paragraph 2 (Present/Future Map) | 第四段：主体段落2（现在/未来地图）

Describe the changes that have occurred or are planned.
描述已经发生或计划中的变化。

**Example**:
```
By 2020, the factory had been demolished and replaced by a modern shopping
center with a large car park. The main road through the town center was
closed to traffic and converted into a pedestrian zone. The farmland to
the north was developed into a residential suburb with a new school and
a sports complex. The bridge was widened to accommodate more traffic, and
the park was expanded to include a children's playground. The river path
was upgraded with a cycle lane and walking trail.
```

## Comparing Past and Present | 比较过去和现在

### Key Phrases | 关键短语

| Timeframe | Phrases |
|-----------|---------|
| **Past** | In [year], / At the earlier time point, / Previously, / Originally, |
| **Present** | By [year], / At the later time point, / Currently, / At present, |
| **Change** | has/have been [past participle], had been [past participle], |
| **Contrast** | whereas previously..., / while in the past..., / in contrast to... |

### Structure for Describing Changes | 描述变化的结构

**Pattern 1**: [Subject] + was/were + [past participle]
```
The factory was demolished.
New houses were constructed on the farmland.
```

**Pattern 2**: [Subject] + was/were + converted/transformed/replaced + into/by
```
The factory was replaced by a shopping center.
The farmland was converted into a residential area.
```

**Pattern 3**: [Subject] + was/were + [adverb] + [past participle]
```
The park was significantly expanded.
The road was slightly widened.
```

**Pattern 4**: There + was/were + [noun] + of
```
There was a significant increase in the number of buildings.
There was a noticeable reduction in green spaces.
```

## Full Sample Response | 完整范文

### Question | 题目

"The maps below show a coastal town called Seaside in 1950 and in the present day."

**1950 Map**:
- Small fishing village
- Fishing boats on beach
- A few houses along the main road
- Farmland to the north
- A lighthouse on the cliff
- No shops or facilities

**Present Map**:
- Large tourist resort
- Hotels along the beach
- Shopping center where farmland was
- Marina with yachts
- Lighthouse still there
- Restaurant and parking area
- New road network

### Sample Answer | 范文

```
The two maps illustrate the changes that have occurred in the coastal
town of Seaside between 1950 and the present day.

Overall, Seaside has been completely transformed from a small fishing
village into a major tourist resort. The most dramatic changes include
the construction of hotels along the beach, the development of a shopping
center, and the replacement of fishing boats with a modern marina.

In 1950, Seaside was a small, quiet fishing village. A handful of houses
lined a single main road that ran parallel to the coast. The beach was
used by local fishermen, with several small fishing boats moored along
the shore. To the north, the land was primarily used for agriculture,
with large fields of farmland. The only notable structure on the cliff
was a lighthouse, which guided ships safely to shore.

In stark contrast, the present-day map shows a bustling tourist destination.
The original houses have been replaced by large hotels that stretch along
the beachfront. The farmland to the north has been developed into a
shopping center with a car park. Where the fishing boats once stood, a
modern marina now accommodates numerous yachts and pleasure craft. A
restaurant has been built near the beach, and a new road network connects
the various facilities. Remarkably, the lighthouse has been preserved
and remains standing on the cliff, serving as a historical landmark
amid the modern development.
```

**Word count**: 222 words

## Describing Future/Planned Changes | 描述未来/计划中的变化

### Useful Phrases for Future Maps | 未来地图的有用短语

```
It is planned/proposed that...
There are plans to...
The authorities intend to...
According to the plan,...
The proposed development will include...
By [year], it is expected that...
The project aims to...
If the plans go ahead,...
```

**Example**:
```
According to the development plan, the old industrial area will be
demolished and replaced by a new residential complex. It is proposed
that a park will be created along the riverbank, and the road network
will be expanded to accommodate the increased traffic.
```

## Useful Phrases Bank | 有用短语库

### Introduction Phrases | 引言短语

- The maps illustrate the changes in...
- The maps compare... at two different points in time.
- The maps show how [location] has changed/will change.
- The two maps depict [location] as it was in [year] and as it is today.

### Location Phrases | 位置短语

- situated/located/positioned in/on/at/to the...
- in the northern/southern/eastern/western part of...
- on the left/right-hand side of the map
- at the top/bottom of the map
- in the foreground/background

### Change Phrases | 变化短语

- was/were [built/constructed/demolished/replaced/converted]
- has/have been [transformed/expanded/developed/modernized]
- was converted/transformed into...
- was replaced by/with...
- gave way to...
- made way for...
- was renamed...

### Comparison Phrases | 比较短语

- In contrast to the earlier map,...
- Unlike in [year],...
- Whereas the [year] map showed..., the present map reveals...
- Compared with the original layout,...
- The most significant/dramatic change is...

## Common Mistakes with Maps | 地图题常见错误

### 1. Using Present Tense for Past Maps ❌

**Wrong**: The factory is in the north. (when describing a 1950 map)
**Right**: The factory was situated in the north.

Use past tense for past maps, present tense for current maps, and future forms for planned changes.
对过去的地图使用过去时，对现在的地图使用现在时，对计划中的变化使用将来时。

### 2. Missing the Overview ❌

Your overview should state the overall nature of the change (e.g., modernization, expansion, urbanization).
你的概述应该说明变化的总体性质（例如，现代化、扩展、城市化）。

### 3. Not Describing Locations Clearly ❌

**Wrong**: A new school was built.
**Right**: A new school was built to the north of the shopping center.

Always include location information when describing new features.
描述新特征时始终包含位置信息。

### 4. Ignoring What Stayed the Same ❌

Mention features that remained unchanged — this shows good observation.
提及保持不变的特征——这显示了良好的观察力。

**Example**:
```
The lighthouse, which was originally built in 1920, has been preserved
and remains in its original position on the cliff.
```

### 5. Being Too Detailed About Minor Changes ❌

Focus on major transformations, not minor details like a small path being slightly moved.
关注主要转变，而不是像小路稍微移动这样的小细节。

## Practice Exercise | 练习

### Exercise 1: Describe Changes | 练习1：描述变化

Write 3-4 sentences describing the changes between these two maps:

**1980**: A village with a few houses, a church, a river, farmland
**2020**: A town with many houses, a shopping center, a school, a sports center, the river has a bridge

**Model answer**:
```
Between 1980 and 2020, the small village was transformed into a
considerably larger town. The farmland was developed into a residential
area with numerous houses, and a shopping center was built to the east
of the church, which has been preserved. A new school and sports center
were constructed on the northern outskirts. Perhaps the most notable
infrastructure change is the addition of a bridge across the river,
connecting the two sides of the town.
```

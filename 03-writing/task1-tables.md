# Task 1: Tables | 任务1：表格

> How to describe tables in IELTS Writing Task 1
> 如何在雅思写作任务1中描述表格

---

## What are Tables? | 什么是表格？

Tables present data in **rows and columns**, showing exact figures. They can be more challenging to describe because the key trends are not as visually obvious as in graphs or charts.
表格以**行和列**的形式呈现数据，显示精确数字。它们可能更具挑战性，因为关键趋势不像图表那样直观明显。

### Types of Tables in IELTS | 雅思中表格的类型

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Comparison table** | Compares categories across countries/groups | 不同国家的消费数据 |
| **Time-series table** | Shows data over multiple time periods | 某国1990-2020年的人口数据 |
| **Mixed table** | Combines categories and time periods | 不同年龄段在不同年份的消费 |
| **Process table** | Shows stages or steps in a process | 生产流程各阶段的数据 |

---

## How to Read Tables Effectively | 如何有效阅读表格

### Step-by-Step Analysis | 分步分析

1. **Read the title**: What is being measured?
2. **Read column/row headers**: What are the categories and time periods?
3. **Identify the highest values**: What stands out?
4. **Identify the lowest values**: What is notably low?
5. **Look for patterns**: Are there trends across rows or columns?
6. **Find notable exceptions**: Any surprising data points?

1. **阅读标题**：测量的是什么？
2. **阅读列/行标题**：有哪些类别和时间段？
3. **识别最高值**：什么最突出？
4. **识别最低值**：什么明显较低？
5. **寻找模式**：行或列中是否有趋势？
6. **找出显著例外**：是否有令人惊讶的数据点？

## 4-Paragraph Structure | 四段结构

### Paragraph 1: Introduction | 第一段：引言

**Templates**:
```
The table compares [what] across [categories/countries] in [year/period].

The table illustrates/shows [what] for [number] [categories] between [year] and [year].

The table gives information about [what] in [where] from [start] to [end].
```

**Example**:
```
The table compares the average monthly household expenditure on six
categories of goods and services in four European countries in 2020.
```

### Paragraph 2: Overview | 第二段：概述

Identify the most significant patterns in the data.
找出数据中最显著的模式。

**Example**:
```
Overall, it is evident that housing and food were the two largest expense
categories across all four countries. The UK had the highest total
expenditure, while Portugal had the lowest. Notably, spending patterns
varied considerably between countries, particularly in housing and
entertainment.
```

### Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 1 | 第三段：主体段落1

Describe the most notable data points or the highest/lowest values.
描述最显著的数据点或最高/最低值。

**Example**:
```
Looking at the highest spending categories, housing costs dominated in
three of the four countries, ranging from €450 in Portugal to €1,200 in
the UK. However, in Italy, food was the largest expense at €680, slightly
higher than housing at €650. The UK stood out with exceptionally high
housing expenditure, which was nearly double that of the other countries.
```

### Paragraph 4: Body Paragraph 2 | 第四段：主体段落2

Describe the remaining data, patterns, or notable comparisons.
描述剩余数据、模式或值得注意的比较。

**Example**:
```
In the remaining categories, the UK again showed the highest figures in
entertainment (€320) and transport (€380). Portugal consistently recorded
the lowest expenditure across most categories, with particularly low
spending on entertainment at just €80. Germany showed relatively balanced
spending patterns, with no single category dominating as dramatically as
housing did in the UK.
```

## Strategies for Describing Tables | 描述表格的策略

### Strategy 1: Highest to Lowest | 策略1：从高到低

Organize your description by comparing the largest and smallest values.
通过比较大值和小值来组织描述。

**Example**:
```
The highest expenditure was recorded by the UK in housing (€1,200),
while the lowest was Portugal's spending on entertainment (€80).
```

### Strategy 2: Category by Category | 策略2：逐类别

Go through each category (row or column) and compare across groups.
逐个类别（行或列）进行组间比较。

**Example**:
```
In terms of food, Italy had the highest spending (€680), followed by
the UK (€550), Germany (€520), and Portugal (€350).
```

### Strategy 3: Country/Group by Country/Group | 策略3：逐国家/组

Describe each country or group's overall pattern.
描述每个国家或组的整体模式。

**Example**:
```
The UK showed the highest spending in four of the six categories,
with particularly large amounts on housing and transport. Portugal,
on the other hand, consistently recorded the lowest figures.
```

### Strategy 4: Time-Based Comparison | 策略4：基于时间的比较

If the table shows data over time, focus on changes between periods.
如果表格显示的是随时间变化的数据，关注各时期之间的变化。

**Example**:
```
Between 2010 and 2020, food expenditure in Germany rose from €400 to
€520, representing a 30% increase. In contrast, transport costs fell
slightly from €320 to €290.
```

## Key Language for Tables | 表格的关键语言

### Expressing Exact Figures | 表达精确数字

| Phrase | Example |
|--------|---------|
| at exactly/precisely | The figure was at exactly 50%. |
| standing at | Expenditure, standing at €1,200, was the highest. |
| with a figure of | Japan, with a figure of 45%, led the table. |
| reaching/totaling | Spending reached €850 in France. |
| amounting to | The total cost amounted to €3,500. |

### Expressing Approximate Figures | 表达近似数字

| Phrase | Example |
|--------|---------|
| approximately/roughly | The figure was approximately 500 units. |
| around/about | Spending was around €600 per month. |
| just over/under | The rate was just over 40%. |
| nearly/almost | Production reached nearly 800 tonnes. |
| in the region of | The cost was in the region of €500. |
| in excess of | Spending was in excess of €1,000. |

### Making Comparisons | 进行比较

| Phrase | Example |
|--------|---------|
| the highest figure | The UK had the highest figure at €1,200. |
| the lowest figure | Portugal recorded the lowest figure at €350. |
| compared to/with | Italy's food spending was high compared to other countries. |
| in contrast to/with | In contrast to Germany, France showed lower figures. |
| while/whereas | While the UK spent €1,200 on housing, Portugal spent €450. |
| X times as much as | UK housing costs were almost three times as much as Portugal's. |
| marginally/slightly | Germany was marginally higher than France. |
| significantly/substantially | The UK was significantly higher than all other countries. |

### Describing Differences | 描述差异

| Phrase | Example |
|--------|---------|
| a difference of | There was a difference of €750 between the UK and Portugal. |
| a gap of | The gap between the highest and lowest was 45 percentage points. |
| the difference between...and... | The difference between the UK and Germany was €380. |
| with a margin of | France led Italy with a margin of €120. |

## Common Challenges with Tables | 表格的常见挑战

### Challenge 1: Too Much Data | 挑战1：数据过多

**Solution**: Don't try to describe every cell. Focus on:
- The highest and lowest values
- Notable patterns or exceptions
- Key comparisons

**解决方案**：不要试图描述每个单元格。关注：
- 最高和最低值
- 显著模式或例外
- 关键比较

### Challenge 2: No Visual Trends | 挑战2：无视觉趋势

**Solution**: Mentally organize the data by:
- Sorting values from high to low
- Grouping similar values together
- Identifying the range (difference between highest and lowest)

**解决方案**：通过以下方式在脑中组织数据：
- 将值从高到低排序
- 将相似的值分组
- 识别范围（最高值和最低值之间的差异）

### Challenge 3: Multiple Categories | 挑战3：多个类别

**Solution**: Group categories logically:
- By similar values (high, medium, low)
- By trends (increasing, decreasing, stable)
- By geographical or logical groupings

**解决方案**：逻辑地分组类别：
- 按相似值（高、中、低）
- 按趋势（增加、减少、稳定）
- 按地理或逻辑分组

## Full Sample Response | 完整范文

### Question | 题目

"The table below shows the percentage of the population living in urban areas in different regions of the world in 1990, 2010, and 2030 (projected)."

| Region | 1990 | 2010 | 2030 |
|--------|------|------|------|
| North America | 75% | 82% | 87% |
| Europe | 70% | 73% | 78% |
| Latin America | 71% | 79% | 86% |
| Asia | 32% | 45% | 56% |
| Africa | 31% | 40% | 48% |

### Sample Answer | 范文

```
The table compares the percentage of people living in urban areas across
five world regions in 1990, 2010, and projected figures for 2030.

Overall, it is evident that urbanization increased in all regions over
the 40-year period. North America consistently had the highest proportion
of urban dwellers, while Africa had the lowest. The most significant
growth occurred in Asia and Africa, which saw the largest increases in
urban population percentages.

Looking at the regions with the highest urbanization rates, North America
led throughout the period, rising from 75% in 1990 to 82% in 2010, and
is projected to reach 87% by 2030. Latin America showed a similar pattern,
increasing from 71% to 79%, with projections suggesting it will reach 86%
by 2030. Europe, while starting at 70% in 1990, experienced the slowest
growth, rising to 73% in 2010 and expected to reach 78% by 2030.

In contrast, Asia and Africa began with significantly lower urbanization
rates. Asia's urban population rose from 32% in 1990 to 45% in 2010, and
is forecast to reach 56% by 2030. Africa showed the lowest figures
throughout, starting at 31% and reaching 40% by 2010, with projections
indicating 48% by 2030. Notably, Africa's projected growth rate of 17
percentage points over 40 years is the second highest after Asia's 24
percentage points.
```

## Common Mistakes with Tables | 表格常见错误

### 1. Describing Every Cell ❌

**Wrong**: The UK spent €680 on food, €1,200 on housing, €250 on clothing, €320 on entertainment, €380 on transport, and €200 on other items.

**Right**: The UK had the highest expenditure in most categories, with housing costs reaching €1,200 — the largest single expense across all countries.

### 2. Missing Comparisons ❌

Don't just list numbers. Always compare them.
不要只是列出数字。始终进行比较。

### 3. Confusing Rows and Columns ❌

Read the table carefully to understand what each row and column represents.
仔细阅读表格以了解每行和每列代表什么。

### 4. Not Identifying Patterns ❌

Look for patterns across rows (across categories) and columns (across time or groups).
寻找行（跨类别）和列（跨时间或组）中的模式。

## Practice Exercise | 练习

### Exercise 1: Describe a Table | 练习1：描述表格

Write 4-5 sentences describing this data:

| Country | Education (%) | Healthcare (%) | Defence (%) |
|---------|---------------|----------------|-------------|
| Country A | 18 | 22 | 15 |
| Country B | 12 | 18 | 25 |
| Country C | 25 | 15 | 8 |

**Model answer**:
```
The table compares government spending on education, healthcare, and
defence as a percentage of total expenditure in three countries. Overall,
Country C allocated the most to education, while Country B spent the most
on defence. Country A showed relatively balanced spending across all three
categories. The most notable difference was in defence spending, where
Country B's figure (25%) was more than three times that of Country C (8%).
Country C's education spending (25%) was the highest single figure in the
table, while its healthcare allocation (15%) was the lowest.
```

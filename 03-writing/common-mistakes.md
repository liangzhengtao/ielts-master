# Common IELTS Writing Mistakes | 雅思写作常见错误

> Avoid these mistakes to improve your IELTS Writing score
> 避免这些错误以提高你的雅思写作分数

---

## Overview | 概述

This guide covers the most common mistakes made by IELTS candidates in both Task 1 and Task 2. Understanding and avoiding these errors can significantly improve your band score.
本指南涵盖了雅思考生在任务1和任务2中最常犯的错误。理解和避免这些错误可以显著提高你的分数。

## Grammar Mistakes | 语法错误

### 1. Subject-Verb Agreement | 主谓一致

**Wrong**: The number of students are increasing.
**Right**: The number of students is increasing.

**Wrong**: Each of the countries have different policies.
**Right**: Each of the countries has different policies.

**Rule**: "The number of" takes a singular verb; "A number of" takes a plural verb.
**规则**："The number of"用单数动词；"A number of"用复数动词。

**Examples**:
- The number of cars **is** rising. ✅
- A number of students **are** absent. ✅

### 2. Article Errors | 冠词错误

**Wrong**: Government should invest in education.
**Right**: **The** government should invest in education.

**Wrong**: Education is the key to the success.
**Right**: Education is **the** key to success. (no "the" before success)

**Wrong**: The pollution is a serious problem.
**Right**: Pollution is a serious problem. (general concept = no article)

**Rules**:
- Use "the" for specific nouns: the government, the environment
- No article for general concepts: education, pollution, crime
- Use "a/an" for singular countable nouns: a problem, an issue

### 3. Run-On Sentences | 逗号拼接句

**Wrong**: Technology is advancing rapidly, it affects every aspect of life.
**Right**: Technology is advancing rapidly**,** and it affects every aspect of life.
**Right**: Technology is advancing rapidly**.** It affects every aspect of life.
**Right**: Technology is advancing rapidly**;** it affects every aspect of life.

**Rule**: Two independent clauses cannot be joined by just a comma.
**规则**：两个独立分句不能仅用逗号连接。

### 4. Sentence Fragments | 句子片段

**Wrong**: Because many people cannot afford healthcare.
**Right**: Because many people cannot afford healthcare, **the government should provide free medical services**.

**Wrong**: Which is why education is important.
**Right**: **This is** why education is important.

**Rule**: Every sentence must have a subject and a verb.
**规则**：每个句子必须有主语和谓语。

### 5. Incorrect Tense Usage | 时态使用错误

**Wrong**: The graph shows that sales increase from 2010 to 2020.
**Right**: The graph shows that sales **increased** from 2010 to 2020.

**Wrong**: More people will use renewable energy in the last decade.
**Right**: More people **used** renewable energy in the last decade.

**Rules**:
- Past data → past tense
- Current situation → present tense
- Future predictions → future tense (will, is expected to)

### 6. Wrong Word Form | 错误的词形

**Wrong**: The government should **education** young people.
**Right**: The government should **educate** young people.

**Wrong**: There was a significant **improve** in health.
**Right**: There was a significant **improvement** in health.

**Wrong**: This is a **dramatical** increase.
**Right**: This is a **dramatic** increase.

**Common word form errors**:
| Wrong | Right |
|-------|-------|
| significent | significant |
| environment | environment |
| goverment | government |
| developement | development |
| improvment | improvement |
| dramatical | dramatic |

### 7. Dangling Modifiers | 悬垂修饰语

**Wrong**: Having analyzed the data, the results show a clear trend.
**Right**: Having analyzed the data, **I found** that the results show a clear trend.

**Wrong**: Looking at the graph, there is a clear increase.
**Right**: Looking at the graph, **we can see** a clear increase.

**Rule**: The subject after the comma must be the one performing the action.
**规则**：逗号后的主语必须是执行该动作的人。

## Vocabulary Mistakes | 词汇错误

### 1. Informal Language | 非正式语言

| Informal (Avoid) | Formal (Use) |
|-------------------|--------------|
| a lot of | many / numerous / a significant number of |
| stuff | items / materials / belongings |
| kids | children / young people / youngsters |
| get better | improve / enhance / ameliorate |
| get worse | deteriorate / decline / worsen |
| big | significant / substantial / considerable |
| good | beneficial / advantageous / positive |
| bad | detrimental / harmful / negative |
| think | believe / argue / contend / maintain |
| show | demonstrate / illustrate / indicate / reveal |
| thing | factor / aspect / element / issue |

### 2. Overused Words | 过度使用的词

| Overused | Alternatives |
|----------|-------------|
| important | crucial, vital, essential, significant, paramount |
| very | extremely, remarkably, considerably, significantly |
| many | numerous, a multitude of, a considerable number of |
| because | since, as, due to, owing to, on account of |
| but | however, nevertheless, nonetheless, although, whereas |
| so | therefore, consequently, as a result, thus |

### 3. Wrong Collocations | 错误的搭配

**Wrong**: do a crime → **Right**: commit a crime
**Wrong**: do a mistake → **Right**: make a mistake
**Wrong**: do an effort → **Right**: make an effort
**Wrong**: big problem → **Right**: serious/major problem
**Wrong**: make research → **Right**: conduct research
**Wrong**: take a decision → **Right**: make a decision

### 4. Confused Words | 混淆的词

| Pair | Difference | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| affect/effect | affect = verb, effect = noun | This affects... / The effect is... |
| rise/raise | rise = intransitive, raise = transitive | Prices rise / Raise prices |
| lie/lay | lie = no object, lay = needs object | Lie down / Lay the book down |
| its/it's | its = possessive, it's = it is | Its color / It's raining |
| their/there/they're | their = possessive, there = place, they're = they are | Their house / Over there |

## Task 1 Specific Mistakes | 任务1特定错误

### 1. No Overview Paragraph ❌

The overview is the **most important paragraph** for your Task Achievement score. Without it, you are likely capped at Band 5.
概述是任务完成度分数中**最重要的段落**。没有它，你可能会被限制在5分。

### 2. Including Opinions ❌

**Wrong**: The chart shows that the government spends too much on military.
**Right**: The chart shows that military spending accounted for 25% of the budget.

Task 1 is about **describing**, not **analyzing** or **opining**.
任务1是关于**描述**，而不是**分析**或**发表意见**。

### 3. Copying the Question ❌

**Wrong** (copying): "The bar chart shows the number of tourists visiting three different Caribbean islands between 2010 and 2020."
**Right** (paraphrasing): "The bar chart compares tourist arrivals in three Caribbean nations over an 11-year period from 2010 to 2020."

### 4. Describing Every Data Point ❌

Don't describe every number. Focus on key features, trends, and comparisons.
不要描述每个数字。关注关键特征、趋势和比较。

### 5. Using "Line" Repeatedly ❌

**Wrong**: The blue line shows... The red line shows... The green line shows...
**Right**: The figure for Japan... The rate for China... The number of visitors to Spain...

### 6. Not Specifying Units ❌

**Wrong**: It increased from 50 to 80.
**Right**: It increased from 50 million to 80 million.

## Task 2 Specific Mistakes | 任务2特定错误

### 1. Not Answering All Parts of the Question ❌

**Question**: "What are the causes of pollution, and what solutions can you suggest?"
**Wrong**: Only discussing causes without solutions.
**Right**: Discussing both causes AND solutions.

### 2. Being Too General ❌

**Wrong**: "Pollution is a big problem in many countries."
**Right**: "Air pollution in cities like Beijing and Delhi regularly exceeds WHO safety limits, posing serious health risks to residents."

### 3. Memorized Essays ❌

Examiners can easily identify memorized essays. Your essay must directly address the specific question asked.
考官可以很容易地识别出背诵的文章。你的文章必须直接针对所问的具体问题。

### 4. Under-Length Essays ❌

| Task | Minimum | Penalty |
|------|---------|---------|
| Task 1 | 150 words | Score reduction |
| Task 2 | 250 words | Score reduction |

Always write at least the minimum word count.
始终写至少最低字数。

### 5. Using First Person in Task 1 ❌

**Wrong**: I think the graph shows...
**Right**: The graph illustrates...

Task 1 should be written in a formal, impersonal style.
任务1应该用正式、客观的风格来写。

## Structural Mistakes | 结构错误

### 1. No Clear Paragraphing ❌

**Wrong**: Writing one long paragraph for the entire essay.
**Right**: Clear paragraphs with one central idea each.

### 2. Missing Conclusion ❌

Both Task 1 and Task 2 should end with a concluding statement.
任务1和任务2都应以结论性陈述结束。

### 3. Weak Topic Sentences ❌

**Wrong**: "And another thing is..." / "Also, there is..."
**Right**: "Furthermore, the impact on mental health is also significant."

### 4. No Transitions Between Paragraphs ❌

Use linking phrases to connect paragraphs naturally.
使用连接短语自然地连接段落。

---

## Punctuation Mistakes | 标点错误

### 1. Comma Splice ❌

**Wrong**: "The economy is growing, unemployment is falling."
**Right**: "The economy is growing, and unemployment is falling."
**Right**: "The economy is growing; unemployment is falling."

### 2. Missing Commas After Introductory Phrases ❌

**Wrong**: "In conclusion the benefits outweigh the drawbacks."
**Right**: "In conclusion**,** the benefits outweigh the drawbacks."

### 3. Apostrophe Errors ❌

**Wrong**: "It's economy is strong." (It's = It is)
**Right**: "Its economy is strong." (Its = possessive)

### 4. Overuse of Exclamation Marks ❌

Never use exclamation marks in academic writing.
在学术写作中永远不要使用感叹号。

## Self-Check Checklist | 自查清单

### Before Submitting Your Essay | 提交文章之前

**Task Achievement/Response**:
- [ ] Have I answered ALL parts of the question?
- [ ] Is my position clear throughout?
- [ ] Have I supported my arguments with examples?
- [ ] For Task 1: Have I included an overview?

**Coherence and Cohesion**:
- [ ] Is each paragraph focused on ONE idea?
- [ ] Have I used linking words appropriately?
- [ ] Is there a logical flow between paragraphs?
- [ ] Does each body paragraph have a clear topic sentence?

**Lexical Resource**:
- [ ] Have I used a range of vocabulary?
- [ ] Have I avoided repeating the same words?
- [ ] Is my spelling correct?
- [ ] Have I used formal language?

**Grammatical Range and Accuracy**:
- [ ] Have I used a variety of sentence structures?
- [ ] Have I checked subject-verb agreement?
- [ ] Have I used articles correctly?
- [ ] Have I checked for run-on sentences?

**General**:
- [ ] Have I met the minimum word count?
- [ ] Have I proofread my essay?
- [ ] Is my handwriting legible (paper test)?

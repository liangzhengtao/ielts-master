## What is Task 1? | 任务1是什么？

In IELTS Academic Writing Task 1, you are presented with **visual information** and asked to describe it in your own words. You must write **at least 150 words** in about **20 minutes**.
在雅思学术类写作任务1中，你会看到**视觉信息**，需要用自己的话来描述。你必须在大约**20分钟**内写**至少150个词**。

## Task 1 Essay Structure | 任务1文章结构

A high-scoring Task 1 response follows a **4-paragraph structure**:
高分任务1回答遵循**四段结构**：

### Paragraph 1: Introduction (改写题目) | 第一段：引言

**Purpose**: Paraphrase the question in your own words.
**目的**：用自己的话改写题目。

**Template**:
```
The [chart type] illustrates/shows/compares [what] in [where] from [when] to [when].
```

**Example**:
```
The line graph illustrates changes in the amount of electricity generated
from renewable sources in four countries between 1980 and 2020.
```

**Key techniques**:
- ✅ Use synonyms: `illustrates` → `shows`, `depicts`, `presents`, `gives information about`
- ✅ Change word order
- ✅ Do NOT copy the question word-for-word

**关键技巧**：
- ✅ 使用同义词：`illustrates` → `shows`, `depicts`, `presents`, `gives information about`
- ✅ 改变词序
- ✅ 不要逐字照抄题目

### Paragraph 2: Overview (概述) | 第二段：概述

**Purpose**: Identify 2-3 main trends or key features. This is the MOST important paragraph.
**目的**：找出2-3个主要趋势或关键特征。这是**最重要**的段落。

**Template**:
```
Overall, it is clear that [main trend 1]. Additionally, [main trend 2].
[Main trend 3 if applicable].
```

**Example**:
```
Overall, it is clear that renewable energy production increased in all four
countries over the period. Germany and Denmark showed the most significant
growth, while France and the UK remained relatively stable until 2000 before
rising sharply.
```

**Key techniques**:
- ✅ Start with "Overall" or "In general"
- ✅ Do NOT include specific numbers in the overview
- ✅ Focus on the BIG picture

**关键技巧**：
- ✅ 以"Overall"或"In general"开头
- ✅ 概述中不要包含具体数字
- ✅ 关注整体大趋势

### Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 1 (细节1) | 第三段：主体段落1

**Purpose**: Describe specific details with data support.
**目的**：用数据支持来描述具体细节。

**Template**:
```
Looking at [category/period], [detail with data]. For example, [specific example].
In contrast/comparison, [contrasting detail].
```

### Paragraph 4: Body Paragraph 2 (细节2) | 第四段：主体段落2

**Purpose**: Describe remaining details, comparisons, or another time period.
**目的**：描述剩余的细节、比较或另一个时间段。

## Key Sentence Structures | 关键句型

### Structure 1: Subject + Verb + Adverb
```
Sales increased dramatically from 2010 to 2015.
The number of visitors rose steadily over the period.
Production fell sharply in the final quarter.
```

### Structure 2: There was a/an + Adjective + Noun + in + Noun
```
There was a significant increase in unemployment rates.
There was a gradual decline in the birth rate.
There was a sharp drop in oil prices.
```

### Structure 3: Noun + saw/experienced/witnessed + Adjective + Noun
```
The country saw a dramatic rise in tourism.
The company experienced a steady growth in revenue.
The market witnessed a sharp decline in demand.
```

### Structure 4: Starting at X, Noun + Verb + to Y
```
Starting at 20%, the figure rose to 45% by 2010.
Beginning at just 5 million, exports climbed to 30 million.
```

### Structure 5: Before/After + Verb-ing
```
Before rising to 60%, the figure remained at 30% for five years.
After falling to its lowest point, the rate recovered slightly.
```

## Band 7+ Techniques | 7分以上技巧

### 1. Avoid Repetition | 避免重复

**Low band**: The graph shows... The graph also shows... The graph indicates...
**High band**: The graph illustrates... This is also reflected in... Furthermore, the data reveals...

### 2. Use Data Naturally | 自然地使用数据

**Low band**: In 1990, it was 50%. In 2000, it was 60%. In 2010, it was 70%.
**High band**: Starting at 50% in 1990, the figure rose steadily to 60% by 2000 before reaching a peak of 70% in 2010.

### 3. Group Data Logically | 逻辑地分组数据

Group by:
- **Time periods**: early period vs. later period
- **Categories**: highest performers vs. lowest performers
- **Trends**: increasing vs. decreasing vs. stable

### 4. Use Passive Voice | 使用被动语态

**Active**: People consumed more electricity in 2020.
**Passive**: More electricity was consumed in 2020.

### 5. Vary Sentence Length | 变化句子长度

Mix short and long sentences for better readability.
混合使用短句和长句以提高可读性。

## Common Mistakes to Avoid | 需要避免的常见错误

### 1. Don't Give Opinions | 不要给出观点

**Wrong**: The chart shows that education spending is too low.
**Right**: The chart shows that education spending decreased by 15%.

Task 1 is about **describing**, not **opining**.
任务1是关于**描述**，而不是**发表意见**。

### 2. Don't Include Every Detail | 不要包含每一个细节

Focus on key features and trends, not every single data point.
关注关键特征和趋势，而不是每一个数据点。

### 3. Don't Forget the Overview | 不要忘记概述

The overview paragraph is critical for Band 7+. Without it, you're likely capped at Band 5-6.
概述段落对7分以上至关重要。没有概述，你可能会被限制在5-6分。

### 4. Don't Copy the Question | 不要照抄题目

Paraphrase using synonyms and different sentence structures.
使用同义词和不同的句子结构来改写。

### 5. Don't Use I/You/We | 不要使用 I/You/We

Task 1 should be written in a formal, impersonal style.
任务1应该用正式、客观的风格来写。

## Practice Exercise | 练习题

### Exercise 1: Introduction Practice | 练习1：引言练习

**Original question**: "The bar chart shows the percentage of households in different income brackets in three countries in 2020."

**Practice**: Write an introduction paragraph that paraphrases this question.

**Model answer**:
```
The bar chart compares the proportion of households across various income
levels in three nations in the year 2020.
```

### Exercise 2: Overview Practice | 练习2：概述练习

**Data description**: A line graph showing electricity consumption in 5 countries from 2000-2020. Country A and B show dramatic increases. Country C remains stable. Country D and E show slight decreases.

**Practice**: Write an overview paragraph.

**Model answer**:
```
Overall, it is evident that electricity consumption trends varied considerably
among the five countries. While Country A and Country B experienced substantial
growth over the two-decade period, consumption in Country C remained largely
unchanged. In contrast, Countries D and E both saw marginal declines in their
electricity usage.
```

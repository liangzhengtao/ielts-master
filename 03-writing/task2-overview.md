# Task 2 Overview | 任务2概述

> How to write IELTS Academic Writing Task 2 essays
> 如何写雅思学术类写作任务2的议论文

---

## What is Task 2? | 任务2是什么？

IELTS Writing Task 2 requires you to write a **formal essay** of at least **250 words** in response to a given topic. You should spend about **40 minutes** on this task. Task 2 carries **twice the weight** of Task 1 in your overall writing score.
雅思写作任务2要求你针对给定话题写一篇**至少250词**的**正式议论文**。你应该花大约**40分钟**来完成这个任务。任务2在你的写作总分中**权重是任务1的两倍**。

## How to Identify the Essay Type | 如何识别文章类型

### Opinion Essays | 观点类
```
"Some people think that governments should invest more in public transport.
To what extent do you agree or disagree?"
```
**Keywords**: "To what extent do you agree or disagree?", "Do you agree or disagree?"

### Discussion Essays | 讨论类
```
"Some people think the internet has brought people closer together,
while others think it has pushed them further apart. Discuss both
views and give your own opinion."
```
**Keywords**: "Discuss both views and give your opinion", "Discuss both sides"

### Problem-Solution Essays | 问题解决类
```
"Many cities around the world are facing a housing crisis. What are
the causes of this problem, and what solutions can you suggest?"
```
**Keywords**: "What are the causes/problems?", "What solutions can you suggest?"

### Advantage-Disadvantage Essays | 利弊分析类
```
"More and more people are working from home. What are the advantages
and disadvantages of this trend?"
```
**Keywords**: "What are the advantages and disadvantages?", "Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?"

## Universal Essay Structure | 通用文章结构

All Task 2 essays should follow a **4 or 5 paragraph structure**:
所有任务2文章应遵循**4或5段结构**：

### 4-Paragraph Structure (Most Common) | 四段结构（最常见）

```
Paragraph 1: Introduction (改写题目 + 表明立场)
Paragraph 2: Body Paragraph 1 (主要论点 1)
Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 2 (主要论点 2)
Paragraph 4: Conclusion (总结 + 重申立场)
```

### 5-Paragraph Structure (For Extended Arguments) | 五段结构（用于扩展论证）

```
Paragraph 1: Introduction (改写题目 + 表明立场)
Paragraph 2: Body Paragraph 1 (主要论点 1)
Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 2 (主要论点 2)
Paragraph 4: Body Paragraph 3 (主要论点 3 或让步)
Paragraph 5: Conclusion (总结 + 重申立场)
```

## Writing the Introduction | 写引言

### Introduction Structure | 引言结构

A good introduction has **two parts**:
一个好的引言有**两个部分**：

1. **Paraphrase the question**: Restate the topic in your own words
2. **Thesis statement**: State your position or outline your essay

1. **改写题目**：用自己的话重述话题
2. **论点陈述**：表明你的立场或概述你的文章

### Templates by Essay Type | 按文章类型的模板

**Opinion Essay**:
```
[Paraphrase of the topic]. In my opinion, I [agree/disagree] with this
view because [reason 1] and [reason 2].

OR

[Paraphrase of the topic]. I firmly believe that [your position], and
this essay will explain why.
```

**Discussion Essay**:
```
[Paraphrase of the topic]. While some people argue that [view 1],
others believe that [view 2]. This essay will discuss both perspectives
before presenting my own opinion.

OR

[Paraphrase of the topic]. There are valid arguments on both sides of
this issue, and this essay will examine them before stating my position.
```

**Problem-Solution Essay**:
```
[Paraphrase of the problem]. This essay will examine the main causes
of this issue and propose some possible solutions.

OR

[Paraphrase of the problem]. There are several factors contributing
to this problem, and various measures can be taken to address it.
```

**Advantage-Disadvantage Essay**:
```
[Paraphrase of the trend/issue]. While this trend offers several
benefits, it also has some notable drawbacks. This essay will discuss
both sides.

OR

[Paraphrase of the trend/issue]. There are both advantages and
disadvantages to this development, which this essay will examine.
```

## Writing Body Paragraphs | 写主体段落

### The PEEL Structure | PEEL结构

Each body paragraph should follow the **PEEL** structure:
每个主体段落应遵循**PEEL**结构：

| Letter | Meaning | Chinese | Description |
|--------|---------|---------|-------------|
| **P** | Point | 论点 | State your main idea clearly |
| **E** | Explain | 解释 | Explain why this is the case |
| **E** | Example | 例子 | Give a specific example |
| **L** | Link | 连接 | Link back to the question or forward to the next point |

### Example Body Paragraph | 示例主体段落

```
[P] One of the main advantages of public transport is that it reduces
traffic congestion in urban areas. [E] When more people choose to travel
by bus or train rather than driving their own cars, there are fewer
vehicles on the road, which leads to smoother traffic flow and shorter
commute times. [E] For example, cities like Tokyo and Singapore, which
have invested heavily in efficient public transport systems, consistently
rank among the least congested cities in the world despite their large
populations. [L] Therefore, increased investment in public transport
could be an effective solution to the traffic problems facing many
modern cities.
```

### Body Paragraph Templates | 主体段落模板

**First Body Paragraph**:
```
To begin with, / Firstly, / The primary reason is that [point].
[Explanation]. For instance, / For example, [example].
[Concluding/linking sentence].

OR

One of the most significant [advantages/reasons/problems] is that [point].
[Explanation]. This is illustrated by [example]. [Link].
```

**Second Body Paragraph**:
```
Furthermore, / In addition, / Moreover, [point]. [Explanation].
A clear example of this can be seen in [example]. [Link].

OR

Another key [factor/advantage/reason] is that [point]. [Explanation].
To illustrate, [example]. [Link].
```

**Third Body Paragraph (if using 5 paragraphs)**:
```
Finally, / Additionally, / On the other hand, [point]. [Explanation].
[Example]. [Link to conclusion].
```

## Writing the Conclusion | 写结论

### Conclusion Structure | 结论结构

A good conclusion has **two parts**:
一个好的结论有**两个部分**：

1. **Summary**: Briefly summarize your main points
2. **Final thought**: Restate your position or offer a recommendation

1. **总结**：简要总结你的主要论点
2. **最终想法**：重申你的立场或提出建议

### Templates | 模板

**Opinion Essay**:
```
In conclusion, [restate your position]. While [acknowledge counterargument],
I believe that [your main argument] because [reason 1] and [reason 2].
```

**Discussion Essay**:
```
In conclusion, both sides of this argument have merit. However, after
considering both perspectives, I believe that [your opinion] because
[reason]. Ultimately, [final thought].
```

**Problem-Solution Essay**:
```
In conclusion, [restate the problem] is a serious issue caused by
[main causes]. However, by implementing [solutions], it is possible
to address this challenge. If these measures are taken, [positive outcome].
```

**Advantage-Disadvantage Essay**:
```
In conclusion, while [topic] has both advantages and disadvantages,
I believe that the [advantages/disadvantages] outweigh the
[disadvantages/advantages]. [Final thought or recommendation].
```

## Planning Your Essay | 规划你的文章

### 3-Minute Planning Template | 3分钟规划模板

```
Question topic: _______________
Essay type: _______________
My position: _______________

Body Paragraph 1:
  Point: _______________
  Explanation: _______________
  Example: _______________

Body Paragraph 2:
  Point: _______________
  Explanation: _______________
  Example: _______________

Body Paragraph 3 (optional):
  Point: _______________
  Explanation: _______________
  Example: _______________
```

### Planning Tips | 规划技巧

1. **Spend 3-5 minutes planning** — Never skip planning
2. **Choose 2-3 strong points** — Don't try to cover everything
3. **Think of examples** — Real examples score higher
4. **Decide your position** — Be clear and consistent
5. **Outline your structure** — Know what goes in each paragraph

1. **花3-5分钟做计划**——永远不要跳过计划
2. **选择2-3个有力论点**——不要试图涵盖所有内容
3. **想出例子**——真实的例子得分更高
4. **决定你的立场**——清晰且一致
5. **概述你的结构**——知道每段写什么

## Band 7+ Essay Techniques | 7分以上写作技巧

### 1. Develop Your Ideas Fully | 充分发展你的论点

**Low band**: Public transport is good because it reduces pollution.
**High band**: Public transport significantly reduces air pollution in urban areas. When commuters switch from private vehicles to buses or trains, the total number of cars on the road decreases, leading to lower carbon emissions. This is particularly important in densely populated cities where vehicle emissions are a major contributor to poor air quality.

### 2. Use Specific Examples | 使用具体例子

**Low band**: Many countries have good education systems.
**High band**: Countries like Finland and Singapore, which consistently rank among the top performers in international education assessments, demonstrate that well-funded public education systems can produce excellent outcomes.

### 3. Vary Your Sentence Structure | 变化句式结构

Mix:
- Simple sentences: "This is a serious problem."
- Compound sentences: "The policy was introduced, but it failed to achieve its goals."
- Complex sentences: "Although the government invested heavily in infrastructure, the expected economic benefits did not materialize."
- Conditional sentences: "If governments took more action, the situation could improve."

### 4. Use Formal Academic Language | 使用正式学术语言

| Informal (Avoid) | Formal (Use) |
|-------------------|--------------|
| a lot of | many / numerous / a significant number of |
| stuff | items / materials / belongings |
| kids | children / young people |
| get better | improve / enhance / ameliorate |
| get worse | deteriorate / decline / worsen |
| big | significant / substantial / considerable |
| think | believe / argue / contend / maintain |
| show | demonstrate / illustrate / indicate / reveal |

### 5. Stay on Topic | 紧扣主题

Always refer back to the question. Use the question keywords throughout your essay.
始终回顾题目。在整篇文章中使用题目中的关键词。

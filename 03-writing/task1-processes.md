# Task 1: Process Diagrams | 任务1：流程图

> How to describe process diagrams in IELTS Writing Task 1
> 如何在雅思写作任务1中描述流程图

---

## What are Process Diagrams? | 什么是流程图？

Process diagrams show **how something is made, works, or happens** through a series of stages or steps. Unlike other Task 1 types, they usually do NOT involve numbers or data.
流程图通过一系列阶段或步骤展示**某物如何制造、运作或发生**。与其他任务1类型不同，它们通常不涉及数字或数据。

### Types of Process Diagrams | 流程图的类型

| Type | Description | Chinese |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Manufacturing process** | How a product is made | 制造流程 |
| **Natural process** | How something occurs in nature | 自然过程 |
| **Life cycle** | The stages of life of an organism | 生命周期 |
| **System/process flow** | How a system or procedure works | 系统/程序流程 |

---

## Key Differences from Data-Based Tasks | 与数据任务的关键区别

| Data-Based Tasks | Process Diagrams |
|------------------|------------------|
| Describe numbers and trends | Describe stages and sequences |
| Use data language (percentages, figures) | Use process language (first, then, next) |
| Focus on comparisons | Focus on sequence and flow |
| Passive voice optional | Passive voice essential |
| Overview = main trends | Overview = number of stages and key transformation |

| 数据任务 | 流程图 |
|----------|--------|
| 描述数字和趋势 | 描述阶段和顺序 |
| 使用数据语言（百分比、数字） | 使用流程语言（首先、然后、接下来） |
| 关注比较 | 关注顺序和流程 |
| 被动语态可选 | 被动语态必不可少 |
| 概述 = 主要趋势 | 概述 = 阶段数量和关键转变 |

## 4-Paragraph Structure | 四段结构

### Paragraph 1: Introduction | 第一段：引言

Paraphrase what the process shows. State the number of stages.
改写流程展示了什么。说明阶段数量。

**Templates**:
```
The diagram illustrates the process of [what] in [number] main stages.

The flow chart shows how [product] is [manufactured/produced/created],
from [starting point] to [end point].

The process diagram depicts the stages involved in [what].
```

**Example**:
```
The diagram illustrates the process of producing chocolate, from the
initial harvesting of cocoa beans to the final packaged product, in
eight main stages.
```

### Paragraph 2: Overview | 第二段：概述

Summarize the entire process in 2-3 sentences. State the beginning and end points.
用2-3句话总结整个过程。说明起点和终点。

**Templates**:
```
Overall, the process begins with [starting point] and ends with [end point].
It involves [number] distinct stages, including [key stages].

The process can be divided into [number] main phases: [phase 1], [phase 2],
and [phase 3].
```

**Example**:
```
Overall, the process begins with the harvesting of cocoa beans and ends
with the packaging of finished chocolate products. It involves eight
distinct stages, including fermentation, drying, roasting, and various
processing steps before the final product is ready for sale.
```

### Paragraph 3: Body Paragraph 1 (First Half) | 第三段：主体段落1（前半部分）

Describe the first half of the process in detail.
详细描述流程的前半部分。

**Example**:
```
In the first stage, ripe cocoa pods are harvested from cocoa trees by
hand. The pods are then split open to extract the cocoa beans, which
are placed in large containers. Over the next five to seven days, the
beans undergo fermentation, during which they are covered with banana
leaves. Following this, the fermented beans are spread out and left to
dry in the sun for approximately one week.
```

### Paragraph 4: Body Paragraph 2 (Second Half) | 第四段：主体段落2（后半部分）

Describe the second half of the process.
描述流程的后半部分。

**Example**:
```
Once dried, the beans are transported to a factory where they are roasted
at high temperatures. The roasted beans are then crushed to separate the
outer shells from the inner nibs. These nibs are ground into a paste
known as chocolate liquor, which is then mixed with sugar and milk. The
mixture is refined and tempered before being poured into molds. Finally,
the cooled chocolate is wrapped and packaged for distribution to retail
outlets.
```

## Essential Vocabulary for Processes | 流程图核心词汇

### Sequencing Language | 顺序语言

| Position | Phrases |
|----------|---------|
| **Beginning** | First, / In the first stage, / The process begins with, / Initially, / To begin with, |
| **Middle** | Next, / Then, / After that, / Following this, / Subsequently, / In the next stage, / Once [X is done], / After [verb-ing], |
| **Ending** | Finally, / In the final stage, / The process concludes with, / The last step involves, / Ultimately, |
| **Simultaneous** | At the same time, / Simultaneously, / Meanwhile, / Concurrently, / During this stage, |

### Passive Voice Constructions | 被动语态结构

Process descriptions **require** passive voice because we focus on the process, not who does it.
流程描述**需要**使用被动语态，因为我们关注的是过程，而不是谁在做。

| Active (Avoid) | Passive (Use) |
|----------------|---------------|
| Workers harvest the beans | The beans are harvested |
| They dry the beans in the sun | The beans are dried in the sun |
| The machine grinds the nibs | The nibs are ground into a paste |
| Workers pack the chocolate | The chocolate is packaged |

### Common Passive Patterns | 常见被动模式

```
[Subject] + is/are + past participle (+ by [agent])

The beans are harvested by hand.
The mixture is heated to 100°C.
The product is then packaged for sale.
The water is filtered before being bottled.
```

### Action Verbs for Processes | 流程动作动词

| Category | Verbs |
|----------|-------|
| **Separating** | separate, divide, split, filter, extract, remove |
| **Combining** | combine, mix, blend, merge, add, incorporate |
| **Changing shape** | grind, crush, mold, shape, press, flatten, cut |
| **Changing state** | melt, dissolve, evaporate, condense, freeze, solidify, liquefy |
| **Heating/Cooling** | heat, boil, cook, roast, cool, chill, freeze |
| **Moving** | transport, transfer, deliver, move, convey, pump |
| **Treating** | treat, process, refine, purify, ferment, dry, cure |
| **Inspecting** | check, inspect, test, measure, evaluate, monitor |
| **Finishing** | wrap, package, label, seal, store, distribute |

### Prepositions of Place | 地点介词

```
in a factory / in containers / in large tanks
on a conveyor belt / on trays / in the sun
into molds / into a paste / into containers
through pipes / through filters / through stages
to a factory / to the market / to retail outlets
from the source / from raw materials / from the beginning
```

## Describing Natural Processes | 描述自然过程

Natural processes (water cycle, photosynthesis, life cycles) follow the same structure but may include:
自然过程（水循环、光合作用、生命周期）遵循相同的结构，但可能包括：

### Useful Phrases for Natural Processes | 自然过程的有用短语

```
The cycle begins when...
As a result of..., [noun] [verb]...
This leads to / results in...
The [noun] then [verb]...
Following this, [noun] [verb]...
The process then repeats itself.
This marks the beginning of a new cycle.
```

### Example: The Water Cycle | 示例：水循环

```
The diagram illustrates the water cycle, which consists of four main
stages: evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and collection.

Overall, the cycle begins with water evaporating from bodies of water
and ends with water returning to these sources, creating a continuous
loop.

In the first stage, heat from the sun causes water from oceans, rivers,
and lakes to evaporate, transforming it from a liquid to a gas. This
water vapor then rises into the atmosphere, where it cools and undergoes
condensation, forming tiny water droplets that create clouds.

As the clouds accumulate moisture, the water droplets combine and become
heavier. Eventually, they fall back to Earth as precipitation in the
form of rain, snow, or hail. The collected water then flows into rivers,
lakes, and oceans through surface runoff or by seeping into the ground,
where it replenishes underground water sources. This collected water is
then available to evaporate again, completing the cycle.
```

## Full Sample Response | 完整范文

### Question | 题目

"The diagram below shows the process of recycling plastic bottles."

**Stages**:
1. Collection of used bottles
2. Sorting at recycling center
3. Cleaning and shredding
4. Melting pellets
5. Manufacturing new products
6. Distribution to shops

### Sample Answer | 范文

```
The diagram illustrates the process of recycling plastic bottles, from
collection to the production of new products, in six main stages.

Overall, the process begins with the collection of used plastic bottles
from consumers and ends with the distribution of newly manufactured
products to retail outlets. The cycle involves both consumer participation
and industrial processing.

In the initial stage, used plastic bottles are collected from households
and public recycling bins. These bottles are then transported to a recycling
center, where they are sorted by type and color. Any contaminated or
non-recyclable items are removed during this sorting phase.

Once sorted, the bottles are thoroughly cleaned to remove labels, adhesives,
and residual contents. The clean bottles are subsequently shredded into
small flakes. These flakes are melted at high temperatures and formed
into small pellets, which serve as raw material for manufacturing.

In the final stages, the pellets are transported to manufacturing plants
where they are used to produce new plastic products, such as bottles,
containers, and packaging materials. The finished products are then
distributed to retail outlets, where they are purchased by consumers,
thus completing the recycling cycle.
```

**Word count**: 198 words

## Common Mistakes with Process Diagrams | 流程图常见错误

### 1. Using Active Voice ❌

**Wrong**: Workers harvest the cocoa beans.
**Right**: The cocoa beans are harvested (by hand).

Use passive voice unless the agent (who does the action) is explicitly shown.
使用被动语态，除非明确显示了执行者。

### 2. Missing the Overview ❌

Your overview should state:
- The total number of stages
- Where the process starts and ends
- Any key transformation

你的概述应该说明：
- 总阶段数
- 流程从哪里开始和结束
- 任何关键转变

### 3. Not Using Sequencing Language ❌

**Wrong**: The beans are dried. The beans are roasted. The beans are ground.
**Right**: First, the beans are dried in the sun. Subsequently, they are roasted at high temperatures. The roasted beans are then ground into a paste.

### 4. Adding Information Not in the Diagram ❌

Don't invent details that aren't shown or clearly implied.
不要编造没有显示或明确暗示的细节。

### 5. Forgetting to Describe Transitions ❌

Don't just list stages. Describe how the material changes or moves between stages.
不要只是列出阶段。描述材料在阶段之间如何变化或移动。

## Practice Exercise | 练习

### Exercise 1: Describe a Process | 练习1：描述流程

Write a full Task 1 response for a diagram showing the process of making paper:
1. Trees are cut down
2. Logs transported to mill
3. Bark removed
4. Wood chipped into small pieces
5. Chips mixed with chemicals and water
6. Pulp screened and cleaned
7. Pulp pressed and dried
8. Paper rolled and cut

**Model answer**:
```
The diagram illustrates the process of manufacturing paper from raw wood,
in eight main stages.

Overall, the process begins with the harvesting of trees and concludes
with the production of finished paper products. It involves both mechanical
and chemical processing to transform raw wood into usable paper.

In the first stage, trees are cut down and the logs are transported to a
paper mill. Upon arrival, the bark is removed from the logs, and the
debarked wood is then chipped into small pieces using industrial machinery.

These wood chips are subsequently mixed with chemicals and water in large
vats to create a pulp. The pulp is then screened and cleaned to remove
any impurities or debris. Once purified, the pulp is pressed to remove
excess moisture and then dried using heated rollers.

In the final stage, the dried paper is wound onto large rolls and cut
into the desired sizes before being packaged and distributed to customers.
```

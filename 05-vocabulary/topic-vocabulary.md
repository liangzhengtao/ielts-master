# 话题词汇 / Topic Vocabulary

## 概述 / Overview

本文档按雅思考试常见话题分类整理核心词汇，涵盖教育、环境、科技、健康、社会、经济、科学和文化八大话题领域。

This document organizes core vocabulary by common IELTS topics, covering eight major areas: Education, Environment, Technology, Health, Society, Economy, Science, and Culture.

---

## 1. 教育话题 / Education

### 1.1 核心词汇 / Core Vocabulary

**academy** /əˈkædəmi/
- **释义 / Definition**: 学院；研究院
- **例句 / Example**: She was accepted into the Royal **Academy** of Arts.
  - 她被皇家艺术**学院**录取了。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: academy award, military academy, academy of sciences

**curriculum** /kəˈrɪkjələm/
- **释义 / Definition**: 课程体系
- **例句 / Example**: The school revised its **curriculum** to include more practical skills.
  - 学校修订了**课程体系**，增加了更多实用技能。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: national curriculum, curriculum design, curriculum vitae

**pedagogy** /ˈpedəɡɒdʒi/
- **释义 / Definition**: 教育学；教学法
- **例句 / Example**: Modern **pedagogy** emphasizes student-centered learning.
  - 现代**教育学**强调以学生为中心的学习。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: progressive pedagogy, pedagogy of teaching

**discipline** /ˈdɪsɪplɪn/
- **释义 / Definition**: 纪律；学科
- **例句 / Example**: Self-**discipline** is essential for academic success.
  - 自**律**对学术成功至关重要。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: academic discipline, maintain discipline, self-discipline

**enroll** /ɪnˈrəʊl/
- **释义 / Definition**: 注册；入学
- **例句 / Example**: Over 500 students **enrolled** in the program this year.
  - 今年有超过 500 名学生**注册**了该项目。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: enroll in, enroll at, enrollment rate

**tuition** /tjuˈɪʃn/
- **释义 / Definition**: 学费；教学
- **例句 / Example**: University **tuition** fees have risen significantly.
  - 大学**学费**大幅上涨。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: tuition fee, private tuition, tuition assistance

**literacy** /ˈlɪtərəsi/
- **释义 / Definition**: 读写能力；素养
- **例句 / Example**: The government launched a campaign to improve **literacy** rates.
  - 政府发起了一项提高**识字**率的运动。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: literacy rate, media literacy, financial literacy

**assessment** /əˈsesmənt/
- **释义 / Definition**: 评估；考核
- **例句 / Example**: Continuous **assessment** is more effective than final exams.
  - 持续**评估**比期末考试更有效。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: formative assessment, assessment criteria, risk assessment

**scholarship** /ˈskɒləʃɪp/
- **释义 / Definition**: 奖学金；学术
- **例句 / Example**: She received a full **scholarship** to study abroad.
  - 她获得了全额**奖学金**出国留学。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: win a scholarship, scholarship program, academic scholarship

**tutorial** /tjuˈtɔːriəl/
- **释义 / Definition**: 辅导课；教程
- **例句 / Example**: The professor holds weekly **tutorials** for graduate students.
  - 教授每周为研究生举行**辅导课**。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: online tutorial, tutorial group, attend a tutorial

## 2. 环境话题 / Environment

### 2.1 核心词汇 / Core Vocabulary

**ecosystem** /ˈiːkəʊsɪstəm/
- **释义 / Definition**: 生态系统
- **例句 / Example**: Deforestation threatens the **ecosystem** of the entire region.
  - 森林砍伐威胁着整个地区的**生态系统**。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: marine ecosystem, fragile ecosystem, ecosystem services

**biodiversity** /ˌbaɪəʊdaɪˈvɜːsəti/
- **释义 / Definition**: 生物多样性
- **例句 / Example**: Protecting **biodiversity** is essential for environmental health.
  - 保护**生物多样性**对环境健康至关重要。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: biodiversity loss, biodiversity conservation, rich in biodiversity

**emission** /ɪˈmɪʃn/
- **释义 / Definition**: 排放；排放物
- **例句 / Example**: The factory must reduce its carbon **emissions**.
  - 工厂必须减少碳**排放**。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: carbon emission, emission reduction, greenhouse gas emissions

**sustainable** /səˈsteɪnəbl/
- **释义 / Definition**: 可持续的
- **例句 / Example**: We need to develop **sustainable** energy sources.
  - 我们需要开发**可持续的**能源。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: sustainable development, sustainable growth, environmentally sustainable

**conservation** /ˌkɒnsəˈveɪʃn/
- **释义 / Definition**: 保护；保存
- **例句 / Example**: Wildlife **conservation** requires international cooperation.
  - 野生动物**保护**需要国际合作。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: nature conservation, conservation efforts, conservation area

**renewable** /rɪˈnjuːəbl/
- **释义 / Definition**: 可再生的
- **例句 / Example**: Solar and wind are **renewable** energy sources.
  - 太阳能和风能是**可再生**能源。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: renewable energy, renewable resources, renewable sources

**pollution** /pəˈluːʃn/
- **释义 / Definition**: 污染
- **例句 / Example**: Air **pollution** is a major health concern in urban areas.
  - 空气**污染**是城市地区的主要健康问题。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: air pollution, water pollution, pollution control

**habitat** /ˈhæbɪtæt/
- **释义 / Definition**: 栖息地
- **例句 / Example**: Urban expansion is destroying natural **habitats**.
  - 城市扩张正在破坏自然**栖息地**。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: natural habitat, wildlife habitat, habitat destruction

**deplete** /dɪˈpliːt/
- **释义 / Definition**: 耗尽；枯竭
- **例句 / Example**: Overfishing has **depleted** fish stocks in the ocean.
  - 过度捕捞使海洋鱼类资源**枯竭**。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: deplete resources, depleted ozone, resource depletion

**fossil** /ˈfɒsl/
- **释义 / Definition**: 化石；化石燃料
- **例句 / Example**: **Fossil** fuels contribute significantly to climate change.
  - **化石**燃料对气候变化有重大影响。
- **搭配 / Collocations**: fossil fuel, fossil record, fossil evidence

## 4. 健康话题 / Health

# 习语和短语动词 / Idioms & Phrasal Verbs

## 概述 / Overview

本文档收录雅思口语和写作中常用的习语、短语动词和固定表达，帮助考生提升语言的自然度和地道性。

This document contains common idioms, phrasal verbs, and fixed expressions for IELTS speaking and writing to help candidates improve naturalness and authenticity.

---

## 1. 常用习语 / Common Idioms

### 1.1 描述人物 / Describing People

**a people person** / ˈpiːpl ˌpɜːsn/
- **释义**: 善于交际的人 / someone who enjoys being with others
- **例句**: She's a real **people person** — she gets along with everyone.
  - 她是个真正的**善于交际的人**——她和每个人都相处得很好。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 1-2

**a hard worker** / hɑːrd ˈwɜːkə(r)/
- **释义**: 勤奋的人 / someone who works with great effort
- **例句**: My colleague is a **hard worker** who never complains about overtime.
  - 我的同事是个**勤奋的人**，从不抱怨加班。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 2-3

**a quick learner** / kwɪk ˈlɜːnə(r)/
- **释义**: 学得快的人 / someone who learns new things quickly
- **例句**: Children are often **quick learners** when it comes to languages.
  - 儿童在语言方面通常是**快速学习者**。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 1-2

**an early bird** / ˈɜːli bɜːd/
- **释义**: 早起的人 / someone who wakes up early
- **例句**: My father is an **early bird** — he gets up at 5 a.m. every day.
  - 我父亲是个**早起的人**——他每天早上5点起床。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 1

**a night owl** / naɪt aʊl/
- **释义**: 夜猫子 / someone who stays up late
- **例句**: I'm more of a **night owl** — I do my best work after midnight.
  - 我更像一个**夜猫子**——我在午夜后工作效率最高。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 1

**down to earth** / daʊn tə ˈɜːθ/
- **释义**: 务实的；脚踏实地的 / practical and realistic
- **例句**: Despite her fame, she remains **down to earth** and approachable.
  - 尽管她很有名，但她仍然**脚踏实地**、平易近人。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 2

**level-headed** / ˌlevl ˈhedɪd/
- **释义**: 冷静的；理智的 / calm and sensible
- **例句**: A good leader needs to be **level-headed** in times of crisis.
  - 好的领导者需要在危机时期保持**冷静理智**。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 3

**thick-skinned** / θɪk skɪnd/
- **释义**: 脸皮厚的；不轻易受打击的 / not easily offended
- **例句**: In business, you need to be **thick-skinned** to handle criticism.
  - 在商业中，你需要**脸皮厚**才能应对批评。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 2-3

### 1.2 描述情况 / Describing Situations

**a blessing in disguise** / ˈblesɪŋ ɪn dɪsˈɡaɪz/
- **释义**: 塞翁失马 / something that seems bad but turns out to be good
- **例句**: Losing that job was **a blessing in disguise** — it motivated me to start my own business.
  - 失去那份工作是**塞翁失马**——它激励我自己创业。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 2-3

**a double-edged sword** / ˌdʌbl edʒd ˈsɔːrd/
- **释义**: 双刃剑 / something that has both positive and negative effects
- **例句**: Technology is **a double-edged sword** — it connects us but can also isolate us.
  - 科技是**一把双刃剑**——它连接我们，但也可能使我们孤立。
- **使用场景**: Writing Task 2, Speaking Part 3

**a vicious circle** / ˈvɪʃəs ˈsɜːkl/
- **释义**: 恶性循环 / a situation where one problem causes another
- **例句**: Poverty and lack of education create **a vicious circle**.
  - 贫困和缺乏教育形成了**恶性循环**。
- **使用场景**: Writing Task 2

**a catch-22 situation** / ˌkætʃ twenti ˈtuː/
- **释义**: 进退两难的境地 / a dilemma with no good solution
- **例句**: It's a **catch-22** — you need experience to get a job, but you need a job to get experience.
  - 这是**进退两难**——你需要经验才能找到工作，但你需要工作才能获得经验。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 3

**the tip of the iceberg** / tɪp əv ˈaɪsbɜːɡ/
- **释义**: 冰山一角 / a small part of a much larger problem
- **例句**: The reported cases are just **the tip of the iceberg**.
  - 报告的病例只是**冰山一角**。
- **使用场景**: Writing Task 2, Speaking Part 3

**a grey area** / ɡreɪ ˈeəriə/
- **释义**: 灰色地带；模糊区域 / an unclear situation
- **例句**: The legality of the practice is **a grey area**.
  - 这种做法的合法性是一个**灰色地带**。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 3, Writing Task 2

**a slippery slope** / ˈslɪpəri sləʊp/
- **释义**: 滑坡；危险的开始 / a course of action that leads to worse things
- **例句**: Censorship can be **a slippery slope** towards authoritarianism.
  - 审查制度可能是通往威权主义的**滑坡**。
- **使用场景**: Writing Task 2

**the bottom line** / ˈbɒtəm laɪn/
- **释义**: 最重要的一点 / the most important fact
- **例句**: The **bottom line** is that we need to reduce costs.
  - **最重要的一点**是我们需要降低成本。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 3

### 1.3 表达观点 / Expressing Opinions

**to be honest** / biː ˈɒnɪst/
- **释义**: 说实话 / to tell the truth
- **例句**: **To be honest**, I think the plan needs more work.
  - **说实话**，我认为这个计划需要更多工作。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 1-3

**in my opinion** / ɪn maɪ əˈpɪnjən/
- **释义**: 在我看来 / from my point of view
- **例句**: **In my opinion**, education should be free for everyone.
  - **在我看来**，教育应该对每个人免费。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 3, Writing Task 2

**as far as I'm concerned** / æz fɑːr æz aɪm kənˈsɜːnd/
- **释义**: 就我而言 / from my perspective
- **例句**: **As far as I'm concerned**, the benefits outweigh the drawbacks.
  - **就我而言**，利大于弊。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 3

**it goes without saying** / ɪt ɡəʊz wɪˈðaʊt ˈseɪɪŋ/
- **释义**: 不言而喻 / it's obvious
- **例句**: **It goes without saying** that education is important for everyone.
  - **不言而喻**，教育对每个人都很重要。
- **使用场景**: Writing Task 2

**I'm of the opinion that** / aɪm əv əˈpɪnjən ðæt/
- **释义**: 我的观点是 / I believe that
- **例句**: **I'm of the opinion that** stricter laws are needed.
  - **我的观点是**需要更严格的法律。
- **使用场景**: Writing Task 2

### 1.4 描述变化 / Describing Changes

**a sea change** / siː ˈtʃeɪndʒ/
- **释义**: 巨大的变化 / a complete transformation
- **例句**: There has been **a sea change** in public attitudes towards the environment.
  - 公众对环境的态度发生了**巨大变化**。
- **使用场景**: Writing Task 2

**a turning point** / ˈtɜːnɪŋ pɔɪnt/
- **释义**: 转折点 / a moment when things change direction
- **例句**: The invention of the internet was **a turning point** in human history.
  - 互联网的发明是人类历史的**转折点**。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 2, Writing Task 2

**a watershed moment** / ˈwɔːtəʃed ˈməʊmənt/
- **释义**: 分水岭时刻 / a critical turning point
- **例句**: The fall of the Berlin Wall was **a watershed moment** in history.
  - 柏林墙的倒塌是历史上的**分水岭时刻**。
- **使用场景**: Writing Task 2

**the dawn of** / ˈdɔːn əv/
- **释义**: ...的开端 / the beginning of something
- **例句**: We are living in **the dawn of** the artificial intelligence era.
  - 我们生活在人工智能时代的**开端**。
- **使用场景**: Writing Task 2

## 2. 常用短语动词 / Common Phrasal Verbs

### 2.1 表达增加和减少 / Expressing Increase and Decrease

**go up** / ɡəʊ ʌp/
- **释义**: 上升 / to increase
- **例句**: The price of oil has **gone up** significantly.
  - 石油价格大幅**上涨**了。
- **正式程度**: Informal; 替代词: increase, rise

**go down** / ɡəʊ daʊn/
- **释义**: 下降 / to decrease
- **例句**: The temperature will **go down** to below zero tonight.
  - 今晚温度将**降至**零度以下。
- **正式程度**: Informal; 替代词: decrease, decline

**bring up** / brɪŋ ʌp/
- **释义**: 提出（话题） / to introduce a topic
- **例句**: She **brought up** an interesting point during the meeting.
  - 她在会议上**提出了**一个有趣的观点。
- **用法**: bring up + topic/issue/point

**set up** / set ʌp/
- **释义**: 建立；设立 / to establish
- **例句**: The company was **set up** in 2010.
  - 这家公司成立于 2010 年。
- **用法**: set up + company/organization/business

**put forward** / pʊt ˈfɔːwəd/
- **释义**: 提出（建议） / to suggest or propose
- **例句**: The committee **put forward** a new proposal.
  - 委员会**提出了**一项新提案。
- **用法**: put forward + proposal/idea/suggestion

### 2.2 表达理解和发现 / Expressing Understanding and Discovery

**find out** / faɪnd aʊt/
- **释义**: 发现；查明 / to discover information
- **例句**: I need to **find out** more about the program before applying.
  - 我需要在申请前**了解**更多关于该项目的信息。
- **用法**: find out + about/that/what

**work out** / wɜːk aʊt/
- **释义**: 解决；算出 / to solve or calculate
- **例句**: We need to **work out** a solution to this problem.
  - 我们需要**想出**这个问题的解决方案。
- **用法**: work out + problem/solution/plan

**figure out** / ˈfɪɡər aʊt/
- **释义**: 弄清楚 / to understand or solve
- **例句**: Scientists are trying to **figure out** the cause of the disease.
  - 科学家们正试图**弄清楚**这种疾病的原因。
- **用法**: figure out + what/how/why

**point out** / pɔɪnt aʊt/
- **释义**: 指出 / to mention or draw attention to
- **例句**: The report **points out** several areas for improvement.
  - 报告**指出了**几个需要改进的领域。
- **用法**: point out + fact/problem/issue

**come across** / kʌm əˈkrɒs/
- **释义**: 偶然发现 / to find by chance
- **例句**: I **came across** an interesting article while browsing the internet.
  - 我在浏览互联网时**偶然发现**了一篇有趣的文章。
- **用法**: come across + something/someone

### 2.3 表达影响和效果 / Expressing Impact and Effect

**bring about** / brɪŋ əˈbaʊt/
- **释义**: 导致；引起 / to cause something to happen
- **例句**: The new policy **brought about** significant changes in the industry.
  - 新政策**带来**了行业的重大变化。
- **用法**: bring about + change/reform/transformation

**lead to** / liːd tuː/
- **释义**: 导致 / to result in
- **例句**: Poor nutrition can **lead to** serious health problems.
  - 营养不良会**导致**严重的健康问题。
- **用法**: lead to + result/outcome/consequence

**result in** / rɪˈzʌlt ɪn/
- **释义**: 导致 / to cause
- **例句**: The accident **resulted in** several injuries.
  - 事故**造成**了数人受伤。
- **用法**: result in + outcome/effect

**cut down on** / kʌt daʊn ɒn/
- **释义**: 减少 / to reduce
- **例句**: People should **cut down on** sugar intake for better health.
  - 人们应该**减少**糖的摄入量以保持健康。
- **用法**: cut down on + noun

**phase out** / feɪz aʊt/
- **释义**: 逐步淘汰 / to gradually stop using
- **例句**: The government plans to **phase out** coal power plants.
  - 政府计划**逐步淘汰**燃煤电厂。
- **用法**: phase out + product/policy/practice

### 2.4 表达处理和应对 / Expressing Handling and Coping

**deal with** / diːl wɪð/
- **释义**: 处理；应对 / to handle or manage
- **例句**: We need to **deal with** the issue before it gets worse.
  - 我们需要在问题恶化之前**处理**它。
- **用法**: deal with + problem/issue/situation

**cope with** / kəʊp wɪð/
- **释义**: 应对；承受 / to manage a difficult situation
- **例句**: It can be hard to **cope with** the pressure of exams.
  - **应对**考试压力可能很困难。
- **用法**: cope with + stress/pressure/difficulty

**get over** / ɡet ˈəʊvə(r)/
- **释义**: 克服；恢复 / to recover from
- **例句**: It took her a long time to **get over** the illness.
  - 她花了很长时间才从疾病中**恢复**过来。
- **用法**: get over + illness/setback/failure

**come up with** / kʌm ʌp wɪð/
- **释义**: 想出；提出 / to think of or produce
- **例句**: The team **came up with** an innovative solution.
  - 团队**想出了**一个创新的解决方案。
- **用法**: come up with + idea/plan/solution

**put up with** / pʊt ʌp wɪð/
- **释义**: 忍受 / to tolerate
- **例句**: I can't **put up with** the noise any longer.
  - 我再也**受不了**噪音了。
- **用法**: put up with + something unpleasant

### 2.5 表达沟通和关系 / Expressing Communication and Relationships

**get along with** / ɡet əˈlɒŋ wɪð/
- **释义**: 与...相处融洽 / to have a good relationship with
- **例句**: She **gets along with** everyone in the office.
  - 她和办公室里每个人都**相处融洽**。
- **用法**: get along with + people

**keep in touch** / kiːp ɪn tʌtʃ/
- **释义**: 保持联系 / to maintain contact
- **例句**: We've **kept in touch** since university.
  - 我们从大学起就一直**保持联系**。
- **用法**: keep in touch with + someone

**turn down** / tɜːn daʊn/
- **释义**: 拒绝 / to reject or refuse
- **例句**: She **turned down** the job offer because of the low salary.
  - 她因为工资低**拒绝了**工作邀请。
- **用法**: turn down + offer/invitation/request

**back up** / bæk ʌp/
- **释义**: 支持；备份 / to support with evidence; to make a copy
- **例句**: You should **back up** your claims with evidence.
  - 你应该用证据**支持**你的主张。
- **用法**: back up + claim/statement/data

**bring together** / brɪŋ təˈɡeðə(r)/
- **释义**: 使联合 / to unite
- **例句**: The event **brought together** people from diverse backgrounds.
  - 这次活动**聚集了**来自不同背景的人。
- **用法**: bring together + people/groups

### 2.6 表达成功和失败 / Expressing Success and Failure

**pull off** / pʊl ɒf/
- **释义**: 成功做到 / to succeed in doing something difficult
- **例句**: The team **pulled off** an impressive victory.
  - 团队**取得了**令人印象深刻的胜利。
- **用法**: pull off + victory/success/plan

**fall through** / fɔːl θruː/
- **释义**: 失败；落空 / to fail to happen
- **例句**: The deal **fell through** at the last minute.
  - 交易在最后一刻**落空了**。
- **用法**: The plan/deal/project fell through.

**live up to** / lɪv ʌp tuː/
- **释义**: 达到；不辜负 / to meet expectations
- **例句**: The product failed to **live up to** expectations.
  - 产品未能**达到**预期。
- **用法**: live up to + expectations/standards/promise

**give up** / ɡɪv ʌp/
- **释义**: 放弃 / to stop trying
- **例句**: Never **give up** on your dreams.
  - 永远不要**放弃**你的梦想。
- **用法**: give up + trying/hope/dream

**hold back** / həʊld bæk/
- **释义**: 阻碍；克制 / to restrain or prevent
- **例句**: Fear of failure can **hold people back** from achieving their goals.
  - 对失败的恐惧会**阻碍**人们实现目标。
- **用法**: hold back + someone/progress/growth

## 3. 学术习语 / Academic Idioms

### 3.1 用于写作和学术讨论 / For Writing and Academic Discussion

**shed light on** / ʃed laɪt ɒn/
- **释义**: 阐明；说明 / to clarify or explain
- **例句**: The study **sheds light on** the relationship between diet and health.
  - 这项研究**阐明了**饮食与健康之间的关系。
- **使用场景**: Writing Task 1-2, Speaking Part 3

**in the long run** / ɪn ðə lɒŋ rʌn/
- **释义**: 从长远来看 / over a long period of time
- **例句**: Exercise pays off **in the long run** by improving overall health.
  - 运动通过改善整体健康**从长远来看**是值得的。
- **使用场景**: Writing Task 2, Speaking Part 3

**on a global scale** / ɒn ə ˈɡləʊbl skeɪl/
- **释义**: 在全球范围内 / worldwide
- **例句**: Climate change affects ecosystems **on a global scale**.
  - 气候变化**在全球范围内**影响着生态系统。
- **使用场景**: Writing Task 2

**to a certain extent** / tuː ə ˈsɜːtn ɪkˈstent/
- **释义**: 在一定程度上 / partly
- **例句**: **To a certain extent**, technology has improved our quality of life.
  - **在一定程度上**，科技改善了我们的生活质量。
- **使用场景**: Writing Task 2, Speaking Part 3

**by and large** / baɪ ənd lɑːdʒ/
- **释义**: 总的来说 / generally speaking
- **例句**: **By and large**, the experiment was successful.
  - **总的来说**，实验是成功的。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 3

**the lion's share** / ˈlaɪənz ʃeə(r)/
- **释义**: 最大的份额 / the largest portion
- **例句**: The **lion's share** of the budget goes to education.
  - 预算的**最大份额**用于教育。
- **使用场景**: Writing Task 2

**from scratch** / frəm skrætʃ/
- **释义**: 从零开始 / from the beginning
- **例句**: The team built the system **from scratch**.
  - 团队**从零开始**构建了这个系统。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 2

**in a nutshell** / ɪn ə ˈnʌtʃel/
- **释义**: 简而言之 / in summary
- **例句**: **In a nutshell**, the project was a success.
  - **简而言之**，这个项目是成功的。
- **使用场景**: Speaking Part 2-3

2. shed light on

4. in the long run

### 6.2 短语动词替换练习 / Phrasal Verb Substitution

|:---|:---|

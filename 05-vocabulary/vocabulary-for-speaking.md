# 口语词汇 / Vocabulary for Speaking

本文档整理雅思口语（Part 1、Part 2、Part 3）高频词汇和自然表达，帮助考生提升口语流利度和表达地道性。

This document organizes high-frequency IELTS speaking vocabulary and natural expressions (Part 1, Part 2, Part 3) to help candidates improve fluency and authentic expression.

### 1.2 Part 1 回答技巧表达 / Part 1 Answer Techniques

| 表达 | 释义 | 语境 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **I'm really into...** | 我很喜欢... | 表达强烈喜好 |

### 6.2 语调提示 / Intonation Tips

**表达观点时**: 使用降调表示确定
- I believe this is the right approach. ↘

**提问时**: 使用升调
- Do you think education is important? ↗

**列举时**: 前几项升调，最后一项降调
- There are three reasons: first ↗, second ↗, and third ↘.

**表达不确定时**: 使用升降调
- I'm not sure about that. ↗↘

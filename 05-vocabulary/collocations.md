# 常用搭配 / Common Collocations

## 概述 / Overview

本文档整理雅思考试高频词汇搭配，包括动词+名词、形容词+名词、副词+形容词等常见搭配模式，帮助考生掌握地道的英语表达。

This document organizes high-frequency IELTS collocations, including verb+noun, adjective+noun, adverb+adjective, and other common patterns to help candidates master authentic English expressions.

## 8. 搭配学习方法 / Collocation Study Methods

### 8.1 如何学习搭配 / How to Learn Collocations

1. **语境记忆**: 在句子中记忆搭配，而非孤立记忆
   - 好：记住 "The research **sheds light on** the causes"
   - 差：只记 "shed light on"

2. **分类整理**: 按话题或功能分类学习搭配
   - 例如：研究类搭配、表达观点类搭配

3. **主动使用**: 在写作和口语中主动使用新学的搭配
   - 练习用搭配造句
   - 用搭配改写简单句

4. **注意介词**: 很多搭配的区别在于介词
   - **result in** (导致) vs **result from** (由于)
   - **contribute to** (促成) vs **contribute towards** (贡献)

### 8.2 常见错误 / Common Mistakes

| 错误搭配 ❌ | 正确搭配 ✅ | 说明 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| make research | **do/conduct research** | research 搭配 do 或 conduct |
| do a mistake | **make a mistake** | mistake 搭配 make |
| make homework | **do homework** | homework 搭配 do |
| big problem | **serious problem** | 问题通常用 serious 修饰 |
| open the light | **turn on the light** | 开灯用 turn on |
| close the light | **turn off the light** | 关灯用 turn off |
| say a lie | **tell a lie** | 谎言搭配 tell |
| make a photo | **take a photo** | 照片搭配 take |
| give attention | **pay attention** | 注意力搭配 pay |
| take a decision | **make a decision** | 决定搭配 make |

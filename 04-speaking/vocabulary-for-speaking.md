# Vocabulary for Speaking 口语高频词汇

> 本文档收录 IELTS 口语考试高频词汇和短语，帮助你丰富表达，提高词汇得分。
> This document contains high-frequency IELTS speaking vocabulary and phrases to enrich your expression.

---

## 词汇学习策略 / Vocabulary Learning Strategies

### 如何有效学习词汇

1. **学习搭配，而非孤立单词**
   - ❌ learn: "important"
   - ✅ learn: "play an important role", "of great importance"

2. **学习同义词替换**
   - good → excellent, outstanding, remarkable, impressive
   - bad → terrible, awful, dreadful, disappointing

3. **学习例句，理解用法**
   - 不要只记单词意思
   - 学习单词在句子中的用法
   - 练习自己造句

4. **定期复习，巩固记忆**
   - 使用间隔重复法
   - 每天复习旧词汇
   - 在口语练习中使用新词汇

## 核心动词 / Essential Verbs

### 表达观点 Expressing Opinions

| 基础 | 进阶 | 高级 |
|------|------|------|
| think | believe | maintain, contend, assert |
| say | state | declare, assert, proclaim |
| show | demonstrate | illustrate, indicate, reveal |
| agree | concur | endorse, support, advocate |
| disagree | object | oppose, challenge, dispute |

### 描述变化 Describing Changes

**增加 Increase**
- increase, rise, grow, expand, surge, soar, skyrocket
- The number of... has increased significantly.
- There has been a dramatic rise in...

**减少 Decrease**
- decrease, decline, drop, fall, plummet, shrink
- The number of... has decreased steadily.
- There has been a gradual decline in...

**保持不变 Remain Stable**
- remain stable, stay constant, maintain, hold steady
- The number has remained relatively stable.
- There has been little change in...

### 描述影响 Describing Impact

- affect: 影响
- influence: 影响（更正式）
- impact: 冲击，影响
- shape: 塑造
- determine: 决定
- contribute to: 促成
- lead to: 导致
- result in: 导致
- give rise to: 引起
- bring about: 带来

## 常用短语 / Useful Phrases

### 开场白 Opening Phrases

**Part 1 开场**
- Yes, definitely. 是的，绝对是。
- Well, actually... 嗯，实际上...
- That's an interesting question. 这是个有趣的问题。
- I've never really thought about that, but... 我从没认真想过这个问题，但是...
- It's hard to say, but... 很难说，但是...

**Part 2 开场**
- I'd like to talk about... 我想谈谈...
- Let me tell you about... 让我告诉你关于...
- I'm going to describe... 我要描述...
- One thing that comes to mind is... 我想到的一件事是...
- The [person/place/event] I'd like to talk about is... 我想谈的[人/地方/事件]是...

**Part 3 开场**
- That's a complex question. Let me think about it. 这是个复杂的问题，让我想想。
- From my perspective... 从我的角度来看...
- I believe that... 我相信...
- In my opinion... 在我看来...
- As far as I'm concerned... 就我而言...

### 过渡语 Transition Phrases

**添加信息**
- Moreover / Furthermore / In addition 此外
- What's more 更重要的是
- Besides 除此之外
- Another point is... 另一点是...
- Not only... but also... 不仅...而且...

**对比转折**
- However / Nevertheless 然而
- On the other hand 另一方面
- In contrast / By contrast 相比之下
- Although / Even though 虽然
- Despite / In spite of 尽管

**因果关系**
- Therefore / Consequently 因此
- As a result 结果
- That's why 这就是为什么
- Due to / Owing to 由于
- Because / Since / As 因为

**举例说明**
- For example / For instance 例如
- Such as / Like 比如
- To give you an example 举个例子
- A good example would be... 一个好的例子是...
- Take... for example 以...为例

**时间顺序**
- First / Firstly / First of all 首先
- Second / Secondly / In addition 其次
- Then / Next 接着
- After that / Subsequently 之后
- Finally / Lastly / Last but not least 最后

**总结概括**
- In conclusion / To sum up 总之
- All in all / Overall 总的来说
- In short / Briefly 简而言之
- To summarize 概括来说
- On the whole 总体而言

### 结束语 Closing Phrases

**Part 1 结束**
- So, that's why I... 所以，这就是我为什么...
- That's basically it. 基本就是这样。
- I think that sums it up. 我想这概括了。

**Part 2 结束**
- So, that's the [person/place/event] I wanted to talk about. 所以，这就是我要谈的[人/地方/事件]。
- This experience taught me... 这次经历教会了我...
- Looking back, I think... 回顾过去，我认为...

**Part 3 结束**
- In conclusion, I believe... 总之，我相信...
- To sum up, my view is... 总结一下，我的观点是...
- So, that's my perspective on... 所以，这就是我对...的看法

## 高级表达 / Advanced Expressions

### 强调表达 Emphasis

- What's particularly interesting is... 特别有趣的是...
- What really matters is... 真正重要的是...
- The key point is... 关键点是...
- It's worth noting that... 值得注意的是...
- It goes without saying that... 不用说...

### 让步表达 Concession

- While it's true that... 虽然...是事实
- Granted, ... 诚然...
- I can see why some people think that... 我能理解为什么有些人这样想
- Admittedly, ... 不可否认...
- Despite the fact that... 尽管...

### 假设表达 Hypothetical

- If I had to choose... 如果我必须选择...
- Suppose that... 假设...
- Imagine if... 想象一下如果...
- In an ideal world... 在理想情况下...
- Hypothetically speaking... 假设地说...

### 推测表达 Speculation

- I suspect that... 我怀疑...
- It's likely that... 很可能...
- There's a good chance that... 很有可能...
- I wouldn't be surprised if... 我不会惊讶如果...
- It's conceivable that... 可以想象...

## 词汇练习方法 / Vocabulary Practice Methods

### 方法一：话题词汇本

1. 准备一个笔记本
2. 按话题分类记录词汇
3. 每个词汇写 2-3 个例句
4. 定期复习和使用

### 方法二：同义词替换练习

1. 选择一个基础词
2. 列出所有同义词
3. 练习在不同语境中使用
4. 录音检查使用是否准确

### 方法三：语境学习

1. 阅读英语文章或听英语播客
2. 记录不熟悉的词汇
3. 学习词汇在语境中的用法
4. 尝试自己使用这些词汇

### 方法四：每日五词

1. 每天学习 5 个新词汇
2. 学习词汇的意思、发音和用法
3. 造 3 个句子
4. 在口语练习中使用

# Part 1: Introduction & Interview 第一部分：自我介绍与面试

> Part 1 是口语考试的开场，考官会问一些关于你个人背景和日常生活的问题。
> Part 1 is the opening section where the examiner asks questions about your personal background and daily life.

---

## 考试流程 / Test Format

- **时间**：4-5 分钟
- **问题数量**：10-12 个问题
- **话题数量**：通常 3 个话题（每个话题 3-4 个问题）
- **特点**：问题简单直接，考察基本交流能力

---

## 常见开场问题 / Common Opening Questions

考官通常会以这些问题开始：

1. **Good morning/afternoon. Can I have your full name, please?**
   - 回答：My full name is [你的全名]. You can call me [英文名].

2. **Can I see your identification, please?**
   - 回答：Sure, here is my ID/passport.

3. **Where are you from?**
   - 回答：I'm from [城市], which is located in [省份/地区] of China.

4. **Do you work or study?**
   - 回答（工作）：I work as a [职业] at [公司/机构].
   - 回答（学生）：I'm currently studying [专业] at [学校].

## 回答技巧 / Response Techniques

### 技巧一：避免简短回答

**❌ 错误示范**：
> Q: Do you like cooking?
> A: Yes, I do.

**✅ 正确示范**：
> Q: Do you like cooking?
> A: Yes, I'm quite keen on cooking, especially Chinese cuisine. I find it really relaxing to prepare a meal after a long day at work. Plus, it's a great way to bond with my family when we cook together.

### 技巧二：使用 AREL 框架

**Question**: Do you like your neighborhood?

**A (Answer)**: Yes, I'm quite fond of my neighborhood.
**R (Reason)**: It's conveniently located near my workplace and has plenty of amenities.
**E (Example)**: For example, there's a lovely park just a 5-minute walk away where I often go jogging in the morning.
**L (Link)**: So overall, it's a great place to live and I can't imagine moving elsewhere.

### 技巧三：加入个人感受和细节

**❌ 平淡回答**：
> I like sports.

**✅ 生动回答**：
> I'm really into sports, particularly basketball. There's something thrilling about the fast pace and teamwork involved. I try to play at least twice a week with my colleagues, which is a fantastic way to unwind after work.

### 技巧四：适当使用连接词

**常用连接词**：
- **补充信息**：Also, Besides, In addition, What's more
- **举例**：For example, For instance, Such as, Like
- **对比**：However, On the other hand, In contrast
- **原因**：Because, Since, As, Due to
- **结果**：So, Therefore, As a result, That's why

## 话题范例答案 / Sample Answers by Topic

### 话题一：Hometown 家乡

**Q: Where are you from?**
> I'm from Chengdu, which is the capital city of Sichuan Province in southwestern China. It's a vibrant city known for its spicy cuisine and relaxed lifestyle. I've lived there for most of my life and I'm quite attached to it.

**Q: Is that a big city or a small place?**
> It's actually a pretty large city with a population of over 16 million people. In recent years, it has developed rapidly and become one of the major economic centers in western China. Despite its size, it still maintains a laid-back atmosphere that I really appreciate.

**Q: What do you like most about your hometown?**
> Without a doubt, it's the food. Chengdu is famous for its Sichuan cuisine, which is known for its bold flavors and liberal use of chili peppers. There are countless street food stalls and restaurants where you can enjoy authentic dishes like hotpot and mapo tofu. The culinary scene there is absolutely incredible.

**Q: Has your hometown changed much in recent years?**
> Yes, it has changed dramatically. The infrastructure has improved significantly with the expansion of the metro system and new highways. Many modern shopping malls and skyscrapers have sprung up, transforming the cityscape. However, I'm glad that the old neighborhoods and traditional teahouses have been preserved.

### 话题二：Work/Studies 工作/学习

**Q: What do you do for a living?**
> I work as a software engineer at a tech company in Beijing. My main responsibility is developing mobile applications for both iOS and Android platforms. It's a challenging but rewarding job that allows me to constantly learn new technologies.

**Q: Why did you choose that job?**
> Well, I've always been passionate about technology and problem-solving. When I was in university, I took several programming courses and realized that software development was something I genuinely enjoyed. Plus, the tech industry offers excellent career prospects and competitive salaries.

**Q: Do you enjoy your work?**
> Yes, for the most part. I find it intellectually stimulating to solve complex technical problems and see my code being used by millions of people. However, it can be stressful at times, especially when we're working on tight deadlines. But overall, I'm quite satisfied with my career choice.

**Q: What's the most interesting part of your job?**
> I'd say it's the creative aspect. Despite being a technical field, software development actually requires a lot of creativity in designing user interfaces and solving problems in innovative ways. I also enjoy collaborating with my team members, as we often brainstorm ideas together.

### 话题三：Weather 天气

**Q: What's the weather like in your city?**
> The weather in my city varies quite a bit throughout the year. Summers are hot and humid with temperatures often reaching 35 degrees Celsius, while winters are relatively mild, usually around 5 to 10 degrees. Spring and autumn are probably the most pleasant seasons with comfortable temperatures.

**Q: What's your favorite season?**
> I'm quite fond of autumn, particularly October and November. The weather is neither too hot nor too cold, and the humidity is at a comfortable level. Plus, the scenery is beautiful with the leaves changing color to vibrant shades of red and gold. It's perfect for outdoor activities.

**Q: Does the weather affect your mood?**
> To some extent, yes. I tend to feel more energetic and motivated on sunny days, especially when the sky is clear and blue. On the other hand, prolonged rainy periods can make me feel a bit gloomy. However, I've learned to adapt and find indoor activities to keep myself occupied during bad weather.

## 常见错误 / Common Mistakes

### 错误一：回答过于简短
**❌** Yes, I like music.
**✅** Yes, I'm really into music, especially pop and R&B. I usually listen to music during my commute to work.

### 错误二：过度使用填充词
**❌** Um, well, you know, like, I think... um...
**✅** I believe... In my opinion... I'd say...

### 错误三：答非所问
**Q: Do you like cooking?**
**❌** My mother is a good cook. She makes delicious food.
**✅** Yes, I enjoy cooking quite a bit. I find it relaxing and creative.

### 错误四：时态混乱
**❌** I go to Beijing last year and I see the Great Wall.
**✅** I went to Beijing last year and saw the Great Wall.

### 错误五：中式英语
**❌** My English is very poor.（谦虚表达不适用于口语考试）
**✅** I'm still working on improving my English, but I can communicate effectively.

## 练习方法 / Practice Methods

### 方法一：录音回听
1. 准备一个录音设备或手机
2. 随机选择一个 Part 1 话题
3. 计时 30 秒回答每个问题
4. 回听录音，分析自己的表现
5. 记录需要改进的地方

### 方法二：镜像练习
1. 站在镜子前练习
2. 观察自己的面部表情和口型
3. 保持自然的微笑和眼神交流
4. 注意不要有太多小动作

### 方法三：影子跟读
1. 选择一个英语播客或视频
2. 听一句，暂停，模仿发音
3. 注意语调和重音
4. 逐渐提高跟读速度

### 方法四：话题卡片
1. 准备 20-30 张话题卡片
2. 每张卡片写一个话题关键词
3. 随机抽取，立即回答
4. 练习即兴表达能力

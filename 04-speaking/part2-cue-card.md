# Part 2: Long Turn (Cue Card) 第二部分：个人陈述

> Part 2 要求考生根据话题卡进行 1-2 分钟的个人陈述，考察考生的长篇表达能力。
> Part 2 requires candidates to give a 1-2 minute talk based on a cue card, testing extended speech ability.

---

## 考试流程 / Test Format

- **准备时间**：1 分钟（可以做笔记）
- **陈述时间**：1-2 分钟
- **考官提示**：1 分钟后考官会说 "Thank you"
- **后续问题**：考官可能会问 1-2 个相关问题

---

## 话题卡结构 / Cue Card Structure

每张话题卡通常包含：

```
Describe [话题]

You should say:
- What/Who [具体要求1]
- When/Where [具体要求2]
- Why/How [具体要求3]
- And explain [个人感受/原因]
```

### 示例 / Example

```
Describe a book that you have read recently.

You should say:
- What the book was
- What it was about
- Why you decided to read it
- And explain how you felt about it.
```

---

## 笔记技巧 / Note-Taking Strategies

### 1 分钟准备时间的有效利用

**Step 1: 快速审题 (10 秒)**
- 仔细阅读话题卡上的所有要求
- 确定要描述的对象

**Step 2: 头脑风暴 (20 秒)**
- 快速写下 3-4 个关键词
- 选择最有话说的角度

**Step 3: 组织结构 (20 秒)**
- 确定开头、主体、结尾
- 按照话题卡要求排列顺序

**Step 4: 检查补充 (10 秒)**
- 确保覆盖所有要点
- 添加细节和例子

### 笔记示例 / Note Example

**话题**：Describe a memorable trip you took.

```
- 2023 summer, Japan with friends
- Tokyo → Kyoto → Osaka
- 2 weeks, cherry blossom season
- Highlights: temple, sushi, onsen
- Memorable: cultural experience, bonding
```

## 回答框架 / Response Framework

### 框架一：时间线框架 (Timeline)

适用于：经历、旅行、活动

```
Opening: 引入话题
    ↓
Past: 背景信息（何时、何地）
    ↓
During: 经过描述（做了什么、看到了什么）
    ↓
After: 后续影响（感受、收获）
    ↓
Closing: 总结
```

### 框架二：STAR 框架

适用于：人物、物品、事件

```
S - Situation (背景介绍)
T - Task (描述对象)
A - Action (具体细节)
R - Result (结果/感受)
```

### 框架三：感官框架 (Sensory)

适用于：地点、美食、体验

```
Visual: 看到的
Auditory: 听到的
Olfactory: 闻到的
Tactile: 触感的
Emotional: 感受的
```

## 话题范例答案 / Sample Answers by Topic

### 话题一：Describe a person who has influenced you

**Opening (引入)**
> I'd like to talk about my high school English teacher, Mr. Wang, who has had a profound influence on my life.

**Background (背景)**
> I first met him when I was 15 years old. He was in his early 30s at that time and had just returned from studying in the UK. He became our English teacher in my second year of high school.

**Details (细节)**
> What made him special was his teaching style. Unlike other teachers who focused on grammar and vocabulary, he encouraged us to think critically and express our opinions. He often organized debates and role-plays in class, which made learning English fun and engaging. He also shared stories about his experiences abroad, which broadened our horizons and inspired many of us to pursue international education.

**Impact (影响)**
> His influence on me was enormous. He didn't just teach me English; he taught me how to think independently and express myself confidently. Because of him, I developed a passion for languages and decided to major in English in university. Even now, I still keep in touch with him and he continues to mentor me.

**Closing (总结)**
> So, Mr. Wang is definitely the person who has influenced me the most. He changed my perspective on learning and helped me become the person I am today.

---

### 话题二：Describe a place you would like to visit

**Opening (引入)**
> I've always dreamed of visiting Iceland, a Nordic island country in the North Atlantic Ocean.

**Background (背景)**
> I first became interested in Iceland about five years ago when I watched a documentary about the Northern Lights. Since then, I've been fascinated by the country's unique landscape and natural phenomena.

**Details (细节)**
> There are several things I'd love to experience there. First, I want to see the Northern Lights, which are one of the most spectacular natural phenomena in the world. Second, I'm eager to visit the Blue Lagoon, a famous geothermal spa with milky blue water. Third, I'd like to explore the Golden Circle, which includes the stunning Gullfoss waterfall and the Geysir geothermal area. I've also heard that Iceland has incredible black sand beaches and glaciers that look otherworldly.

**Why (原因)**
> The main reason I want to visit Iceland is its untouched natural beauty. Unlike many tourist destinations, Iceland is relatively unspoiled and offers a chance to connect with nature in its purest form. I believe it would be a truly unique and unforgettable experience.

**Closing (总结)**
> I'm hoping to make this trip happen next summer. It's definitely at the top of my travel bucket list.

---

### 话题三：Describe a memorable event in your life

**Opening (引入)**
> One of the most memorable events in my life was my graduation ceremony three years ago, which marked the end of an important chapter.

**Background (背景)**
> It took place in June 2022 at the university's main auditorium. The ceremony was attended by all graduates from my department, their families, and several professors.

**Details (细节)**
> I remember waking up that morning feeling a mix of excitement and nervousness. When I put on my graduation gown and cap, I felt a sense of accomplishment that I had never experienced before. During the ceremony, the dean gave an inspiring speech about embracing challenges and pursuing our dreams. When my name was called and I walked across the stage to receive my diploma, I could hear my parents cheering in the audience. That moment was incredibly emotional for me.

**Feelings (感受)**
> I felt an overwhelming sense of pride and gratitude. Pride because I had worked hard to earn my degree, and gratitude because I knew I couldn't have done it without the support of my family and professors. There was also a bittersweet feeling as I realized I was saying goodbye to my university years.

**Closing (总结)**
> This graduation ceremony remains one of the happiest days of my life. It reminded me that hard work and perseverance always pay off, and it gave me the confidence to face new challenges in the future.

---

### 话题四：Describe a skill you would like to learn

**Opening (引入)**
> A skill I've been wanting to learn for a long time is playing the guitar.

**Background (背景)**
> I've been interested in music since I was a child, but I never had the opportunity to learn a musical instrument properly. A few months ago, I bought an acoustic guitar, and it's been sitting in my room waiting to be played.

**Details (细节)**
> There are several reasons why I want to learn guitar. First, I think it's a versatile instrument that can be used in many genres of music, from pop to classical. Second, I'd love to be able to play my favorite songs and maybe even write my own music someday. Third, I believe learning an instrument is a great way to express creativity and relieve stress.

**Challenges (挑战)**
> I know it won't be easy. I've heard that learning guitar requires a lot of practice, especially in the beginning when your fingers get sore from pressing the strings. I'll need to practice regularly and be patient with my progress. I'm planning to start with online tutorials and maybe take some formal lessons later.

**Closing (总结)**
> I'm really excited to start this musical journey. Even if I don't become a professional guitarist, I think the process of learning will be incredibly rewarding and enjoyable.

## 高级表达技巧 / Advanced Expression Techniques

### 1. 开场多样化 Varied Openings

**避免**：I want to talk about...
**尝试**：
- I'd like to share with you...
- Let me tell you about...
- One thing that comes to mind is...
- I'm going to describe...
- The [person/place/event] I'd like to talk about is...

### 2. 细节丰富化 Adding Details

**感官细节**：
- Visual: It looked like... / The scenery was breathtaking...
- Sound: I could hear... / It sounded like...
- Smell: The air was filled with... / It smelled like...
- Touch: It felt like... / The texture was...
- Taste: It tasted like... / The flavor was...

**情感细节**：
- I felt overwhelmed by...
- I was moved to tears when...
- It was a heartwarming moment when...
- I couldn't help but feel...

### 3. 时间表达 Time Expressions

**过去**：
- Back in... / A few years ago... / When I was younger...
- I remember when... / At that time... / In those days...

**现在**：
- Nowadays... / Currently... / These days...
- At the moment... / Presently...

**未来**：
- I'm hoping to... / I plan to... / In the future, I'd like to...
- One day, I hope to... / Someday...

### 4. 连接词过渡 Transitions

**顺序**：
- First of all... / To begin with... / Firstly...
- Secondly... / In addition... / Moreover...
- Finally... / Last but not least...

**对比**：
- However... / On the other hand... / In contrast...
- Despite this... / Nevertheless... / Although...

**因果**：
- Because of this... / As a result... / Therefore...
- That's why... / Due to... / Consequently...

## 时间控制 / Time Control

### 如何达到 1.5-2 分钟

**开头 (15-20 秒)**
- 简要引入话题
- 提供基本背景信息

**主体 (60-80 秒)**
- 详细描述，覆盖所有要点
- 加入具体例子和细节
- 使用感官描述和情感表达

**结尾 (15-20 秒)**
- 总结感受或观点
- 可以展望未来

### 时间分配示例

**话题**：Describe a place you visited recently

```
Opening (15秒): 引入地点，说明何时去的
Point 1 (20秒): 描述地点的外观和环境
Point 2 (25秒): 讲述在那里做了什么
Point 3 (20秒): 分享感受和收获
Closing (15秒): 总结为什么这次旅行特别
```

## 常见错误 / Common Mistakes

### 错误一：时间过短
**问题**：不到 1 分钟就结束了
**解决**：准备更多细节和例子，使用 AREL 框架

### 错误二：偏离话题
**问题**：说到其他不相关的内容
**解决**：严格遵循话题卡要求，做笔记时列出要点

### 错误三：缺乏细节
**问题**：只说 "It was great" 没有具体说明
**解决**：使用感官描述，加入具体事例

### 错误四：照读笔记
**问题**：一直低头看笔记
**解决**：笔记只写关键词，保持眼神交流

### 错误五：语速过快
**问题**：紧张时语速加快，导致发音不清
**解决**：有意识地放慢语速，适当停顿

## 实用句型 / Useful Sentence Patterns

### 描述人物 Describing People
- He/She is the kind of person who...
- What I admire most about him/her is...
- He/She has a great influence on...
- One thing that stands out about him/her is...

### 描述地点 Describing Places
- The place I'd like to talk about is...
- It's located in/on/at...
- What makes this place special is...
- The atmosphere there is...

### 描述物品 Describing Objects
- It's a [type of object] that...
- I got it from/when...
- What I like most about it is...
- It's important to me because...

### 描述经历 Describing Experiences
- It happened when/where...
- I remember feeling...
- The most memorable part was...
- Looking back, I think...

### 描述感受 Describing Feelings
- I felt overwhelmed by...
- It was a moving experience because...
- I was touched by...
- It left a deep impression on me because...

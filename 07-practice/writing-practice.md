# IELTS Writing Practice / 雅思写作练习

> ✍️ **Instructions / 说明**: This section contains 10 Task 1 prompts and 10 Task 2 prompts. Practice writing under timed conditions: 20 minutes for Task 1 (minimum 150 words) and 40 minutes for Task 2 (minimum 250 words).
> 本节包含 10 道任务一和 10 道任务二题目。在限时条件下练习：任务一 20 分钟（最少 150 词），任务二 40 分钟（最少 250 词）。

---

## Part A: Task 1 Prompts / 任务一题目

### Academic Module / 学术类

---

#### Task 1 #1: Bar Chart — University Enrollment / 柱状图——大学入学人数

> The bar chart below shows the number of students enrolled in three different courses at a university from 2018 to 2022.
> 下面的柱状图显示了2018年至2022年某大学三个不同课程的入学学生人数。

| Course / 课程 | 2018 | 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Computer Science / 计算机科学 | 320 | 380 | 450 | 520 | 610 |
| Business / 商学 | 480 | 460 | 440 | 430 | 420 |
| Arts / 艺术 | 250 | 240 | 220 | 200 | 190 |

**Task / 任务**: Summarize the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
通过选择和报告主要特征来总结信息，并进行相关比较。

**Writing Tips / 写作提示**:
- 开头段改写题目（Paraphrase the question）
- 概述段指出最显著的趋势（Overview: most notable trends）
- 用具体数据支持描述（Support with specific data）
- 比较不同课程之间的差异（Compare differences between courses）

---

#### Task 1 #2: Line Graph — Energy Consumption / 折线图——能源消耗

> The line graph below shows energy consumption in a country from 2000 to 2020, by source.
> 下面的折线图显示了某国2000年至2020年按来源分类的能源消耗。

| Year / 年份 | Oil / 石油 | Natural Gas / 天然气 | Renewable / 可再生能源 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2000 | 40% | 25% | 5% |
| 2005 | 38% | 28% | 8% |
| 2010 | 35% | 30% | 14% |
| 2015 | 30% | 28% | 22% |
| 2020 | 25% | 25% | 32% |

**Task / 任务**: Write a report describing the trends shown in the graph.
撰写报告描述图表中显示的趋势。

**Writing Tips / 写作提示**:
- 注意总体趋势：石油下降，可再生能源上升
- 标注交叉点（如2020年石油和天然气持平）
- 使用趋势词汇：increase, decrease, plateau, fluctuate

---

#### Task 1 #3: Pie Charts — Household Expenditure / 饼图——家庭支出

> The pie charts compare household expenditure in two countries in 2020.
> 饼图比较了2020年两个国家的家庭支出。

**Country A / A国**: Food 35%, Housing 25%, Transport 15%, Education 15%, Other 10%
**Country B / B国**: Housing 30%, Food 20%, Education 20%, Transport 10%, Other 20%

**Task / 任务**: Compare the two pie charts and summarize the key differences.
比较两个饼图并总结主要差异。

**Writing Tips / 写作提示**:
- 用比较语言：while, whereas, in contrast, compared to
- 注意最大和最小类别的对比
- 不要逐一列出所有数据，突出关键差异

---

#### Task 1 #4: Table — Tourist Arrivals / 表格——游客到达人数

> The table below shows the number of tourists (in thousands) visiting five countries in 2019 and 2023.
> 下面的表格显示了2019年和2023年五个国家的游客人数（千人）。

| Country / 国家 | 2019 | 2023 | Change / 变化 |
|---|---|---|---|
| France / 法国 | 89,400 | 72,200 | -19.2% |
| Spain / 西班牙 | 83,700 | 85,100 | +1.7% |
| Thailand / 泰国 | 39,800 | 28,200 | -29.1% |
| Japan / 日本 | 31,900 | 25,100 | -21.3% |
| Mexico / 墨西哥 | 45,000 | 38,300 | -14.9% |

**Task / 任务**: Summarize the data, highlighting key changes.
总结数据，突出关键变化。

---

#### Task 1 #5: Process Diagram — Recycling / 流程图——回收过程

> The diagram below shows the process of recycling plastic bottles.
> 下面的流程图展示了塑料瓶回收的过程。

**Stages / 阶段**:
1. Collection from households / 从家庭收集
2. Sorting at recycling center / 在回收中心分类
3. Cleaning and shredding / 清洗和粉碎
4. Melting into pellets / 熔化成颗粒
5. Manufacturing new products / 制造新产品
6. Distribution to stores / 分发到商店

**Task / 任务**: Describe the process shown in the diagram.
描述流程图中展示的过程。

**Writing Tips / 写作提示**:
- 使用被动语态：is collected, is sorted, is melted
- 使用顺序词：firstly, then, subsequently, finally
- 注意流程的完整性，不要遗漏步骤

### General Training Module / 培训类

---

#### Task 1 #6: Formal Letter — Complaint / 正式信函——投诉

> You recently purchased a laptop from an online store, but it arrived with a damaged screen. Write a letter to the customer service manager.
> 你最近从一家在线商店购买了一台笔记本电脑，但到达时屏幕损坏。给客服经理写一封信。

**In your letter / 在信中**:
- Describe the problem / 描述问题
- Explain how it has affected you / 解释对你的影响
- State what action you would like / 说明你希望采取的行动

---

#### Task 1 #7: Semi-Formal Letter — Request / 半正式信函——请求

> You are planning to attend a language course abroad and need to request information from the school.
> 你计划参加国外的语言课程，需要向学校索取信息。

**In your letter / 在信中**:
- Explain which course you are interested in / 说明你感兴趣的课程
- Ask about accommodation options / 询问住宿选择
- Request details about fees and schedule / 请求费用和时间安排详情

---

#### Task 1 #8: Informal Letter — Advice / 非正式信函——建议

> A friend is planning to visit your city for the first time. Write a letter giving suggestions.
> 一个朋友第一次计划来你的城市旅游。写一封信给出建议。

**In your letter / 在信中**:
- Recommend places to visit / 推荐参观的地方
- Suggest the best time to visit / 建议最佳旅游时间
- Offer practical tips / 提供实用建议

## Part B: Task 2 Prompts / 任务二题目

---

#### Task 2 #1: Agree or Disagree / 同意或不同意

> **Some people believe that technology has made our lives more complicated rather than simpler. To what extent do you agree or disagree?**
> **一些人认为科技让我们的生活变得更复杂而非更简单。你在多大程度上同意或不同意？**

**Essay Structure Guide / 文章结构指南**:
1. **Introduction**: Paraphrase + your position / 改写题目 + 你的立场
2. **Body 1**: First main argument + examples / 第一个主要论点 + 例子
3. **Body 2**: Second main argument + examples / 第二个主要论点 + 例子
4. **Body 3**: Counter-argument (if partially agree) / 反论点（如果部分同意）
5. **Conclusion**: Restate position + final thought / 重申立场 + 最终想法

---

#### Task 2 #2: Discussion / 讨论类

> **Some people think that the best way to reduce crime is to give longer prison sentences. Others believe there are better alternative ways to reduce crime. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.**
> **一些人认为减少犯罪的最好方法是延长刑期。其他人认为有更好的替代方法来减少犯罪。讨论两种观点并给出你的看法。**

**Key Vocabulary / 关键词汇**:
- deterrent / 威慑
- rehabilitation / 改造
- recidivism / 累犯
- community service / 社区服务
- incarceration / 监禁

---

#### Task 2 #3: Problem & Solution / 问题与解决

> **In many cities, the quality of air pollution has become a serious problem. What are the causes of this, and what solutions can you suggest?**
> **在许多城市，空气污染已成为严重问题。其原因是什么？你能提出什么解决方案？**

**Causes to consider / 考虑的原因**:
- Vehicle emissions / 车辆排放
- Industrial pollution / 工业污染
- Deforestation / 森林砍伐
- Lack of regulation / 缺乏监管

**Solutions to consider / 考虑的解决方案**:
- Stricter emission standards / 更严格的排放标准
- Investment in public transport / 投资公共交通
- Green energy transition / 绿色能源转型

## ✍️ Common Mistakes to Avoid / 常见错误需避免

### Task 1 / 任务一
1. ❌ **Don't** give opinions — only report data
   **不要**给意见——只报告数据
2. ❌ **Don't** describe every data point — select key features
   **不要**描述每个数据点——选择主要特征
3. ❌ **Don't** use informal language
   **不要**使用非正式语言
4. ✅ **Do** include an overview paragraph
   **一定要**包含概述段
5. ✅ **Do** use appropriate data language (approximately, roughly, just over)
   **一定要**使用恰当的数据语言

### Task 2 / 任务二
1. ❌ **Don't** go off-topic
   **不要**跑题
2. ❌ **Don't** use memorized phrases without context
   **不要**无语境地使用背诵的短语
3. ❌ **Don't** write less than 250 words
   **不要**写少于 250 词
4. ✅ **Do** address all parts of the question
   **一定要**回答问题的所有部分
5. ✅ **Do** provide specific examples
   **一定要**提供具体例子

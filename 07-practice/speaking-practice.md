# IELTS Speaking Practice / 雅思口语练习

> 🎤 **Instructions / 说明**: This section contains 20 speaking topics covering Parts 1, 2, and 3 of the IELTS Speaking test. Each topic includes a sample answer and key vocabulary. Practice speaking aloud and time yourself.
> 本节包含 20 个口语话题，涵盖雅思口语考试的第一、二、三部分。每个话题包含范例回答和关键词汇。大声练习并计时。

---

## Speaking Test Format / 口语考试形式

| Part / 部分 | Duration / 时长 | Format / 形式 |
|---|---|---|
| Part 1 | 4–5 minutes | General questions about familiar topics / 关于熟悉话题的一般问题 |
| Part 2 | 3–4 minutes | Individual long turn (cue card) / 个人长时间发言（话题卡） |
| Part 3 | 4–5 minutes | Abstract discussion related to Part 2 / 与第二部分相关的抽象讨论 |

---

## Part 1 Topics / 第一部分话题

### Topic 1: Your Home / 你的家

**Q: Can you describe where you live?**
你能描述你住在哪里吗？

**Sample Answer / 范例回答**:
"I live in an apartment in a residential area on the outskirts of the city. It's a two-bedroom flat on the eighth floor of a modern building. The neighborhood is quite peaceful, with several parks nearby. What I particularly like about my home is that it has a balcony with a nice view of the city skyline."

"我住在城市郊区一个居民区的公寓里。那是一栋现代建筑八楼的两居室公寓。社区相当安静，附近有几个公园。我特别喜欢我家的地方是它有一个阳台，可以看到城市天际线的美景。"

**Key Vocabulary / 关键词汇**:
- residential area / 居民区
- outskirts / 郊区
- two-bedroom flat / 两居室公寓
- balcony / 阳台
- city skyline / 城市天际线

**Q: What do you do for a living? / What are you studying?**
你做什么工作？/ 你学什么专业？

The coursework is quite challenging, especially the algorithms and data structures modules, but I find it incredibly rewarding when I can build a working application from scratch."

"我目前是一名计算机科学专业的大学生。我选择这个领域是因为我一直对技术和问题解决着迷。课程相当有挑战性，特别是算法和数据结构模块，但当我能从零开始构建一个可运行的应用程序时，我觉得非常有成就感。"

- rewarding / 有回报的

## Part 2 Cue Cards / 第二部分话题卡

### Topic 8: Describe a Memorable Trip / 描述一次难忘的旅行

**Cue Card / 话题卡**:

Describe a trip you took that you remember well.
描述一次你记忆犹新的旅行。

You should say:
- Where you went / 你去了哪里
- When you went / 你什么时候去的
- Who you went with / 你和谁一起去的
- Why it was memorable / 为什么这次旅行令人难忘

**Sample Answer / 范例回答**:
"I'd like to talk about a trip I took to Yunnan province about two years ago. I went with three of my closest friends during the National Day holiday. We spent a week exploring the region, starting from the ancient town of Lijiang and then heading to Shangri-La.

What made this trip truly unforgettable was the breathtaking scenery. The Jade Dragon Snow Mountain was absolutely magnificent — standing at 5,596 meters, it was covered in snow and surrounded by lush green forests. We also visited Erhai Lake in Dali, where the water was so clear that you could see the reflections of the mountains perfectly.

Another highlight was experiencing the local culture. We tried traditional Naxi cuisine, watched a folk music performance, and even participated in a local festival. The people were incredibly warm and welcoming.

The trip was memorable not just because of the beautiful scenery and culture, but also because it strengthened our friendship. We created so many wonderful memories together, and I still look back at the photos with great fondness."

"我想谈谈大约两年前我去云南省的一次旅行。国庆假期我和三个最亲密的朋友一起去的。我们花了一周时间探索这个地区，从丽江古城开始，然后前往香格里拉。

让这次旅行真正难忘的是令人叹为观止的景色。玉龙雪山绝对壮丽——海拔5596米，白雪皑皑，四周环绕着郁郁葱葱的森林。我们还参观了大理的洱海，湖水清澈到可以完美地看到山的倒影。

另一个亮点是体验当地文化。我们尝试了纳西族传统美食，观看了民间音乐表演，甚至参加了当地的节日。那里的人们非常热情好客。

这次旅行令人难忘，不仅因为美丽的风景和文化，还因为它加深了我们的友谊。我们一起创造了那么多美好的回忆，我至今还满怀深情地翻看那些照片。"

**Key Vocabulary / 关键词汇**:
- breathtaking / 令人叹为观止的
- magnificent / 壮丽的
- lush green forests / 郁郁葱葱的森林
- reflections / 倒影
- strengthened our friendship / 加深了我们的友谊
- with great fondness / 满怀深情地

### Topic 9: Describe a Book That Influenced You / 描述一本影响了你的书

**Cue Card / 话题卡**:

Describe a book that had a significant influence on you.
描述一本对你有重大影响的书。

You should say:
- What the book is / 是什么书
- When you read it / 你什么时候读的
- What it is about / 它讲了什么
- How it influenced you / 它如何影响了你

**Sample Answer / 范例回答**:
"The book I'd like to talk about is 'Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind' by Yuval Noah Harari. I first read it during my second year of university, and it completely changed my perspective on human civilization.

The book covers the entire history of our species, from the emergence of Homo sapiens in Africa to the present day. It explores how cognitive, agricultural, and scientific revolutions have shaped the way we live. What struck me most was Harari's argument that many things we consider natural — like money, nations, and human rights — are actually 'imagined realities' that exist because we collectively believe in them.

This book influenced me profoundly. It made me question things I had always taken for granted and encouraged me to think more critically about the world. It also sparked my interest in history and anthropology, leading me to read many more books on these subjects. I've recommended it to almost everyone I know."

"我想谈的书是尤瓦尔·赫拉利的《人类简史》。我第一次读它是在大学二年级，它完全改变了我对人类文明的看法。

这本书涵盖了我们物种的全部历史，从智人在非洲的出现到现在。它探讨了认知革命、农业革命和科学革命如何塑造了我们的生活方式。最让我震撼的是赫拉利的论点：许多我们认为自然的东西——如金钱、国家和人权——实际上是'想象的现实'，因为我们的集体信仰而存在。

这本书深刻地影响了我。它让我质疑了一直视为理所当然的事物，并鼓励我更批判性地思考世界。它还激发了我对历史和人类学的兴趣，引导我阅读了更多这些领域的书。我几乎向认识的每个人都推荐了这本书。"

**Key Vocabulary / 关键词汇**:
- perspective / 视角
- cognitive revolution / 认知革命
- imagined realities / 想象的现实
- profoundly / 深刻地
- take for granted / 视为理所当然
- critically / 批判性地

### Topic 10: Describe a Skill You Learned / 描述你学会的一项技能

**Cue Card / 话题卡**:

Describe a new skill you learned recently.
描述你最近学会的一项新技能。

You should say:
- What the skill is / 是什么技能
- How you learned it / 你怎么学的
- How long it took / 花了多长时间
- How you feel about it / 你对此有什么感受

**Sample Answer / 范例回答**:
"Recently, I learned how to cook Chinese dumplings from scratch. I'd always wanted to learn but never had the opportunity until last Spring Festival when my grandmother offered to teach me.

The process was more complex than I expected. First, I learned to make the dough by mixing flour and water in the right proportions. Then came the filling — a mixture of minced pork, Chinese cabbage, ginger, and various seasonings. The most challenging part was the folding technique. My grandmother made it look effortless, but it took me several attempts to get the pleats right.

It probably took me about three afternoons of practice before I could make dumplings that looked reasonably presentable. The first batch was quite misshapen, but by the third session, I was quite proud of the result.

I feel a real sense of accomplishment now. Not only can I make delicious dumplings, but I also feel connected to my family's culinary tradition. It's a skill I plan to pass on to my own children someday."

"最近，我学会了从头开始包饺子。我一直想学但一直没有机会，直到去年春节我奶奶主动教我。

这个过程比我预期的更复杂。首先，我学会了用面粉和水按正确比例和面。然后是馅料——猪肉末、白菜、姜和各种调料的混合物。最具挑战性的部分是包饺子的手法。我奶奶做起来毫不费力，但我花了很多次尝试才把褶子包好。

我大概花了三个下午的练习才能包出看起来还算像样的饺子。第一批饺子形状相当奇怪，但到第三次练习时，我对结果相当自豪。

我现在有一种真正的成就感。我不仅能做出美味的饺子，还感觉自己与家族的烹饪传统有了联系。这是一个我计划将来传给自己孩子的技能。"

**Key Vocabulary / 关键词汇**:
- from scratch / 从头开始
- proportions / 比例
- filling / 馅料
- folding technique / 折叠手法
- effortless / 毫不费力的
- misshapen / 变形的
- sense of accomplishment / 成就感
- culinary tradition / 烹饪传统

## Part 3 Discussion Topics / 第三部分讨论话题

### Topic 11: Education / 教育

**Q: Do you think the education system in your country needs to change?**
你认为你所在国家的教育体系需要改变吗？

**Sample Answer / 范例回答**:
"Yes, I believe there are several aspects that could be improved. The current system places too much emphasis on rote memorization and standardized testing, which doesn't necessarily develop critical thinking or creativity. I think schools should incorporate more project-based learning and encourage students to ask questions rather than simply absorb information. Additionally, vocational education deserves more attention and respect — not every student needs to pursue an academic path."

"是的，我认为有几个方面可以改进。目前的体系过于强调死记硬背和标准化考试，这不一定能培养批判性思维或创造力。我认为学校应该更多地融入项目式学习，鼓励学生提问而不是简单地吸收信息。此外，职业教育应该得到更多关注和尊重——不是每个学生都需要走学术路线。"

**Key Vocabulary / 关键词汇**:
- rote memorization / 死记硬背
- standardized testing / 标准化考试
- critical thinking / 批判性思维
- project-based learning / 项目式学习
- vocational education / 职业教育

### Topic 12: Technology / 科技

**Q: How has technology changed the way people communicate?**
科技如何改变了人们的交流方式？

**Sample Answer / 范例回答**:
"Technology has fundamentally transformed communication. Social media platforms and instant messaging apps allow us to stay connected with people across the globe in real time. Video calls have made face-to-face conversations possible regardless of distance. However, there's a downside — many people now prefer texting over talking, which can lead to misunderstandings and weaker emotional connections. I think the key is to use technology as a supplement to, rather than a replacement for, genuine human interaction."

"科技从根本上改变了沟通方式。社交媒体平台和即时通讯应用让我们能够与全球的人实时保持联系。视频通话使得无论距离多远都能进行面对面交谈。然而，也有负面影响——现在很多人更喜欢发信息而不是说话，这可能导致误解和更弱的情感联系。我认为关键是把科技作为真正人际互动的补充，而非替代品。"

**Key Vocabulary / 关键词汇**:
- fundamentally / 根本上
- instant messaging / 即时通讯
- in real time / 实时
- supplement / 补充
- replacement / 替代品

### Topic 13: Environment / 环境

**Q: What can individuals do to protect the environment?**
个人可以做什么来保护环境？

**Sample Answer / 范例回答**:
"There are numerous actions individuals can take. On a daily basis, people can reduce their carbon footprint by using public transport, cycling, or walking instead of driving. Reducing consumption of single-use plastics is another effective measure — carrying reusable bags and water bottles makes a significant difference. At home, conserving energy by using LED bulbs and being mindful of water usage helps. Beyond personal habits, individuals can also make an impact through their consumer choices — supporting environmentally responsible companies and buying locally-produced goods."

"个人可以采取许多行动。在日常生活中，人们可以通过乘坐公共交通、骑自行车或步行代替开车来减少碳足迹。减少一次性塑料的使用是另一项有效措施——携带可重复使用的购物袋和水瓶会产生显著影响。在家使用 LED 灯泡节约能源、注意用水也有帮助。除了个人习惯外，个人还可以通过消费选择产生影响——支持对环境负责的公司和购买本地生产的商品。"

**Key Vocabulary / 关键词汇**:
- carbon footprint / 碳足迹
- single-use plastics / 一次性塑料
- reusable / 可重复使用的
- conserving energy / 节约能源
- environmentally responsible / 对环境负责的

### Topic 14: Culture / 文化

**Q: Do you think globalization is destroying local cultures?**
你认为全球化正在摧毁本土文化吗？

**Sample Answer / 范例回答**:
"I think it's a nuanced issue. Globalization certainly poses risks to local cultures — the spread of Western media, fast food chains, and English as a dominant language can erode traditional practices and languages. However, it can also promote cultural exchange and awareness. Many people now have greater appreciation for diverse cultures thanks to global connectivity. The challenge is finding a balance — embracing the benefits of a connected world while actively preserving and celebrating local traditions, languages, and customs."

"我认为这是一个复杂的问题。全球化确实给本土文化带来风险——西方媒体的传播、快餐连锁店和英语作为主导语言可能会侵蚀传统习俗和语言。然而，它也可以促进文化交流和意识。由于全球互联互通，许多人现在对多元文化有了更大的欣赏。挑战在于找到平衡——拥抱互联世界的好处，同时积极保护和弘扬本地传统、语言和习俗。"

**Key Vocabulary / 关键词汇**:
- nuanced / 细致入微的
- erode / 侵蚀
- cultural exchange / 文化交流
- global connectivity / 全球互联互通
- preserving / 保护

### Topic 15: Health / 健康

**Q: Why do you think some people struggle to maintain a healthy lifestyle?**
你认为为什么有些人难以保持健康的生活方式？

**Sample Answer / 范例回答**:
"There are several factors at play. Firstly, modern lifestyles are incredibly busy — long working hours and commuting leave little time for exercise or cooking healthy meals. Secondly, unhealthy options are often more convenient and affordable than healthy alternatives. A fast food meal is cheaper and quicker than preparing a nutritious dinner from fresh ingredients. Thirdly, there's a psychological aspect — habits are hard to break, and the immediate gratification of unhealthy choices often outweighs the long-term benefits of a healthy lifestyle. Finally, lack of health education means some people simply don't know how to make better choices."

"有几个因素在起作用。首先，现代生活方式非常忙碌——长时间工作和通勤几乎没有时间锻炼或烹饪健康餐食。其次，不健康的选择通常比健康替代品更方便、更实惠。快餐比用新鲜食材准备营养晚餐更便宜、更快。第三，有一个心理层面——习惯很难打破，不健康选择带来的即时满足感往往超过健康生活方式的长期益处。最后，缺乏健康教育意味着有些人根本不知道如何做出更好的选择。"

**Key Vocabulary / 关键词汇**:
- at play / 在起作用
- nutritious / 有营养的
- immediate gratification / 即时满足
- outweighs / 超过

## Part 2 Additional Cue Cards / 第二部分更多话题卡

### Topic 16: Describe a Person Who Inspires You / 描述一个激励你的人

**Cue Card / 话题卡**:
Describe a person who has inspired you in your life.
描述一个在你生活中激励了你的人。

You should say:
- Who this person is / 这个人是谁
- How you know them / 你怎么认识他们的
- What they have achieved / 他们取得了什么成就
- Why they inspire you / 他们为什么激励了你

**Sample Answer / 范例回答**:
"The person who inspires me most is my high school English teacher, Ms. Chen. She taught me for three years and had an enormous impact on my academic and personal development. She had a unique ability to make even the most complex grammar rules seem simple and intuitive.

What I admire most about her is her dedication. She would stay after school to help struggling students, created engaging lesson plans, and constantly updated her teaching methods. She also encouraged me to participate in English speech competitions, which built my confidence significantly.

She inspires me because she showed me that with patience, hard work, and genuine passion, you can make a real difference in people's lives. Her influence is one of the main reasons I'm studying English so seriously today."

"最激励我的人是我的高中英语老师陈老师。她教了我三年，对我的学业和个人发展产生了巨大影响。她有一种独特的能力，能让最复杂的语法规则看起来简单直观。

我想去冰岛是因为它提供了世界上其他地方找不到的独特自然体验。冰与火的结合、原始的风景和稀少的人口，让它感觉像是在访问另一个星球。"

## 📊 Speaking Score Tips / 口语提分技巧

### Band 7+ Criteria / 7分以上标准

1. **Fluency / 流利度**: Speak at length without noticeable effort
   轻松自如地进行长篇发言

2. **Vocabulary / 词汇**: Use less common vocabulary and idiomatic expressions
   使用不常见的词汇和惯用表达

3. **Grammar / 语法**: Use a range of complex structures with flexibility
   灵活使用各种复杂结构

4. **Pronunciation / 发音**: Be clear and natural with good intonation
   发音清晰自然，语调良好

### Useful Phrases for Speaking / 口语实用短语

| Function / 功能 | Phrases / 短语 |
|---|---|
| Giving opinions / 表达观点 | In my view / From my perspective / I'd say that |
| Adding details / 添加细节 | What I mean is / To be more specific / In other words |
| Giving examples / 举例 | A case in point is / Take... for example / Such as |
| Expressing uncertainty / 表达不确定 | I'm not entirely sure, but / It's hard to say / I suppose |
| Contrasting / 对比 | On the flip side / Having said that / That said |
